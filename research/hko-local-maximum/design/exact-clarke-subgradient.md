# Exact Clarke-Subgradient Plan For HKO2024

## Purpose

Record the proof-oriented route for the `M_10` local-maximality claim with the
current execution default: prefer the largest exact-computation route that still
has a simple, trustworthy setup and a backend-neutral witness artifact. Use the
paper and symmetry reductions first as validation, bookkeeping, and geometric
explanation; make them load-bearing only if the larger exact route fails to
become thesis-ready.

This note is a research/design surface, not an implementation logbook.

## Research Question

Can we prove the intended `M_10` subresult from `RESULTS.md` by exact
computation over a number field with a simple, trusted witness artifact, while
keeping paper-derived orbit geometry and symmetry reductions as supporting
validation and explanation surfaces rather than making them computationally
load-bearing too early?

Target statement shape:

- For HKO2024 in dual-vertex coordinates `a_i`, the Clarke-flat directions at
  the base point are exactly the infinitesimal symmetry directions generated by
  translation, scaling, and `Sp(4)`.
- Equivalently, the active-gradient matrix has kernel equal to the symmetry
  tangent space.

This would replace the current "strong numerical evidence" route with an exact
first-order quotient-space statement.

## Decision

Use SageMath as the current primary exact algebra system, but keep the witness
contract backend-agnostic.

Reason:

- Exact number fields and exact linear algebra are the core workflow, not a
  secondary layer.
- The API matches the mathematical task directly: define a field, build
  matrices over it, compute rank/kernel/subspace relations.
- This is preferable to implementing a custom algebra package inside the repo.

Secondary confidence plan:

- If the final orbit-gradient formulas collapse to a quadratic subfield, add an
  independent exact block-embedding cross-check there.
- Otherwise, treat the actual simple number field as the primary exact surface
  and do not force the route back into a quadratic formulation.
- If the Rust number-field backend becomes ready and can emit the same witness
  contract faster, allow it to replace Sage as the computational workhorse for
  the larger exact route.
- The current first concrete Sage-facing surface is the widened-seed witness
  bundle at
  `experiments/hko-local-maximum/exact-clarke/widened-seed-witness.json`,
  with Sage verification script
  `experiments/hko-local-maximum/exact-clarke/verify_widened_seed_witness.sage`.
  That bundle is not the final theorem witness, but it fixes the producer /
  verifier contract for Packet 3.

## Intended Input Surface

Default theorem-input preference:

- a larger exact finite candidate or active surface is acceptable if the
  backend can certify action minima/gaps exactly and emit exact row/rank/kernel
  witnesses;
- paper-derived orbit families and symmetry reductions remain important as
  validation and explanation surfaces, and may become load-bearing only if the
  larger exact route proves too costly or too opaque.

Take from HKO2024 and our exactified repo scaffolding:

- the HKO2024 polytope itself,
- whichever finite orbit-family or representative-family surface we can certify exactly
  with the current trusted backend/witness setup,
- the closed-form coefficients needed to write their gradients.

Take from our current repo only when it is purely definitional and already
understood:

- the dual-vertex parameterization `K = {x : a_i . x <= 1}`,
- the formula for `sys = c_EHZ^2 / (2 vol)`,
- the definition of the symmetry tangent space in `R^40`.

Do not make the final theorem artifact depend on:

- floating-point KKT solves,
- numerical tie detection,
- empirical active-set discovery.

Numerical planning surfaces are acceptable for deciding what to exactify next,
but they are not themselves theorem evidence.

## Expected Coefficient Field

The HKO2024 dual geometry is now confirmed to live in a small simple extension
larger than `Q(sqrt(5))`: the committed dual coordinates are naturally
expressed using `t = tan(pi/5)`, which satisfies `t^4 - 10 t^2 + 5 = 0`.

Clarification:

- the quartic field is not an artifact of dividing quadratic normals by a
  quadratic support height;
- the common support height `h = cos(pi/5)` is quadratic, but the standard
  facet normals already involve `sin(pi/5)` terms outside `Q(sqrt(5))`;
- therefore the dual vertices `a_i = n_i / h_i` remain naturally quartic, and
  a global rescaling of the primal polytope cannot collapse the dual geometry
  into `Q(sqrt(5))`.

Open check:

- verify whether the orbit-gradient formulas stay in that quartic field or
  collapse to a smaller subfield;
- only widen the CAS plan further if the paper-derived gradient formulas force
  a genuinely larger field.

## Output Contract

The checker should produce exact, reviewable artifacts for these claims:

1. the active-gradient matrix `G` over the exact coefficient field,
2. `rank(G)`,
3. a basis of `ker(G)`,
4. a basis of the infinitesimal symmetry space,
5. exact certificates that each basis lies in the span of the other.

Preferred artifact shapes:

- a machine-readable dump of the exact matrix and exact basis vectors;
- a short generated theorem-facing summary for `formal/` or `thesis/`;
- optional rational-block-embedding cross-check output when the field is
  quadratic.
- The same artifact shape should be emitted independent of whether the backend
  is Sage, SymPy scaffolding, or the future Rust exact backend.

Current concrete Packet 3 artifact shape:

- one backend-neutral JSON witness bundle carrying the field, dual geometry,
  symmetry basis, exact representative-row families, expected row counts/ranks, and
  exact common scalar checks;
- one Sage verifier that reads only that witness and writes a machine-readable
  verification summary.

## Minimal Mathematical Plan

1. Fix an ordering of the 10 facets and the `R^40` coordinates.
2. Encode the HKO2024 dual vertices exactly.
3. Encode the orbit-family list from the paper.
4. For each active orbit family, write its exact gradient contribution in
   dual-vertex coordinates.
5. Form the matrix of active gradients.
6. Compute its kernel exactly in SageMath.
7. Independently construct the symmetry tangent space.
8. Prove equality of the two subspaces by exact linear algebra.

If this closes cleanly, the current second-order note becomes supporting
evidence rather than the main route to the `M_10` statement.

## Failure Modes

- The paper may not give enough closed-form orbit data to reconstruct the exact
  gradients without additional derivation.
- The orbit-gradient formulas may still force a field larger than the current
  quartic geometry field, requiring a more general number-field setup.
- The exact first-order statement may still leave a quotient-space theorem gap
  if the symmetry tangent space is not encoded correctly.
- The active set in the paper may need interpretation before it yields a finite
  exact matrix. In particular, the current exact Packet 2 evidence suggests
  that some paper families are best treated as exact KKT segments whose
  interior points do not contribute new extreme first-order rows.

If any of these occur, stop and record the specific obstruction before opening
another implementation line.
