# Bounded prior-art check: unrestricted instability of the HKO pentagon product

Checked 29 October 2026. Target is the project result in `/tmp/msc-math/restart/multicut/complete-simple/RESULT.md`: an explicit inward twenty-row family `K_t` through the Haim-Kislev–Ostrover (HKO) product `H`, with positive right derivative of `log(c_EHZ(K_t)^2/(2 Vol_4(K_t)))`. On the project proof and its independent audit, this implies that `H` is **not a Hausdorff local maximizer among all convex four-bodies**, and that some sufficiently small positive `t` has ratio strictly greater than `(3+sqrt(5))/5`. The proof/certificate audit is owned elsewhere. There is not yet an exhibited certified nonzero `t` or numerical improvement size.

## Calibrated conclusion and limits

**No direct anticipation or mathematical contradiction was found in the inspected primary sources.** Conditional on the project's proof/certificate review, the specific contribution is an **unrestricted Hausdorff ascent at HKO**, yielding a strict improvement of the known four-dimensional EHZ systolic counterexample value. The project's existing local theorem for at most eleven facets is consistent with a twenty-inequality ascent.
