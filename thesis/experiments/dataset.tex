\subsection{The Dataset}
\label{sec:dataset}

We compute the EHZ capacity (the minimum symplectic action over all generalized
Reeb orbits, see Definition~\ref{def:ehz-capacity}) for 3,238 convex 4-polytopes
organized across four primary data files plus one test fixture:

\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{polytopes.jsonl}: 338 polytopes. These are computed with HK2017
		algorithm only. The dataset includes both literature-known reference polytopes
		(simplex, hypercube, crosspolytope, and Haim-Kislev-Ostrover counterexample)
		and random generic convex 4-polytopes.

	\item \textbf{pentagon\_sweep.jsonl}: 361 polytopes. All are Lagrangian products
		of the form $P_5 \times_L R(\theta) P_5$ where $P_5$ is a regular pentagon
		and $\theta$ varies. Both HK2017 and billiard algorithms are applied.

	\item \textbf{polygon\_grid.jsonl}: 2,272 polytopes. All are Lagrangian products
		$P_n \times_L R(\theta) P_m$ for pairs of regular polygons. Both HK2017 and
		billiard algorithms are applied.

	\item \textbf{random\_products.jsonl}: 234 polytopes. All are random Lagrangian
		products $K_q \times_L K_p$ where $K_q$ and $K_p$ are random convex polygons.
		Both HK2017 and billiard algorithms are applied.

	\item \textbf{capacity\_dataset.json} (test fixture): 33 polytopes used for
		correctness tests in the HK2017 crate. These are regenerated by \texttt{cargo test}.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Algorithms}

\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{HK2017}: Exhaustive search over cyclic orderings of facets.
		Works on any convex polytope. Exponential cost in facet count.
		Applied to all polytopes in polytopes.jsonl, and to the Lagrangian products
		in pentagon\_sweep.jsonl, polygon\_grid.jsonl, and random\_products.jsonl
		(for algorithm agreement validation). Details: Section~\ref{sec:main-result}.

	\item \textbf{Billiard}: Exploits the product structure $K_q \times_L K_p$
		(Cartesian product with Lagrangian embedding). Works only when both $K_q$
		and $K_p$ are convex polygons. Polynomial cost.
		Applied to the Lagrangian products in pentagon\_sweep.jsonl (361), polygon\_grid.jsonl (2,272),
		and random\_products.jsonl (234), totaling 2,867 polytopes. Details: Section~\ref{sec:billiard}.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Algorithm agreement}

For the Lagrangian products in pentagon\_sweep.jsonl, polygon\_grid.jsonl, and
random\_products.jsonl, both HK2017 and billiard are applied. The data files
record whether the two algorithms agree (field: \texttt{algorithms\_agree}), with
tolerance $10^{-8}$ relative error (chosen to exceed double-precision roundoff).
