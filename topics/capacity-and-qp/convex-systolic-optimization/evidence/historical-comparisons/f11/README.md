# Separate eleven-facet HKO local theorem

This folder preserves the source for the scoped historical comparison in Section 4 of the report. The separately accepted computer-assisted theorem states: for the standard Haim--Kislev--Ostrover pentagon product H, some Hausdorff neighborhood has systolic ratio at most rho(H) on every convex four-polytope with at most eleven genuine facets. It does not cover the twenty-facet family in this paper.
