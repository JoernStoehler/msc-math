# Empirical method portfolio: mathematical discovery beyond predicting sys

Prepared 2026-09-18. Design and source audit only; no methods or capacity producers were run. Historical dispositions below describe their recorded scope, not a new judgment that they suffice for the current user request.

## The principal correction

A dataset need not have one privileged response column. We can provisionally treat a polytope as a source of many measurable quantities and look for low-complexity relations, exceptional objects, decompositions, and stable regimes. Mathematical interpretation follows candidate discovery, and can change which objects and quantities we compute next. A high sys predictor is one output of this process, not its definition.

The concrete loop is:

1. Assemble geometrically diverse objects and available measurements, preserving generation and computational provenance.
2. Run several qualitatively different pattern-finding operations, including operations with no designated target.
3. Select patterns by simplicity, surprise, robustness, mathematical interpretability and the ability to obtain a decisive next example; not just predictive score.
4. Inspect the actual geometries and definitions. Translate the pattern into a quantified statement, decomposition, construction, or explicit obstruction.
5. Compute missing measurements or generate objects where proposed explanations diverge.
6. Simplify survivors toward exact mathematics; preserve informative refutations and revise representations or sampling as needed.

A discovery objective and procedure suffice to start step 2. Requiring a precise mathematical hypothesis before discovery would forbid the part we are trying to enable. Conversely, a discovered hypothesis should acquire a clear domain and potential falsifiers before costly follow-up or thesis promotion.

## Portfolio by scientific operation

These are proposals, not assertions of method effectiveness on this problem. They deliberately span questions instead of supplying another estimator leaderboard.

| Operation and question | Tools / concrete deployment | Interpretation and next experiment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Relations without a target.** Which measured quantities constrain one another? | Exact duplicate/identity checks; pairwise rank and transformed scatter screens; sparse linear relations among transformed features; low-rank residuals; restricted symbolic search among small feature subsets. Include capacity and volume separately with scaling degrees recorded. | Reconstruct definitions to remove tautologies. Search products, sums, powers and ratios permitted by scaling. Generate objects where an apparent relation should fail if it is only a family identity. |
| **Inequalities and sharp examples.** What bounds seem universal or family-specific? | Fit low-complexity upper/lower envelopes; enumerate simple normalized ratios and inspect extrema; quantile fits as scouting; constrained sparse expressions. | Turn an envelope into an explicit inequality with a domain. Maximize its violation; simplify near-equality objects. A fitted quantile is not a bound, and no finite point cloud proves universality. |
| **Factorization and separability.** Does a complicated response decompose? | GAMs, low-order interaction models, tensor/rank factorizations on designed product grids, logarithmic transforms where quantities are positive. Compare simple decompositions against richer predictors. | A discovered dependence on one factor or interaction suggests a product formula or reduction. Generate crossed factor pairs omitted from discovery, not merely a random split of the original pairs. |
| **Conditional structure.** Which apparent associations vanish or reverse when other structure is held fixed? | Within-family plots and fits; partial/residual analyses; conditional dependence scouting with explicit adjustment choices; hierarchical models for family-specific responses. | Distinguish mixing from a common geometric relation. Build matched geometries or parameter paths that preserve proposed controls. Conditional independence in observational data does not identify geometry's causal mechanism. |
| **Change of regime.** Where does a response law change? | Piecewise regressions, change-point detection on parameter paths, rule induction, segmentation by known combinatorial/active-orbit signatures. | Inspect a transition and conjecture its mathematical boundary. Densify only near the candidate boundary and check active branches or face incidences. |
| **Geometry of the feature cloud.** Are there low-dimensional strata or distinct kinds of examples? | PCA, hierarchical clustering, diffusion/spectral embeddings, neighborhood graphs, local dimension estimates. Run with and without bucket labels and with alternative normalizations. | Translate axes or clusters back to actual shapes and feature formulas. Seek exemplars and boundary cases. Embedding structure must survive reasonable representation choices before geometric interpretation. |
| **Residuals and contradictions.** Which bodies defeat our best simple account? | Cross-fitted residual ranking, matched-feature/divergent-target pairs, model disagreement, nearest-neighbor contrasts, exceptional rule violations. | These are an efficient queue for new feature invention. Inspect why apparently similar objects differ, define a new quantity, and test it on additional contrasting pairs. Do not tune only to the first hand-picked pair. |
| **Compression by a short formula.** Can a complicated predictor be replaced by mathematics? | Sparse polynomial/rational fits, symbolic regression, dimension/scaling-constrained expression grammars, small rule lists. Calibrate first on a known exact family. | Verify on adversarial and off-grid examples, seek exact coefficients, then prove or disprove. Allow piecewise laws because capacity branches may change; a smooth global fit can hide the essential structure. |
| **Representation discovery.** Are scalar summaries throwing away the relevant arrangement? | Richer incidence graphs, fields of face quantities, sets of facet or vertex data, graph kernels, permutation-invariant set models, modest autoencoders or contrastive embeddings. | Compare to explicit feature baselines and inspect disagreement examples. A successful representation is useful even before a theorem, but needs interpretable probes or simplification to serve the mathematical account. |
| **Transformation response.** What changes when we act on a fixed shape? | Paired scans over rotations, determinant-one transforms, support perturbations, truncations or facet insertion; functional summaries of trajectories; variance decomposition over shape versus transformation. | Separate intrinsic shape effects from alignment and representation artifacts. Identify a response law, symmetry, or failure of monotonicity; then investigate it analytically in a simple family. |
| **Active measurement.** Which unevaluated body or feature would clarify a relation? | Committee disagreement, uncertainty sampling with diversity, targeted tests of competing formulas, batches balanced across unexplored regimes. | Evaluate where rival hypotheses differ, not only where a predictor expects high sys. Compare useful new distinctions per total compute against a small random control stream. |
| **Extremal search.** Can we find a new threshold example or stronger extremum? | Random multistart, local derivatives/direct search, evolutionary populations, surrogate ranking, low-dimensional Bayesian optimization, hybrid restarts. | Validate promising bodies independently; cluster endpoints and simplify witnesses. Evaluate candidate quality per total cost. Optimizer ranking alone is not the mathematical objective. |
| **Diverse extremes.** Are there multiple unrelated ways to be interesting? | Quality-diversity archives indexed by a few interpretable descriptors; maintain best sys, largest formula violation, or strongest residual in each region. | Produces a library of different witnesses rather than one champion. Compare structures between occupied regions; map whether continuation connects them. Descriptor choice strongly shapes the archive. |
| **Rare events and accessibility.** How much of a generator reaches a specified region? | Stratified sampling, importance sampling with recorded weights, tail diagnostics, carefully specified splitting designs. | Useful for search-allocation and geometric prevalence questions. Separate a good search policy from a valid probability estimator; adaptively selected samples do not retain the initial generator law. |
| **Local landscape anatomy.** What explains stalls or transitions? | Trace analysis, derivative comparisons, active-set signatures, branch-gap time series, matched restarts, local directional probes. | Extract recurring obstructions or conjectures about extremizers. Repeated optimizer endpoints do not establish local maximality; use mathematical or certified checks on selected cases. |
| **Validity discovery.** Which data patterns reveal a broken computation or definition? | Invariance tests, paired numerical/exact checks, residual dependence on solver status, feature missingness/failure clustering, duplicated shapes under different encodings. | Repair the measurement model before interpreting a spurious scientific regularity. Failures themselves can reveal numerical degeneracy strata worth understanding. |

The rows can exchange outputs. For example, target-free relations suggest a candidate inequality, inequality violations supply an active-query criterion, query results suggest a new feature, and symbolic fitting on the enriched stratum suggests a proof. There is no requirement to finish a large invariant list before doing anything with the first useful measurements.

## Representation and sampling choices that materially alter the methods

**Invariants are useful, not the only admissible observations.** A symplectic invariant with known scale/translation behavior is convenient for intrinsic statements. Raw normals, support numbers, Euclidean moments, or coordinate-dependent descriptors can nevertheless expose alignment phenomena or help predict an invariant target. They need an explicit transformation law or matched transformation tests. Permutation invariance of a set model alone does not grant translation, scaling or symplectic invariance. The full symplectic group is noncompact; a finite augmentation distribution neither samples it uniformly nor proves invariant behavior. Track whether an observed relation is intrinsic, equivariant, or specific to a coordinate convention.

**Heterogeneity is both asset and confounder.** Broad heterogeneous sampling can reveal exceptions and regime structure absent in a single family. Start with both pooled and stratified views rather than banning pooled discovery. Then ask if a pattern survives within strata, spans strata, or merely identifies the source. A rare stratum may be scientifically central despite contributing little pooled variance. Likewise, random train/test splits can make family interpolation look like transfer; use source/family/combinatorial splits when that is the scientific question.

**The response need not be sys.** Model capacity, volume, active-branch signatures, gaps, degeneracy, computation cost, failure predicates, and relations among geometric quantities. Capacity-derived orbit descriptors are valid post-computation explanatory objects; they are not cheap pre-capacity selection features unless separately computed at accounted cost. Scaling should be handled explicitly when comparing capacity and volume rather than eliminated reflexively: their separate responses may explain a ratio effect.

**High-dimensional data with costly labels favor staged measurements.** Preserve raw geometries once; cache cheap features; calculate an expensive feature or capacity only on selected bodies. Measure incremental feature cost and whether it adds a new scientific distinction. Small supervised sample size does not automatically rule out learned representations: cheap unlabeled geometries and known transformations may supply auxiliary training material. That is an opportunity with an implementation/validation cost, not grounds to launch a large neural project first.

**The population can change.** Keep a stable comparison stream, a discovery stream and targeted interventions distinguishable. Existing labeled bodies support inexpensive exploratory screens, while later batches can deliberately fill a gap, test a formula or search an extreme. Evaluation partitions should respect adaptive reuse. In a fully adaptive project, a new purpose-built challenge set is often more meaningful than pretending an old holdout remained untouched.

## Five runnable research designs worth scoping next

These are independent scouts, not mandatory thesis work. They can run largely on existing measurements; actual compute timing and data availability must be checked before assigning budgets.

1. **Target-free relation scout.** Inventory units/scaling degrees and algebraic identities, then inspect low-complexity relations across all supported columns, not merely sys. Deliver a handful of explicit formulas or inequalities, counterexamples already found, and the cheapest new bodies needed. Different output from existing correlation screening.
2. **Capacity/volume decomposition scout.** On a coherent retained intervention packet, plot and explain changes in each component alongside the ratio. Compare shape changes with alignment changes. Deliver a concrete explanation or an explicit unresolved component, not a regressor score.
3. **Representation blindspot scout.** Recover contrasting geometries matched on current summaries; compare their raw/incidence structures. Propose and implement one or two measurements that distinguish a meaningful subset, then challenge them on further pairs. An existing compact incidence-feature negative is input, not a blanket rejection of arrangement information.
4. **Adaptive hypothesis discriminator.** Take two low-complexity surviving descriptions and search cheap geometry/features for bodies where they disagree. Evaluate capacity on a small diverse set plus controls. Deliver which description survives, where neither does, and revised mathematical questions. This directly tests on-demand data generation's value.
5. **Symbolic calibration plus transfer.** Ask a formula-search pipeline to recover a known relation without receiving its formula; inspect whether its choices are interpretable and affordable. Then apply the same restricted procedure to another structured family. Recovery is a tooling check, not a new theorem or evidence the second family will yield one.

A scout may return no clean pattern. It is informative if it narrows a representation or relation class, identifies a meaningful counterexample, or shows the next measurement is required. Merely listing null p-values or saying an algorithm ran is insufficient.

## Primary-source precedents and their limited implications

- Davies et al., [Advancing mathematics by guiding human intuition with AI](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-04086-x) (2021), explicitly combine prediction, attribution and mathematical interpretation in an iterative process. Their examples support the existence of useful mathematics-discovery workflows; they do not establish success probabilities for polytopes or justify any particular estimator here.
- Udrescu and Tegmark, [AI Feynman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11481), explore symbolic regression exploiting mathematical structure. Relevant as a source of formula-discovery ideas; a fitted expression still needs independent checking and proof in this project.
- Mouret and Clune, [Illuminating search spaces by mapping elites](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.04909), motivate retaining high-quality solutions across descriptive regions. Adapting the archive to geometrically distinct polytopes is our proposal, not a demonstrated symplectic application.
- Settles, [From Theories to Queries: Active Learning in Practice](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v16/settles11a.html), provides practical active-learning context. Here the query can be a newly generated object or an additional measurement, and the objective can be distinguishing mathematical explanations rather than minimizing prediction error.
- Zaheer et al., [Deep Sets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.06114), provide permutation-invariant set architectures. This addresses unordered facets/vertices only; the additional geometric group actions remain a separate design obligation.

## What not to infer

The project has not exhausted empirical approaches because a bounded method ledger closed. Nor does the expanded portfolio imply all these methods should be executed. The appropriate broadness is several distinct discovery operations with access to new representations and new data, followed by outcome-dependent mathematical work. Selecting a small initial batch is reasonable only if it leaves those alternatives visible and does not silently redefine success as better prediction on the existing table.
