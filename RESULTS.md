## Mathematical Research

[main result][conjectural]
- HKO2024 is a local maximum of sys.
  - [sub][conjectural] This is the broad local-maximality claim; the subresults below say which parts are proved, which are empirical, and which remain future work.
  - [sub][M_10][writeup-gap][verification-gap] The intended proved subresult is local maximality on M_10 modulo the natural sys symmetries.
  - [sub][M_10][proof-plan] In dual-vertex coordinates R^40, prove that the Clarke-flat first-order directions are exactly the 15-dimensional tangent space generated by translations, scaling, and Sp(4).
  - [sub][M_10][verification-gap] Write the exact Q(sqrt(5)) linear algebra record: the active-gradient flat subspace has dimension 15, the infinitesimal symmetry space has dimension 15, and the two spaces are equal.
  - [sub][M_10][evidence] Current first-order computations give 0 in the convex hull of the active gradients and a 15-dimensional flat subspace, matching the symmetry count.
  - [sub][M_10][caveat] The theorem should state local maximality modulo symmetries. It should not claim strictness in raw R^40, where translations, scaling, and symplectic linear maps preserve sys.
  - [sub][M_10][caveat] The second-order flat-direction write-up is an older route. The symmetry-space proof should either replace it or state how the curvature checks fit the quotient picture.
  - [sub][M_10][empirical] Numerical F=10 perturbation checks have not found a sys improvement over HKO2024.
  - [sub][M_10][pending] LICCA-scale F=10 perturbation runs are the next numerical stress test for the HKO2024 neighborhood.
  - [sub][beyond-M_10][empirical] F=10 to F=11 facet-splitting and cut-and-ascent checks did not find a sys improvement over HKO2024.
  - [sub][beyond-M_10][pending] Larger-F validation, for example F=12 or F=13, remains future work.

[main result][empirical][pending]
- The sys landscape appears hostile to generic search for new sys > 1 examples.
  - [sub][evidence] Random generic polytopes, random Lagrangian products, rotated regular products outside the known pentagon case, fixed-F ascent, and variable-F ascent have not produced a new sys > 1 example.
  - [sub][caveat] Current seed counts are too small for a strong density claim; LICCA-scale ascent sampling is load-bearing evidence for that part of the headline.
  - [sub][mechanism] The landscape has many local maxima, and the non-HKO local maxima found so far stay below sys = 1.
  - [sub][mechanism] HKO2024 has a small, anisotropic sampled sys > 1 neighborhood in LP(5,5) coordinates; current radius and volume-fraction estimates should be quoted only with their experiment record.
  - [sub][evidence] In the one-dimensional regular pentagon rotation family, sys > 1 occupies a visible interval around the HKO angle, but shape perturbations add many narrow directions.
  - [sub][evidence] Variable-F ascent often improves lower local maxima, but the observed improvements still stay below sys = 1.
  - [sub][mechanism] Combinatorial cells are narrow, anisotropic, and crossed frequently by gradient steps; sys behaves continuously across tested boundaries while gradients and active orbits can change sharply.
  - [sub][evidence] A bounded standard-method pattern-search pass on normalized random and ascent-endpoint datasets found regime-specific but non-transferable structure: cheap geometry helps within random, richer sigma-local orbit/KKT scalars improve the random `orbit` block further, sigma-local orbit features still improve endpoint prediction relative to the other non-metadata blocks, fixed-`F` trajectory aggregates from `step_events.jsonl` stay near-null, metadata still dominates the endpoint regime, and transfer stays strongly negative.
  - [sub][artifact] The closure packet lives in `experiments/sys-landscape/feature-pattern-search/` with `feature_geometry.jsonl`, `feature_skeleton.jsonl`, `feature_omega.jsonl`, `feature_orbit.jsonl`, `feature_trajectory.jsonl`, and `feature_pattern_search_summary.md`; fixed-`F` endpoint and random-baseline cache rows now also persist bounded `orbit_scalars`, while `variable_f_ascent` still relies on the one-best-sigma fallback path.
  - [sub][interpretation] The current data-science evidence therefore supports the hostile-landscape reading rather than a transferable search heuristic from generic random samples to ascent-found endpoints.
  - [sub][method] The hostile-search claim rests on reproducible fixed-F and variable-F ascent runs, with capacity and orbit computations recorded by the experiment pipeline.
  - [sub][caveat] Random polytope distributions and Lagrangian product families are reproducible baselines, not uniform models of all convex bodies.
  - [sub][interpretation] Blind sampling is therefore a weak strategy unless it is guided by structure near a known high-sys family.
