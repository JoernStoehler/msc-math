# Bounded novelty check: sharp EHZ systolic ratio for all six-dimensional simplices

Checked 29 October 2026. The canonical statements inspected were `topics/capacity-and-qp/convex-systolic-optimization/sections/sixdim-simplex.tex` (local exact golden example) and `sixdim-global.tex` (all-six-simplex upper bound and unique equality class). The new claimed theorem is
`c_EHZ(K)^3/(6 Vol_6 K) <= (945+75 sqrt(5))/1922 = 0.5789308524...`
for every six-simplex, with equality in one class modulo translation, positive dilation, and linear symplectomorphism. Its proof combines the known simplex capacity/order formulation, a five-vertex Pfaffian cubic, and a computer-assisted exact classification of the six-item ordering relaxation. Mathematical proof and replay audit belong to their other owners; this note addresses prior art.

## Assessment

I found **no earlier proved sharp global six-simplex inequality with this constant or its unique equality class** in the inspected primary sources and targeted current searches. The strongest nearby 6D source found is Leipold's numerical optimization; the exact golden hypergraph value predates the project, and the general simplex capacity/order method predates it. A calibrated description is therefore: *a source-checked candidate for a new sharp global six-dimensional simplex theorem and rigidity classification*, using known combinatorial ingredients. This is bounded negative evidence, **not** a priority certificate or independent proof audit.
