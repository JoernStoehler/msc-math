## Surface Longlist And Disposition

### Method Families

- Covered for narrow retained wording: target scan, row/provenance audit, EDA
  and tail summaries, source/facet/product summaries, scalar associations,
  PCA/projection, k-means/anomaly diagnostics, ridge/RF in-table ranking,
  shallow tail rules, metadata/provenance baselines, permutation/bootstrap
  checks where packet READMEs cite them.
- First-wave design/review: standard-repertoire disposition audit against
  `method-coverage-checklist.md` and current method READMEs.
- Conditional execution: tiny retained-table missing-baseline packet covering
  lasso/elastic-net, gradient boosting, high-tail classification, and
  feature-family ablation under grouped holdout.
- Park unless audit promotes: SVM/kNN/kernel methods, neural/autoencoder,
  Bayesian/GP/posterior methods, density/mixture/one-class methods,
  derivative-free local/global optimization, Bayesian optimization, and direct
  search variants without a new source interface.
- Reject for this slice unless a new interface appears: time series,
  trajectory methods, reinforcement learning, bandits, online optimization, and
  endpoint/ascent-specific methods routed to another thesis slice.

### Generated-Candidate Proposers

- Covered as boundary evidence: scalar generated-candidate filters selected
  before `sys`, including the compact 100k `promising-scalars` packet.
- First-wave design/review: decide whether to close scalar-filter proposers for
  now or freeze exactly one two-feature rescue design.
- Conditional execution only after design: independent generated-candidate
  validation with rule frozen before `sys`.

### Producer And Distribution Coverage

- Covered: current retained producer contract only.
- First-wave design/review: broader producer/distribution scout separating
  independent same-contract seeds, height intervals, facet ranges, product side
  ranges, Latin-hypercube/space-filling parameter designs, and alternative
  random polytope models.
- No producer run in the first wave unless a packet card names the thesis
  sentence, one producer axis, stopping rule, resource estimate, and review
  standard.

### Mechanism And Geometry

- Covered as diagnostic: ridge magnitude is a bucket-local empirical signal;
  concentration features remain seeds; pure product/combinatorial proxy is too
  strong; small-area fractions look weak.
- First-wave design/review: thesis-safe mechanism wording audit using
  `ridge-mechanism-discriminator/` and topic files.
- Conditional execution: freeze one concentration-rule validation plan if
  mechanism wording or proposer rescue needs it.
- Parked: HKO-local conclusions, unless an explicit HKO-local or mechanism
  packet reopens them.

### Tail And Rare Events

- Covered: zero-positive facts for retained tables and model-sensitive tail
  summaries.
- First-wave design/review: tail thesis-use audit separating source-backed
  zero-positive facts from model-sensitive extrapolations.
- Parked: calibrated hit-rate/density language and expensive blind scale-up
  until stronger tail/generator evidence exists.

### Theory/Proof Bridges And Figures

- Theory bridges are useful if they turn ridge observations into falsifiable
  geometric statements. They are not first-wave execution unless a mechanism
  audit names the measured object and falsifier.
- Figures/inspection views are useful only after the claim they support is
  named. Do not start with figure polish.

## Stop And Failure Rules

This design is not full slice completion. The P1/P3 design milestone and P2
execution/review/synthesis milestone are complete. The next milestone is
complete only when a parent launches or implements P5, or records a concrete
loud-failure/continuation prompt and owner in `active-work.md`.

Fail loudly if:

- P1 cannot classify method families without changing the definition of
  standard repertoire;
- P3 cannot name producer axes without making unsupported random-model claims;
- a packet prompt cannot state its thesis sentence or source artifact;
- any packet tries to turn in-table prediction into a generated-candidate
  proposer claim;
- bounded retained wording is used to close the full slice without explicit
  Jörn or thesis-control downgrade.
