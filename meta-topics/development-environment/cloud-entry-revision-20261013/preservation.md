Read-only review, 13 October 2026; checkout `/workspaces/msc-math`. Reviewed root AGENTS.md plus the narrowly relevant owners listed below. No repository edits, tests, cloud launches or deployment assessment.

## Keep explicit in the root entry

1. Project identity and completion bar: multiple related results; claims/caveats/references/reproduction promises must match support; builds and tests establish only part of completion. Avoid a generic “follow best practices” substitute.
2. Work in the assigned checkout/environment. Workstation paths are examples for this workstation, not requirements imposed on a cloud machine. Explicitly assigned cloud work follows the cloud owner. A local file/commit being present does not establish delivery or instruction loading elsewhere.
3. Start from task-relevant owners, search claims/symbols/artifact names and synonyms before concluding absence, follow summaries to sources. Legacy/generated trees are poor broad orientation defaults.
4. Preserve the evidence chain and claim-strength boundary: paper → formal argument → implementation/experiment → thesis. Finite experiments, exact arithmetic and LaTeX builds do not supply missing implications. This is scientific work's central invariant, not just conditional producer procedure.
5. Put new working material in task/run scratch; retain selected lasting results with their scientific/meta-topic owner. Inspect expensive/replacing producers before running. Detailed Git/R2 mechanics can be conditional.
6. Preserve brief-acknowledgment continuation: “thx” does not stop unfinished authorized work. Also preserve its boundaries: stop at completion, explicit stop, or a clearly pending required input; do not invent work after completion.
7. Retain exact-message review as an explicit obligation/trigger with direct synchronous discussion and private native returns exempt. Skill descriptions alone help selection but are not equivalent to this project-wide gate; cloud recipients may lack automatic skill discovery. If review is unavailable, preserve pending delivery.
8. Preserve substantial-spending approval and useful preliminary exploration together. Unknown numeric thresholds must not become a gate for every small authorized task; splitting sessions does not enlarge authorization.
9. Async questions remain prohibited pending verified delivery; direct chat is the fallback. Submission, silence, elapsed time and automatic action approval do not answer a required plan decision. Preserve optional-question fallback so this does not become indiscriminate gating.
10. Bounded delegation needs scope/output/resources/stop-or-review point/receiving owner; integration/commit/cleanup ownership and actual tool authorization matter. Coordinator supervision includes pending reviews/dependencies as well as available telemetry; missing telemetry/quiet threads do not establish spare capacity.

Items 6, 8 and 9 are recurring corrective rules with user/source context; shortening must not retire their interpretation-critical boundaries.
