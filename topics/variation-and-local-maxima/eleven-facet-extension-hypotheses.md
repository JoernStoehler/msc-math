## Possible cuts-additivity route at the exact base body

The prompt conjectured that Proposition 1.13 and its appendix in
[Haim–Kislev's extrema paper](../literature/open-thesis/extrema.md),
arXiv:2511.16644v1, might cover exact base-HKO normals exposing two-faces or
facets. Its preliminary reasoning was that pure-factor normals are explicit,
and an edge normal belongs to both endpoint cones, potentially permitting
paired endpoint indices differing by 0 or ±1 modulo five. This was the
prompt author's deduction, not a statement verified from the paper.
Factor order, rotation, symplectic signs and the theorem's actual hypotheses
need checking before using the deduction.

If an applicable cuts-additivity theorem gave `c(R)=c0-c(C)` for a removed cap
`C` and retained body `R` of the fixed HKO body, the desired systolic-ratio
comparison would reduce to

```text
c(C)/c0 >= 1 - sqrt(1 - Vol(C)/V0).
```

This is a conditional route to investigate. The existing insertion estimate
supplies quadratic capacity loss, while two-face cap volume is quadratic and
facet cap volume linear; coefficient control or a stronger estimate remains
necessary. Capacity loss alone does not settle the ratio comparison.

## Representation and uniformity obligations

A fixed-body or fixed-direction estimate would not establish a coupled
neighborhood theorem. Cut depth, added-normal direction and old-normal motion
may vanish at different rates; preserve the zero-slope cone and symmetry
quotient. Exact-base coverage does not automatically persist after old facets move.
A direction-dependent near-boundary allowance must become uniform before a
compactness argument can be used.

An at-most-eleven-facet Hausdorff statement also requires a representation
bridge: relevant nearby bodies must admit ten labelled normalized rows tending
to the HKO rows and at most one bounded extra row. Exclusion of fewer than ten
facets nearby and classification of redundant-interior versus boundary limits
need justification. None of these obligations was closed by the launch prompt
or this retention edit. The selected thesis's ten-facet result retains its scope.
