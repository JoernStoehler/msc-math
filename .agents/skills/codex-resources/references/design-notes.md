This complete sectioned baseline was independently reviewed and integrated into project discovery on 6 October 2026. Guidance integration adds no account action, monitor, scheduler, fallback executor or live reset authority. The inactive earlier candidate remains at its review owner.

The [investigation handoff](../../../../docs/history/reset-investigation-20261005/skill-maintenance-handoff.txt) reports the actual ownership gap and Jörn's codex-usage naming objection. Its production refusal cause remains unknown; mock CLI behavior is bounded evidence. Protocol documentation was read on 6 October; it supplies response/attempt contracts, not a diagnosis of the refusal. Raw private account evidence is not reproduced.

Start with one complete sectioned account-resource workflow. Checking, allocation and action have distinct permissions but often share facts: an expiring credit may require an already authorized scheduled action; a refusal needs fresh state and a recovery owner. A split into tracking plus resets is still a candidate if useful behavior establishes that reduced loading outweighs the extra route/selection burden. The comparison is in the [review package](../../../../docs/coordination/skill-candidates/20261006/resource-workflow-review.md). No measured optimum or behavioral gain is claimed.

Keep the existing Claude-cloud setup workflow separate and propose a discriminating name for it. Account resources do not own sign-in/bootstrap, universal model selection or scientific task choices. Supervision remains with the coordinator; this skill routes existing observation capabilities rather than reimplementing them.

The next important execution gap is a selected, tested persistent recovery mechanism. A draft contract does not implement it. Test refusal, ambiguous success, clear new attempt and expiry with a local mock before a future production promise. No real redemption is required to assess the control flow.
