## Remembered unfinished work can still lack a next action

Configuration completed its accounting repairs legitimately. Its final answer also named the unresolved selected need for ongoing resource awareness, then ended without issuing the proposed discussion question. Recurring information read its running, owned, unblocked original design task, changed it to ready while retaining its owner and unfinished objective, and ended because its narrower “bounded pass” was complete. Thus missing task memory alone does not explain these endpoints. The state writes and endings express the same narrowing; the record does not show that writing `done` or `ready` mechanically caused termination. [Report: C L1609–1643](../history/session-postmortem-2026-10-04/report.html#c-l1609-component); [R L1960–2099](../history/session-postmortem-2026-10-04/report.html#r-l1963-next)

The recoveries distinguish **available authority/information, selection of a useful concrete next operation, and actual execution**. Project structure had already specified its proposed drafting package before stopping; preparation resumed from existing facts. Recurring information instead remained stuck after Jörn restarted it, until Fix supplied a concrete delegated comparison. Restarting inference was not enough in that case. [Report: S L1262–1365](../history/session-postmortem-2026-10-04/report.html#s-l1262-gap); [R L2056/L2087–2099](../history/session-postmortem-2026-10-04/report.html#evidence-r)

Fix was already receiving one recurring-information artifact; that did not transfer the remaining original design obligation. An artifact recipient, a stored next step and an owner label therefore do not establish that the parent work has a functioning continuation. This is narrower than requiring every root to continue indefinitely: configuration later completed its selected local accounting outcome, recurring information obeyed Fix's explicit resource-stop directive, and substantial execution remained genuinely unapproved. ## A supervisor can observe the right event and judge the wrong scope

The configuration supervisor read the root's final answer at 22:16:59, accepted the local runtime-adoption check and ended at 22:17:31. It had seen the broader sustained-delivery caveat. More frequent polling would not fix missing judgment about an event already observed. The encrypted assignment was not recovered, so responsibility cannot be assigned between a narrow parent brief and the child's interpretation. The supervisor also found stale state, delivered useful usage reports and later checked a valid local completion. [Report: C L2463–2497](../history/session-postmortem-2026-10-04/report.html#c-l2474); [supervisor L201–225/L464](../history/session-postmortem-2026-10-04/report.html#cs-l205)

## Content, intent and delivery failed in different ways

Some questions demonstrably transferred retrievable analysis back to Jörn: he explicitly requested comparative value work, then received broad menus, context-poor proof questions or requests to repeat already-returned prose history. Useful open expert critique did not require predetermined answer choices; it required an adequate object and purpose. By contrast, a transcript/question mismatch alone does not establish whether a question was displayed, read or semantically adequate. [Report: P L534–562](../history/session-postmortem-2026-10-04/report.html#p-l534); [T L1466–1512](../history/session-postmortem-2026-10-04/report.html#t-l1466); [recurring-information question evidence](../history/session-postmortem-2026-10-04/report.html#r-l1517)

Task design emitted an async question, accurately called delivery unconfirmed, then ended and waited through the known-unverified route. Server acceptance established emission, not frontend rendering or receipt. At 22:49:14 it repeated the full request directly in commentary and kept the turn open; later waiting was not exclusively tool-dependent. The exact disappearance mechanism remains unproved. [Report: T L1601–1662](../history/session-postmortem-2026-10-04/report.html#t-l1603)

## What the validation permits

The six-case generic paper test found no robust incremental decision benefit from the earlier note. A distinct four-task historical-reconstruction test compared a curated archive against that archive plus a frozen report packet. All eight answers were adequate; small qualification gains did not change adequacy. Packet users read **75,940 versus 70,786 words** and were slower in every pair. The largest score difference had unequal information: only the packet supplied a separate Fix probe. **No reconstruction-cost saving was demonstrated.** Both arms already benefited from investigator curation, so this does not estimate the value of investigating raw logs. [Generic study](../history/session-postmortem-2026-10-04/tests/transfer/REPORT.md); [historical reconstruction study](../history/session-postmortem-2026-10-04/tests/archival-consumption/analysis.md)

Those tests used current Astra agents and short tasks, not historical Sol roots in sustained work. All historical roots and compared children requested Sol/high; their different task/context roles are not a model comparison. Neither the operational account nor these trials establishes an internal model cause, a reliable prevention mechanism or avoided future human labor.

This shortened note removes repeated general advice and routes by the historical distinction needed. It was revised after the tested snapshot; reduced length is not a tested behavioral improvement. The evidence remains available without making whole-note exposure a new routine or asking Jörn to reconstruct the same incidents.
