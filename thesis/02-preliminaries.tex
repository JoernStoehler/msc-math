\section{Preliminaries}\label{sec:preliminaries}

This section fixes the notation used later for convex bodies, Reeb orbits, and
Clarke's dual action principle. All convex bodies are compact convex subsets of
\(\mathbb{R}^4\) with non-empty interior. Unless stated otherwise, we assume
that the origin lies in the interior.
