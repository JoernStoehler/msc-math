## Frozen formula

For P={x:a_i.x<=1}, analytic center z uniquely maximizes
sum_i log(1-a_i.z). Put x_i=a_i/[n(1-a_i.z)]. First-order stationarity gives
sum x_i=0; alpha_i=1/[n(1-a_i.z)] sum to 1 by dotting stationarity with z.
These are strictly positive normalized closure weights. Construct y_j likewise.

For EACH cyclic order (0,permutation(1..n-1)) of A weighted steps, let U be
its prefix points before each A step. Independently place each B step y_j in
a gap maximizing p.y_j. Then Q=1/2 sum_j max_{p in U} p.y_j. Maximize over
the (n-1)! A cyclic orders. This exactly optimizes WORDS for the ONE selected
closure pair. It does not enumerate, optimize or respond in closure weights.
Set C_ac=1/(2 Q), S_ac=C_ac^2/(2 area(P)area(Q)). This is an upper-bound
functional, not a newly claimed exact capacity. Compare width separately;
do not take a minimum as a post-result rescue.
