\subsection{Executable exact verification of the HKO bounds}
\label{app:hko-cas}

This appendix isolates the finite certificate used in
Lemma~\ref{lem:hko-exact-bounds}. The listings below form a complete
SageMath program. Facet labels and positions in each word are
displayed one-based; the executable converts them to zero-based indices at
the section boundary.

The geometric lemmas in the main text establish that these ten labelled dual
vertices form the relevant chart, that volume is smooth there, and that each
positive feasible section supplies a touching upper bound.  The finite CAS
task is to instantiate 26 such sections, differentiate their upper bounds,
and check the quotient certificate.

\paragraph{Symmetry and finite conclusion.}
The final source block constructs the four translations, dilation, and ten
$\mathfrak{sp}(4)$ columns, then checks symmetry annihilation, row rank 25,
and a strictly positive normalized left-kernel relation. The check
\texttt{q(w,b)==qm} establishes that each section gives the known HKO action
at the base point, so its upper bound touches the systolic ratio there.
Thus the explicitly assigned \texttt{rows}, together with the computed
\texttt{rr} and \texttt{lam}, witness the existence assertions of
Lemma~\ref{lem:hko-exact-bounds}. The rank and positive relation supply the
hypotheses of Lemma~\ref{lem:hko-negative-slopes}.
