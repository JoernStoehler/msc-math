# Project management

This topic owns reusable project-management knowledge and lasting obligations:
what completion means, which decisions remain pending and how human-facing
coordination stays consistent with its sources. Current dispatches and changing
resource observations remain in [coordination](coordination.md),
whose `current.json` feeds the dashboard; an old plan here does not authorize work.

[AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) owns the spending and delegation boundaries.
[Collaboration rationale](../agent-workflow/collaboration-rationale.md) gives the
source and open choices behind those boundaries. A pending required decision
must survive handoff; a deferred proposed solution does not dispose of its parent
objective. [Planning context](planning-context.md) retains the September examples
and attributed choices underlying that distinction, with dated priorities kept
separate from today's selected work.

[Outcome coverage](outcome-coverage.md) records a bounded September discovery of
thesis needs beyond its proposed session portfolio. It remains historical input;
[current thesis planning](thesis-work.md),
[project facts](../../docs/project-facts.md) and the selected manuscript own
current obligations. The [optional working card](optional-working-card.md) is a
human habit aid and carries no agent authority or validation claim.

[Collaboration friction](friction/README.md) owns the stable F01–F16 records,
status meanings, evidence and reconciliation contract for the Pages overview.
Moving those records changes their encounter path; it does not resolve a friction
item or establish new monitoring. Current status changes require dated evidence.

The canonical [registry](current.json), [thesis obligations](thesis-work.md),
[handoff](handoff.md) and [generated graph](graphs/tasks.svg) live here.
[The frozen pre-migration snapshot](../../docs/history/coordination/current-state-before-pm-migration-2026-10-12.md)
retains dated resource/change accumulation removed from live presentation; it
is historical recovery evidence and carries no current authorization.

[Expired-window dispositions](expired-window-retention-2026-10-12.md) distinguish
retained obligations and historical design evidence from retired launch requests.
