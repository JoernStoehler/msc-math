## Strategies currently disfavored and why

- Scaling many near-identical reviewers as the acceptance gate: observed shared endorsements conflict with human judgments. This does not rule out genuinely different specialists or retaining a useful narrow check.
- More emphatic prohibitions alone: user's hypothetical and observed residual habits motivate broader search; no universal impossibility result.
- Treating locations as sufficient guidance: directly unsuccessful in this small screen.
- Treating explicit-feedback compliance, majority vote, fewer complaints, or improved drafts as proof of PASS: these omit the actual acceptance criterion.
- Inferring detector precision from selective annotations or treating unmarked spans as clean: invalid labels.
- Spending the preparation phase on a universal detector before checking a complete repair cycle: may optimize an intermediate task with little downstream benefit.
- Giving all reviewers one authoritative interpretation: may synchronize their blindspots. Context provision itself is still necessary in some tasks.

These are scoped reservations, not permanent exclusions. Identify counterexamples or conditions that would reverse them.
