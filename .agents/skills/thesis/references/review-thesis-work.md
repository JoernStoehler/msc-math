# Review Thesis Work

Use this reference for a judgment-based review of thesis-facing prose or an
integrated thesis artifact. Choose lenses according to the candidate and its
downstream decision; this is not a complete finding taxonomy.

- **Correctness:** claims hold under their stated conditions.
- **Source support:** the relevant proof, citation, artifact, or accepted
  decision supports the wording.
- **Claim strength:** theorem, computation, empirical evidence, conjecture,
  and cited literature remain distinguishable.
- **Thesis fit:** the material answers important reader questions and serves
  its downstream use.
- **Reader understanding:** the intended reader can form the intended model
  without repository knowledge.
- **Writing quality:** purpose, order, coherence, precision, emphasis, and
  length reduce effort and misunderstanding.
- **Scope:** required source material is absorbed or deliberately excluded
  without unrelated expansion.
- **Presentation:** notation, equations, references, citations, assets, and
  the rendered surface support reading.

For a judgment-based finding, cite a concrete location and explain its effect.
Report important findings outside these reminders. Separate review passes when
mathematical correctness, source strength, reader effect, or presentation
would otherwise crowd one another out.

Agents own ordinary review. Escalate theorem-strength wording, advisor
framing, thesis scope, and final readiness when the decision genuinely
requires Jörn or an advisor.
