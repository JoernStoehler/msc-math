## Scientifically consequential omissions

- **Permutation screen:** omit the coarse family-max p-value, since the section reports an exploratory association rather than significance or confirmatory inference. Its absence must not be read as independent replication.
- **Face enumeration:** omit the numerical missing-face caveat and the sum-versus-mean constant conversion. The section defines the mathematical sum unambiguously, but it does not establish complete historical feature extraction. That technical obligation remains in the source packet.
- **Mixed-area lower bound:** omit R ≥ 8, its equality characterization, and the stronger triangular bound. The chosen question is the descriptor's relation to the target, for which the explicit adverse paths suffice. This section does not explain the full geometry of descriptor minima.
- **Capacity proof detail:** refer to the pentagon account rather than reproduce endpoint interpolation and the six-facet word. The ridge algebra is shown; the capacity profile is an imported theorem, not inferred from the statistics.
- **Bounce decomposition and two-bounce width theorem:** omit because they require their own notation and unresolved theorem-review context. The section offers one exact explanation, not a complete mechanism for the retained product association.
- **Optimizer results:** omit the 448-run comparison and historical 0.984783 median. Their branch-envelope reasoning belongs in the companion optimizer account identified in the opening. This short section is not a complete DS chapter or an overall search-method ranking.
- **Orientation and tangentialization panels:** omit both current panels and the older fixed-champion orientation study. They answer intervention/allocation questions beyond the ridge-descriptor selection question. No claim of current-code replication of the principal association is made.
- **Transfer effect sizes:** the supplied summary gives finite enrichment but no numerical mean differences for the fresh selector/transfer examples. None are invented. As a result, the section establishes the direction and bounded existence of enrichment without conveying its practical size.
- **Implementation/replay receipts:** omit hashes, paths, schema differences and reproduction commands. The final preserves the historical target limitation but is not a reproducibility appendix.
