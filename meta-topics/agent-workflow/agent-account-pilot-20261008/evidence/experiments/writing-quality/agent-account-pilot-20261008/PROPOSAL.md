# Proposed route to a complete agent-facing thesis account

8 October 2026, `/root`, fde8 worktree. This is the proposal requested after a
15-minute exploration, not approval or activation of the next programme.

## Recommendation

Build an integrated scientific account by reusing existing packets and filling
their missing connections. Do not routinely rewrite complete proofs into a
second maintained copy. The account must explain the selected results and
their relationships, enough for an agent to reconstruct the science and write
from it; its document boundaries need not match the final chapters.

The first useful continuation is a bounded **45–60-minute tranche**, with one
integrator and at most two substantive workers. These are proposed wall-clock
caps, not measured throughput or a monetary estimate. Actual quota/billed spend
are unknown. No paid API, new scientific producer or expensive certificate
regeneration is proposed. Follow-on execution needs Jörn's selection; this
15-minute task selected exploration and a proposal only.

Independent source-fidelity assessment found no substantive contradiction in
the candidates, but exposed a numerical-account gap: “ten fits” did not explain

or the floating-point trust boundary. This matters for the implementation story,
not the elementary proof's validity. Revision 2 adds that interface, explicit
symmetrization-midpoint attainment and the thesis selection/notation interface.
A targeted repeated consumer recovered those additions; source review found no
scientific error introduced by the repair. This demonstrates a concrete local
feedback cycle, not mathematical certification or downstream human acceptance.

Evidence is retained in `input-manifest.json`, `consumer-task.md`,
`reference-summary.md`, the three `consumer-*.md` initial responses,
`full-account-v2.md` and `consumer-full-v2-targeted.md`. All initial inputs
remain frozen. No human text was newly drafted or graded; the existing two-page
human-section candidate and figure were reused as source material.

Limits: one unusually well-developed proof, one consumer per condition,
different information supplied to each condition, no randomized repeated trial,
shared model lineage and possible prior knowledge. Closed-input questions favor
consolidated information; real agents can follow links. No reduction in cost,
whole-thesis readiness, human readability or absolute correctness was measured.

   mechanism and relationship to the others; retain the multiple-results frame.
   Use existing substantive packets where they suffice. Identify which sections

3. **Proof/evidence worker: test one more demanding existing account.** Use the

The integrator receives and checks both returns, resolves interface findings
and produces one coherent account checkpoint. Stop after one revision cycle
within the proposed tranche; return actual remaining gaps and effort, rather
than making indefinite review loops the new finishing task.

## Checking and eventual completion

Use two complementary checks. From account to sources, inspect exact support,
hypotheses, normalization, implications, data lineage and reproduction promises.
From relevant sources to account, search independently for omitted selected
results, dependencies, limitations and contradictions. The second direction
must not traverse only the account's declared links.

Fresh agents should perform scientific tasks: reconstruct a proof implication,
apply a theorem near its boundary, distinguish experiments and populations, or
trace a plotted result to its actual inputs. They can follow sources in the
next tranche; record which answers require retrieval and whether that retrieval
resolves the gap. A link check tests availability, a source hash tests change,
and a semantic review checks a specific relationship. None alone proves
correctness. Existing executable verifiers cover their stated predicates only.

For cheap reruns, retain dated source identities and previously checked
relationships. Changed statements or source dependencies trigger targeted
reinspection; new material outside declared dependencies requires broader
source discovery. No general framework, scores, critic training or automated
approval loop is needed to try this.

The agent-facing account is complete only when every selected content area has
its substantive account, required support and truthful limitations, and no
missing implication prevents the claimed conclusions. Scientific unknowns can
be explicit caveats where that is an honest selected outcome; an unresolved
required proof cannot be closed by labeling it unknown. Inclusion decisions
and human factual choices remain visible pending decisions.

After the harder cases establish a workable process, extend it over all selected
areas: foundations/algorithms, variation/HKO, empirical exploration, product side
results, computational trust/reproduction, and AI disclosure/reflection. Existing
chapters may already supply some accounts. Use the stable units for human
authoring while completing remaining units; scientific dependencies must be
checked before their human prose is treated as final. Human composition still
requires emphasis, prerequisites, pacing and editorial choices. Recheck that
expansion/omission/rephrasing preserve claims, then assess the actual integrated
PDF with Jörn and the recorded Kai flow. Agent success does not transfer human
PASS automatically.

Cheaper fallback: retain a compact synthesis plus supplements to existing
packets, and author directly from those and the already prepared human draft.
If an additional account mostly duplicates existing content or prompts repeated
rewriting without exposing consequential gaps, use that fallback. A successful
small feedback loop is a reason to attempt useful next work, not a reason to
commit to a full new writing infrastructure.
