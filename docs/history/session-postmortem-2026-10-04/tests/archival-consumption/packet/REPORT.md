# Postmortem: why the MSc/Codex roots required repeated human rescue

Substantial activity produced useful components, but repeatedly failed to provide the service Jörn needed: select worthwhile work, obtain his input while it was available, continue the authorized work, and leave results with trustworthy completion boundaries. Instead, he repeatedly had to restart roots, reconstruct their questions, restore the relevant comparison and challenge their reviews. The attempted repair session reproduced those failures. No substantial unattended research/thesis programme was approved and launched during the reviewed window.

The strongest explanation is more specific than “the agents forgot the goal.” In several decisive cases they **read or wrote the unfinished parent obligation immediately before ending**, but did not turn it into a concrete next action. They treated completion of their latest bounded activity as sufficient to stop. Sometimes an already-described step was available; sometimes further decomposition was needed and was later supplied by another agent. Their task records preserved the gap, but did not make it actionable. A supervisor sometimes saw the ending and accepted the same narrower result. Its ordinary native communication also could not restart an idle root.

A second mechanism affected planning. Conditions on specialist findings were lost when those findings were used to select work. Corrections changed the latest criticized feature without establishing that the recommendation met other still-applicable requirements. Four workers plus root integration persisted across substantially different proposals; reviewers improved work within that design without adequately comparing a larger team with delegated integration. These defects made plans look increasingly concrete without resolving the judgments that actually justified spending the window.

Neither mechanism explains every incident. Quota setup also involved misunderstanding the requested service and a fallback with shared dependencies. Configuration eventually achieved a legitimate local accounting outcome. The earlier error-list audit appears to have completed its narrower request. Some later stops were explicitly instructed. Those distinctions matter both for recovering useful work and for avoiding repairs that would turn legitimate completion or a real approval boundary into endless activity.

This investigation is retained now to avoid paying for the same reconstruction through Jörn's attention and future compute. Existing September guidance already covered parent-objective loss, inadequate local review and delivery problems. October adds evidence about **where those principles failed to control decisions**, which apparently plausible repairs did not address the mechanism, and which explanations the traces rule out. Saving another reminder is not itself evidence that behavior has improved.

The historical scope is eight MSc/Codex roots active during **18:24:26–23:24:26 UTC on 3 October 2026**, or **20:24:26–01:24:26 Berlin time**. Earlier messages establish assignments. Household power and host backups are excluded. Quota redemption was scheduled for **04:00 Berlin / 02:00 UTC**, after both the freeze and this report's deadline; its result remains pending. The later postmortem process and its transfer test are marked separately below. Source citations use root aliases and original frozen JSONL line numbers; the source key is at the end.

Three conditions must be distinguished: authority and information for further work exist; someone selects a worthwhile concrete next action; and a process actually executes it. The earliest recoverable break was therefore not always retrieval. It was the transition after local success: continue the selected parent work, issue a consequential request, or obtain an actual transfer. Marking a component complete, preserving a caveat and writing a `next_step` were insufficient. Existing structural checks of coordination files could verify their format and projection, but were never evidence that this transition was justified.

Negative cases prevent an indiscriminate persistence remedy. Configuration eventually verified the selected local accounting requirements and had a defensible endpoint. Recurring information's terminal park followed an explicit resource stop. Some substantial production and live activation remained genuinely unapproved. A guard that restarts every final answer or executes every stored next step would fail those cases. The stronger evidence changes the account of supervision. A renewed configuration watch did not merely miss an event between polls. It read the root's actual final answer and preceding task-state update.

At 22:16:41, the root reported two real successes: time/context information reached its running session, and supervised usage information had been delivered. It also said sustained delivery remained unestablished, then ended. At 22:16:59 the supervisor inspected that final. At 22:17:31 it ended its own watch, describing bounded documentation/review and a concrete user answer, and treating the runtime-adoption check as complete. The criterion it applied concerned the local check; it did not establish that the root's broader continuation was justified. [C L2463–2477](report.html#c-l2474); [supervisor source L201–205, L222–225](report.html#cs-l205)

Exact supervisor assignment payloads are encrypted in the inspected raw and native records. The report therefore cannot determine whether the parent gave it an inadequately narrow remit or whether it narrowed a broader remit itself. Its documented task and actual behavior establish the practical coverage gap. This is more informative than saying an independent reviewer existed: independent personnel can still evaluate the same incomplete completion condition.

The delivery route had a separate limitation. The root recorded the child's completion event at 22:17:31, but no new root turn began. Jörn's message started one at 22:17:35. The queued child report entered the root's context only after a subsequent tool boundary, marked `trigger_turn:false`. The observer's completion therefore did not itself recover the idle root. [C source L2478–2497](report.html#c-l2478)

This trace matches the inspected historical runtime. Root metadata reports Codex 0.160.0. The daemon still running at audit time also reported 0.160.0 and had started before the frozen window; the matching release source and binary provenance were retained. This is strong consistency evidence, not absolute historical binary attestation. In that release, child finals are queued with `trigger_turn=false`, native `send_message` is queue-only, and the native trigger-turn path rejects a root target. These are claims about that route and release, corroborated by the recorded event sequence—not a claim that roots cannot be restarted at all. Fix used a separate app-server message-to-thread route to restart roots successfully. [Runtime audit; release source `completion.rs:97–103`, `multi_agents_spec.rs:204`, `api.rs:127–133`; F action L183/result L188; R L1817](report.html#runtime-source)

Judgment and continuation effects are separate requirements. This episode did not contain a correct warning defeated by the transport: the observer endorsed the local endpoint. The non-waking behavior is nevertheless an established capability limit. If a correct warning were sent through that same native route only after root termination, it would not by itself resume the parent. A better restart mechanism would also not correct the observed narrow judgment. A supervisor's useful contract must therefore include what it judges, what it can observe, and what its authorized delivery/action path can actually cause. While the parent remains active, native warnings can still influence it; external controllers and other APIs can behave differently.

Supervision was not uniformly useless. It found stale approval state, helped deliver usage updates, and eventually checked a genuinely completed local outcome. Fix also performed useful restarts. Those successes establish particular mechanisms, not sustained control of the full assignment. The practical lesson is to trace observation through judgment to effect, rather than treating the presence of a watcher as protection.

## Plans became more detailed without preserving the whole case

Planning failed before the spending boundary, not because the boundary itself was imaginary. The agents had to establish which outcomes merited the available window and what Jörn needed to judge. They repeatedly promoted a narrower property into that larger conclusion.

Existing evaluators and measured paths supported the feasibility of an unattended saturation investigation. They did not establish its priority relative to proof simplification, supported exposition, other research or the human review burden left after the window. Jörn explicitly corrected the substitution. In priorities, a broad six-block writing plan became eight detailed production assignments after a vagueness objection. When Jörn asked what useful judgment he should make, it became a shorter broad permission question. After rejection of the missing value case, the root moved to smaller mathematical questions; one lacked sufficient context, and another asked for writing history already supplied by project facts. [T L218–231](report.html#t-l218); [P L366, L414, L428, L435, L534–553](report.html#evidence-p); [source L173–176](report.html#evidence-p)

The corrections were responsive locally, but did not reliably accumulate into a sufficient recommendation. Concreteness, a value case, manageable review effort, mathematical context and valid authority all mattered together. Improving the latest criticized dimension while leaving another requirement unsupported did not converge on a usable proposal. Some caveats remained explicitly written throughout; their presence did not make the missing comparison. This is observable in the successive objects, not a claim that the model deliberately optimized appearances. A risky programme can merit selection under justified uncertainty; proved success was not required.

## What the evidence now settles

The strongest conclusions are operational and conditional, rather than six equally plausible general causes.

**First, missing task memory is inadequate for the decisive stopping cases.** Known gaps were immediately written or reread before ending. The supported failure lies between that knowledge, selection of a concrete next contribution, and a justified stopping decision. Some cases had the next preparation already specified; others needed further decomposition and received it later from Fix. Attention or salience may still contribute internally; the traces do not measure them. Another persistence paragraph, automatic restart or dashboard refresh would not by itself supply a valuable missing next action.

**Second, local review and supervision did not consistently test the disputed transition.** Some reviews were valuable and some local endpoints valid. But specialist correctness, incorporated review findings and a completed runtime check could all coexist with an unclosed parent obligation or untested allocation. Independence must concern the particular assumption and evidence, not merely a different agent name. For the inspected native route, observation after root termination also lacked an automatic parent-resume effect.

**Third, task and plan constraints were not reliably preserved through refinement.** The successive proposals, child correction and staffing sequence support this directly. “More detail,” “ask a question,” “compare options” and “use a reviewer” each occurred without making the combined decision adequate. A future diagnosis should inspect the meaning carried from source finding to recommendation and from correction to revised proposal, not infer success from the presence of those activities.

**Fourth, authority boundaries were real but did not require all the observed inactivity.** Useful analysis and preparation resumed without new permission after several protests. Large execution and hook activation nevertheless remained gated, and some stops were explicit. Removing the gate would have allowed weakly selected work to run; it would not establish its value. Changing ownership or ending a turn cannot silently convert a waiting package into approved work.

**Fifth, several tempting explanations of individual incidents are contradicted or limited by the evidence.** Accounting's original twenty passing tests missed silent fork/missing-counter errors; later targeted cases and fixes were useful. Quota's self-accusation of treating a scheduled attempt as a completed reset conflicts with its repeated earlier caveats. More caveats would not repair its missing route analysis. Fix's literal confession of missing a 300-second deadline is not supported by the recorded 292-second interval, although its stale timing promise and unsuitable proposal remain problems. [C source L1394](report.html#c-l1394); [Q L1041 versus L197/L535/L721/L945/L1029](report.html#q-l1041); [F L693/L706/L777](report.html#f-l693)

The quota fallback illustrates the required specificity. Its Claude route invoked the same `redeem.py --redeem`, credit and idempotency key as the primary. It could recover some transient primary-process failures, but did not bypass a shared script/client defect and introduced another authentication/model dependency. Verified login and guarded timer startup did not exercise a Claude model turn or redemption. This establishes the installed dependency structure, not that all fallback designs are useless or that this one must fail. [Q L748](report.html#q-l748); [source L1294, L1470](report.html#q-l1294-command)

The stop-review experiments likewise do not establish a universal reliability verdict. Three wrapper replays changed conditions: one failed before reading evidence, one received 25 selected messages, and one received all 71 visible messages but no explicitly passed parent instruction/tool/task stack. The fresh reviewer's full effective context was uncaptured. Its wrong verdict cannot be attributed solely to missing context without a matched enriched comparison. The semantic runs took roughly 39–46 seconds; correct judgment and tolerable latency would both matter. No live hook was activated. [R source L906/L936/L1029/L1035/L1060–1082](report.html#r-l906); [L1465–1505/L1653](report.html#evidence-r)

The accepted messaging skill is a useful positive boundary. An initial general rule confused root replies with child finals; structural checks missed the scope error. Conditional routing and fresh probes exposed further receipt clutter before a revision handled one synthetic case and Jörn accepted it. That preserves a recovered local tool while limiting the behavioral claim. Discovery, suitable scope and one passing example are different from general reliability. [R L590–614, L745–806](report.html#evidence-r)

The useful bounded child outputs support a more specific explanation than a different model: in those cases, the checked proposition matched the conclusion returned. Roots then had to preserve its conditions while selecting a larger programme or the next action. The writing auditor's cap-versus-target correction successfully restored that connection. Other children helped form the weak broad plan, retained the four-worker/root-assembly pattern, or accepted the narrow stopping criterion. Delegation therefore did not remove the synthesis problem; a useful handoff had to preserve what was checked, under which conditions, and which parent decision it supported. [Research-choices child L122](report.html#rc-l122); [writing child L96/L128](report.html#wa-l96); [allocation child L74/L90](report.html#ta-l74); [supervisor L201–225](report.html#cs-l205)

Broader model, context and speed explanations remain less established. All eight roots and the compared children requested gpt-6.1-sol at high effort; this is not a model comparison or proof of equal inputs, tools or task difficulty. Narrow children and the same roots produced useful work. Large inputs and delayed responses coexist in the traces, but do not isolate context length, generation speed, scheduling or reasoning effort as the cause. The concrete allocation and continuation findings are stronger than a blanket claim of model incapacity or context overload.

## Costs, validation and the remaining uncertainty

No accepted substantial programme was launched in the reviewed window. Useful preparation did occur, and its presence shows that lack of large-package approval did not prevent all progress. The record cannot determine which alternative portfolio would have passed, exactly how much work would otherwise have moved into the window, or a monetary loss. Jörn's financial concern is his stated concern, not a measured charge. Future reset success remains unknown.

Recorded resources show concentration in roots: among 1,812 completed-response records from eight roots and 29 descendants, roots produced about 83% of output tokens and accounted for about 78% of input processing. Input repeatedly counts context, mostly cached; output includes reasoning and tool calls. Independent subprocess calls are a known gap. These quantities describe allocation, not an optimal ratio, a bill, user-attention minutes or proof of waste. [Recorded aggregates and method](report.html#resource-metrics). They make root concentration worth examining, while the event traces identify the actual work that remained serial.
