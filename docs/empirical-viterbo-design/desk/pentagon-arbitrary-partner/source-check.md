# Focused source check: symmetric partners of a regular pentagon

2026-09-18. Bounded primary-source check following the independently checked
[symmetric-partner proof](symmetric-partner-lemma.md). **No statement duplicating
the exact fixed-pentagon formula or its sharp symmetric-partner ratio was found
in the sources below. This is not a literature-wide novelty certificate.**

The responsible presentation is “an elementary fixed-pentagon consequence of
the normal-triangle criterion,” with attribution to that criterion and explicit
novelty uncertainty. Its value for this campaign is already concrete: it closes
capacity behavior for an infinite control class and interprets A2 for arbitrary
partners. More numerical evidence is unnecessary for that conclusion.

The 2025 [Vicente note](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07572) also appeared in the
search. Its stated subject is duality for Young-function/Luxemburg bodies,
not this fixed-pentagon class; only the abstract/scope was checked, so it is
not represented above as a complete exclusion audit.

Searches combined regular pentagon, centrally symmetric, two-bounce/2-periodic,
inradius, Minkowski billiards and normal triangles. They located the sources
above but did not locate the formula `(10+6sqrt(5))/25` in this context.
Keyword absence is weak negative evidence. A specialist may recognize the
containment as folklore or a corollary stated elsewhere. No external author
contact was made and none is implied by this check.

**Recommendation:** keep the self-contained proof and exact scope; do not label
it a new literature theorem in reader-facing prose yet. If publication-level
novelty becomes consequential, obtain a specialist literature judgment using
this short statement and source map. Do not hold empirical interpretation
hostage to that separate attribution question.
