# Empirical Research

Load only what the current role and decision need. The package separates
binding conventions, accepted evidence facts, and optional suggestions.

## Authority classes

- Binding conventions: `coordination-conventions.md`, `research-ledger.md`,
  `plan-and-review.md`, and `evidence-handling-conventions.md`. An owner may
  challenge one, but must coordinate before acting incompatibly.
- Accepted source truth: `evidence-facts.md`. Surface contradictory evidence;
  do not silently weaken it.

Files named `suggest-*` contain suggestions only. They promote considerations
and arguments to attention; they do not grant authority, require an
architecture, or justify rejecting a sound plan. Load only the cognitive aid
relevant now:

- `references/suggest-brainstorming.md`: generate and diversify questions and
  ideas;
- `references/suggest-assessing-experiments.md`: assess questions, proposals,
  outcomes, value, and cost;
- `references/suggest-investigation-and-delegation.md`: seconds/minutes checks,
  self versus subagent work, and simple execution routes;
- `references/suggest-interpretation.md`: observations, inferences, beliefs,
  and claim boundaries;
- `references/suggest-investigation-displays.md`: diagnostic tables and plots.

Read `references/improve-workflow.md` only when deliberately diagnosing,
testing, or editing this process knowledge.

## Adjacent skills

- Use `$subagent-prompting` when a fresh bounded assignment needs non-obvious
  context, ownership, or a return contract after its outcome and delegation
  reason are selected.
- Use `$rust` when experiment code engages project-specific mathematical,
  numerical, observability, performance, or reusable-crate contracts.
- Use `$licca` before writing any command Jörn should run on, to, or from
  LICCA.
- Use `$thesis` when the main consumer is publication-facing prose, proof,
  figure/table design, or integrated thesis review. Investigation evidence
  becoming a thesis asset is a new consumer transition.
- Use `$gpt-56-harness` for durable skill/AGENTS/custom-agent design and
  behavior evaluation. This skill owns empirical outcome measures, not a
  duplicate general harness protocol.
