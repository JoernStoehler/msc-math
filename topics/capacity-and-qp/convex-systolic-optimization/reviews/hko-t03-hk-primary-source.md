# Primary source check for the geometric simple-minimizer premise

Checked 29 October 2026, 00:50 UTC by geometry_frontiers.

Source: Pazit Haim-Kislev, *On the symplectic size of convex polytopes*, arXiv:1712.03494v3 (4 January 2019), Theorem 1.5: https://arxiv.org/html/1712.03494 . Directly read primary HTML at https://arxiv.org/html/1712.03494 , lines 86–92 in tool extraction. Published as GAFA 29(2) (2019), 440–463, DOI 10.1007/s00039-019-00486-4; existing bibliography key HK2017 refers to this article.

The theorem applies to every convex polytope in R^{2n}. It provides an action-minimizing closed characteristic with piecewise constant velocity. Each velocity is a positive multiple of J times one facet normal, and each facet-normal direction is used in at most one segment. Thus a minimizer uses distinct facet labels. No facet-number restriction or genericity hypothesis appears.

This directly supplies the geometric simple-minimizer premise used in the moderate-parameter certificate. The paper uses the opposite J sign to the packet; changing that matrix convention does not change the geometric statement. Facet coactivity and the nonnegative pairing condition follow separately from the support inequalities as explained in COVERAGE-PROOF.md.
