\section{Generalized Reeb Orbits On Polytopes}\label{sec:generalized-reeb-orbits-polytopes}

The boundary of a polytope is not smooth, so the smooth Reeb vector field is
replaced by a differential inclusion.  The inclusion still has contact
normalization, but it allows convex combinations of several facet directions
at a nonsmooth face.  The purpose of this section is to show why the capacity
problem nevertheless contains one minimizer described by finitely many pure
facet directions.  It first separates the finite velocity data from the
additional base-point condition that places the path on the prescribed
facets, and then proves the simple-minimizer theorem that supplies the input to
the Haim--Kislev quadratic program.
