## Maintenance implication and remaining scope

This audit observed two kinds of drift: broken paths after source migration,
and valid paths pointing to a superseded proof route. A path check catches the
first but cannot establish which proof a selected manuscript uses. When a proof
route changes, its topic README and selective findings index need comparison
with the selected source, not just path replacement. This is a lesson from the
observed defects, not evidence that a new workflow has been validated.

The audit did not crawl historical trees or all experiment runbooks. Imported
manifests, old labels and exact historical strings remain evidence of their
original runs; their presence is not itself a current-document defect.
Final integrated PDF quality, claim coverage and human acceptance remain with
the coordinator and their source owners.
