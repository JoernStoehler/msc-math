# Fresh reader questions (verbatim)

1. What precisely is the acceptance condition for the random samples, and could it itself induce the observed relationship between \(R\) and \(\widehat{\mathrm{sys}}\)?

2. Does the strong negative rank correlation persist within the generic-polytope group and individual polygon-product buckets, or could it mainly reflect differences between groups in the pooled table?

3. How was \(\widehat{\mathrm{sys}}\) computed, and what uncertainty follows from the missing evaluator receipts? Can we trust its ordering, its absolute values, or neither without further checks?

4. In the pentagon example, which capacity and normalization define \(\mathrm{sys}\), and what geometric or variational argument produces the stated capacity profile independently of the edge-pair calculation?

5. What does “the random table did not sample that exact structured family successfully” mean: was the family excluded by construction, merely unlikely under continuous sampling, or approached without recording its ratios above one?

6. For the designed \(3\times6\) and \(4\times4\) paths, are the intermediate ratios rigorously established or numerical estimates? What supports the conclusion that these paths invalidate a universal instruction to minimize \(R\)?

7. What descriptors did the selectors actually use, how were controls chosen, and what was the predeclared enrichment criterion? Do these results isolate the usefulness of \(R\), or establish only that a combined selection procedure concentrates high recorded targets?

8. What changed between the original source and the transfer source, and how were the 91 evaluated bodies distributed between selected candidates and controls? What conclusion about transfer is warranted by that comparison?
