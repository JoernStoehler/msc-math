## What was and was not timed

The baseline is already a curated bounded archive: another investigator identified the relevant root/child families, removed private reasoning/credentials, preserved original visible messages, and extracted exact receipts and code. Its inventory materially reduces search compared with an unselected raw-log corpus. This experiment measures consuming those bounded sources with or without an additional packet; it does not measure the cost of constructing either resource, finding the relevant roots in the first place, or reconstructing the whole history from raw logs.

Preparation/curation and parent coordination were not instrumented as a clean incremental cost and are not assigned a fabricated token or human-time total. Native consumer/scorer usage is retained separately. Cumulative cached input counts repeated history; it is not unique material read, billed dollars, attributable quota percentage, or realized future savings. The experiment's own overhead is not a proxy for future retrieval cost.

Four authored tasks and one consumer per condition/task do not establish significance, general reliability, causal mechanism repair, or future human-time savings. No further experiment is included in this authorization.
