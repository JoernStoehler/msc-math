## Fair source access

The source owner is assembling full visible relevant exchanges, exact selected tool/runtime receipts and a neutral inventory. The source boundary is task-relevant C/R/CS, Q, or R/repair families, not the entire private corpus. Interpretive labels and private reasoning/credentials are excluded. Source line IDs, timestamps, roles, hashes and omission markers remain. The existing tiny public quotation selection alone is insufficient as an uncurated baseline; it already embodies substantial investigative work.

Archive access is identical within each pair. Treatment's packet is an extra available route, not free reading: its delivered words count toward the same cap. It may use the archive directly. Current installed code is excluded unless identity with frozen code is established, or explicitly segregated as a later observation that cannot support the historical claim. Full private conversations stay outside public repository deliverables.

An instrumented reader will permit only index, search and bounded line reads of the assigned frozen sources. It will record exact delivered output, IDs, cumulative words and time, and stop delivering sources at the cap. Queries and repeated reads count. A small per-read bound and adequate outer/nested output budgets will prevent silent output truncation; native model-facing outputs will be verified against the reader receipts. Initial task/guidance words are recorded separately from source-access cost. The full native input contract remains bounded by observable messages/receipts, not presumed hidden context.

## Frozen outcomes and interpretation

Source-grounded scoring will distinguish critical fact recovery, source/citation fidelity, claim strength, correct dispositions/authorization, useful next action and material omissions. Criteria will specify acceptable alternatives and facts that genuinely remain unknown. The report is not the truth oracle: a consumer who finds and supports a packet error must receive credit. Material packet facts unavailable in the baseline archive must be supplied before launch or flagged as unequal information.

Primary comparison: source words delivered, read/search calls and wall time **conditional on an adequate, supported answer**. Also report unsupported/contradicted claims, missing necessary historical facts, and avoidable requests to Jörn for facts already documented. A cheaper wrong answer is not a successful saving. Report both accuracy and cost if neither condition dominates. Time/word-cap censoring, unavailable source evidence and tool failure are distinct from reasoning errors.

Two blinded scorers will receive anonymized answers in independently randomized order with the frozen criteria and sufficient canonical source exhibits to verify them. They will not receive condition labels, read traces or the narrative packet. Their own reading/scoring overhead is separate from consumer cost. Native tokens will separate cached input, uncached input and output; no billed spend or quota will be inferred. Current native model/configuration is recorded, not treated as a reproduction or comparison of historical Sol.

All eight returns and both scorer judgments, including unfavorable results and delivery repairs, will be retained. Mechanical unchanged-input delivery repairs using the same agents are preauthorized but must be recorded; cases, criteria and treatment bytes cannot be retrofitted. The experiment's authored tasks and tiny sample do not support significance, general reliability, live behavior improvement or realized future monetary savings.
