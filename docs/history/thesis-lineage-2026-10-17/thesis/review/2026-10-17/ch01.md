## Finding

**C01-1 — low severity, mathematical hypothesis; unambiguous clarification.**
`thesis/chapters/01-introduction.tex:81`, baseline p. 7: the capacity identity
`c_EHZ(L × L°) = 4` is stated for a “centrally symmetric convex body” without
specifying that the origin is its centre. The polar is taken about the origin;
merely assuming that the origin is interior does not suffice. The cited source
explicitly defines its symmetric class as bodies symmetric **with respect to
the origin** in §1.4. For example, in dimension one the off-centred interval
`L = [-1,2]` is centrally symmetric, but its origin-polar is `[-1,1/2]`; the
product rectangle has area, and hence planar EHZ capacity, `9/2`, not `4`.
If “centrally symmetric” is intended to mean origin-symmetric, the current
statement is conventional shorthand rather than a false intended theorem.
Either way the exact edit removes the ambiguity: replace “For a centrally
symmetric convex body” by “For a convex body centrally symmetric about the
origin”. No human decision or new experiment is needed. Source checked:
[Artstein–Avidan–Karasev–Ostrover, §1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/1303.4197v2).
