## Concrete one-run authority topology

- **Root:** receives user steering, immediately propagates stop/scope changes; owns exceptions to approved outcome/resources. Does not own routine patch acceptance, builds, dashboard reconciliation, commits, or final repository assembly. A status question does not silently replace the outcome; an explicit stop does.
- **Operational lead:** sole owner of candidate assembly and coordination state; routes held edits to bounded domain acceptance; accepts/rejects or returns patches; resolves ordinary file overlaps; establishes the exact candidate snapshot; owns build, PDF inspection, evidence/path closure, relevant checks, commit/cleanup and delivery manifest. Can delegate these outputs with named acceptance owners and accept their evidence without routing every item through root. Can pause arrivals or drop lower-value additions when completion is endangered. Existing uncommitted work is preserved, not blindly accepted or reverted.
- **Domain workers/reviewers:** deliver a bounded accepted unit to the operational lead: changed paths, claim/evidence impact, relevant validation, residual concern and exact candidate identity. Adjacent repairs within that outcome can proceed without permission round trips; material scope expansion changes estimated review work and needs lead disposition.
- **Supervisor/strategy reviewer:** independently observes selected obligations, incoming acceptance work, cleared work, oldest consequential pending item, estimated remaining review/assembly effort and current completion path. Can directly hold production, route a review to another available qualified owner, and request narrower scope within authorization. The operational lead must disposition a challenge; only true scope/resource conflicts escalate to root. Verify actual message/control reachability once during assignment; do not assume a child can mechanically override its parent. If root ignores a hold and independently dispatches, the intervention has failed, even if the status report looks good.

Supervisor observations need not become another large permanent dashboard. Existing coordination is the durable owner. Track workload in units that reflect service effort and dependencies, not just message or agent counts: one subtle proof review can exceed ten navigation corrections. Compare arrivals and completed acceptance over successive observations and project the unfinished work against time to deadline. A burst need not imply steady overload; sustained growing consequential backlog or an impossible completion path does require intervention. No prescribed idle fraction or fixed worker ceiling follows.

Strategy comparison occurs concurrently with robust work after authorization: held-patch acceptance, an exact baseline build and resolving known claim/evidence defects can proceed while the independent reviewer compares where marginal time most improves this PDF/repo. Reviewer must compare correctness investigation, reader-facing repair and reproduction closure, including their assembly costs; new speculative production waits for that comparison where its value depends on it. This preserves useful work without treating the incumbent chapter allocation as already justified.
