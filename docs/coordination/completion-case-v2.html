The earlier completion case was insufficient. Agent checks of the core, abstract and appendices established specific observations, not that those sections would pass a reader’s exposition standard. Limiting serious writing work to DS/AI had no adequate basis. Nor may a final verdict be used as another diagnostic checkpoint followed by a repair loop. Diagnostic review and feedback can repeat; final submission, review and grading happen once.

The corrected construction is: assess exposition across the entire thesis; establish with representative diagnostic comparisons whether the available writing process produces the needed improvement; repair every affected chapter and cross-chapter dependency; integrate and diagnose the complete candidate; finish corrections; then offer one final frozen submission for one-time review and grading. We cannot yet demonstrate that this work fits the remaining time and quota. The missing evidence is the size of the exposition deficit and the rate at which it can be resolved to the reader’s standard.

3 · Coordination pattern and useful concurrency

Selected pattern: owners of complete outcomes with their own support teams. Root owns project scope, the evolving completion forecast, resource decisions and human interaction. The writing lead owns complete DS/AI candidates; the whole exposition lead owns both assessment and resulting remediation across the rest of the thesis; the integrator owns one consistent source/PDF artifact. Owners exchange artifacts and dependency changes directly. Root does not combine paragraph fragments from every child.

A root-centered fragment pool keeps too much synthesis and scope reasoning in one lane. A flat pool of ten authors increases coverage but risks duplicate edits and missing cross-chapter ownership. The selected hierarchy addresses those costs, but cannot compensate for an inadequate writing method or a mistaken scope model. The v1 plan failed on scope even though it proposed hierarchy.

Useful substantive concurrency may include several independent chapter authors, their readers, DS/AI ownership, scientific resolution of a concrete gap, summary/argument reconciliation and integration. Luna can assess coverage and gather anchors cheaply; Astra/Sol handle demanding composition and judgment. Approximately ten substantive lanes may be useful when the assessment identifies enough independent remediation. They are not a fixed capacity target, and fifty agent-hours cannot be presumed affordable or productive. Readers and integrators activate as their actual inputs become available.

Progress is an improved artifact or established property. Attempts, calls and threads are activities, with many-to-many relations to outputs and shared scientific inputs. Record their resource observations without allocating all shared cost to one paragraph or summing concurrent durations into project elapsed time.
