## Questions And Selection Rationale

The exploration asked which bounded work could most improve either the hostile
search for `sys > 1` or mathematical understanding of `sys`, without treating
the recovery agent's earlier closure as authority. Work was selected by its
ability to eliminate or reroute a research family cheaply:

- quotient and broaden the old ridge/combinatorial interpretation;
- validate one pre-target pool-screening rule on fresh generated candidates;
- inspect matched/residual examples for omitted geometric or action structure;
- mine existing regular-product and transition owners before new computation;
- test the corrected product action observation across the full retained table;
- reconcile the project-required random/gradient method with tracked evidence;
- park direct adaptive search when its remaining marginal value lost to cheaper
  existing-evidence work.

No new dataset was collected merely because a producer or method existed.

## Supported Empirical Claims

1. **Named negative benchmark.** The reviewed retained random/product table and
   generated scalar packets contain no newly discovered trusted `sys > 1` row.
   This is a result for the named generators and budgets, not an impossibility
   or calibrated rarity statement. Sources: the direct scan and closure owners
   indexed in `../methods/README.md`.

2. **Ridge-family enrichment.** Low normalized symplectic two-face-area
   magnitude enriches the observed sub-threshold `sys` tail within product
   buckets. Within a fixed ridge-count bucket, sum and mean have identical ranks
   by the algebraic identity `sum = ridge_count * mean`; their agreement is not
   independent replication. Max, dispersion, upper-tail, and concentration
   summaries are distinct measured quantities. Sources:
   `../methods/statistical-associations/` and
   `../methods/extreme-scalar-rejection-proposer/`.

3. **Validated pool-screening proposer.** On a fresh 100k random-product pool,
   the frozen cascade first selected the lowest 1% normalized ridge sums within
   each bucket and then the lower max-share half. The second stage improved mean
   `sys` relative to its stage-one complement in the predeclared number of
   buckets. This validates that exact rule for sub-threshold pool screening
   under that source. It is not a validated route to `sys > 1`, a candidate
   generator independent of the random source, or mechanism evidence. Source:
   `../methods/extreme-scalar-rejection-proposer/artifacts/100k-ridge-concentration-validation/`.

4. **Product best-sigma bounce-label association.** Across all 10,240 retained
   random products, rows whose producer-recorded `best_sigma()` has three
   bounces are shifted toward higher `sys` than two-bounce-labelled rows in
   every exact `(k,m)` bucket and
   in both lower- and upper-tail comparisons. A minimal ridge-summary adjustment
   leaves a large descriptive association. `product_bounces` is derived from
   the same capacity optimization as `sys`; this is neither a pre-target
   predictor nor causal evidence. At exact ties it records whichever minimizing
   sigma the producer returns, not all minimizing bounce classes. Source:
   `../methods/product-bounce-distribution/`.

5. **Bounded random-start gradient method.** On twelve deterministic random
   general `F=10` starts, the current observed-multi-direction candidate accepted
   all eight configured finite steps for every seed, with positive start-to-end
   change and no operational failure. All runs stopped at the iteration cap;
   every finite endpoint scan still found an above-threshold move. This supports
   systematic finite-step ascent progress and a measured cost profile on the
   named panel. It does not support a heuristic endpoint, local maximum, or
   threshold-search claim. Source:
   `../../sys-landscape/gradient-ascent-observed-general/`.

6. **Bounded negative scouts.** The compact incidence-area panel did not give a
   one-direction discriminator across its four frozen pairs. The existing
   first-boundary atlas contains several event types and explicit exceptions,
   with no single event explanation. Sources:
   `../methods/residual-exemplar-seeds/` and its indexed transition owner.

## Corrected Interpretations

- The four exact cyclic classes seen in two lower-`sys` product examples were
  not four capacity-value branches. Two independently ordered same-type pair
  blocks force a `2^2` enumeration overcount; same-type commutation preserves
  the HK quadratic program. The surviving observation was two-bounce versus
  three-bounce winner type, now tested across the retained distribution.
- The planar Minkowski-billiard literature restricts minimizers to the
  two-/three-bounce families under the named hypotheses; see the sources cited in
  `../../../papers/hko2024/counterexample.tex` and
  `../../../papers/citation-index.md`. No reviewed source forces the observed
  all-bucket `sys` association. A plausible description is that a short
  two-bounce witness often supplies a capacity shortcut. HKO has tied
  two-/three-bounce minima, so neither three-bounce necessity nor a universal
  two-bounce `sys <= 1` claim is allowed.
- Existing 100k/1m generated artifacts do not sample broad nested ridge bands.
  They support enrichment and an incremental concentration effect, not a
  monotone saturation or Goodhart curve.
- Existing regular-product artifacts show the exact smooth pentagon profile,
  known midpoint symmetry, sparse wider sweeps, and no unexplained competitive
  phase that justifies another broad producer.

## Failed, Parked, And Reopen Conditions

- **Compact incidence-area statistics:** stopped after the frozen negative
  panel; reopen only for an independently motivated mathematical definition.
- **Ridge selection-pressure curve:** parked. Reopen only if distinguishing
  sustained ordering, saturation, reversal, or generator mixture would change
  whether scalar-proposer work receives resources.
- **Regular-product phase mining:** closed at current scope. Reopen for a named
  general `(n,m)` theorem or branch-feasibility question, not another sweep.
- **Class-specific bounce minima `(A_2,A_3)` and gap:** scientifically plausible
  but optional. Reopen if the high normalized intersection of the two lower
  envelopes becomes a selected HKO/thesis question or a cheap pre-target width
  rule is proposed.
- **Equal-budget IID/local/CEM product search (S0):** parked target-free. Its
  implementation cost and verification burden exceeded its current marginal
  information value; no real target run occurred. Reopen only after a new
  portfolio comparison names an outcome that changes a retained decision.
- **Alternative generator and broader invariant families:** technically viable,
  not forbidden. Reopen for a named transfer, counterexample, or geometric
  uniqueness question whose possible outcomes outrank current integration work.
- **Blind same-generator scale, more ordinary models on the same columns,
  feature zoos, deep generation, generic RL/MCMC, and LICCA without a local
  blocker:** rejected for current exploration by value/cost comparison.

## Prohibited Stronger Claims

Current evidence does not establish:

- absence or negligible probability of `sys > 1` outside named samples;
- a calibrated threshold hit rate;
- ridge geometry as a mechanism or a unique natural invariant family;
- a ridge Goodhart/saturation law;
- producer-recorded bounce label as a proposer, cause, complete tie
  classification, or generator-independent law;
- completeness of HK branch or transition types;
- gradient endpoints, local maxima, or absence of nearby improvements;
- theorem evidence from any empirical packet.

whether class-specific bounce competition deserves future mathematical work,
whether a meaningful alternative generator would reverse current feature
relations, and whether a longer/different gradient policy reaches useful
heuristic endpoints. None currently has a cheap unrun packet likely to change
the retained claims or the decision to move into later integration and
demonstration. Separately, FACTSHEET 34.1 requires more data before thesis-level
data-science closure; the later session must choose its datasets and budgets for
the actual thesis consumer rather than infer them from these parked research
