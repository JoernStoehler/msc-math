# Thesis

Route work to the one-purpose references needed for the live decision. Do not
load the package as a process checklist or assume its references exhaust the
ways thesis work can fail. Project facts, chapter state, source truth, and
current completion state remain in their authoritative local sources.

## Artifact, Readers, And Surfaces

- Read `references/publication-target.md` when scope, final artifact, or the
  current publication gate affects the task.
- Read `references/model-thesis-readers.md` when choosing or reviewing
  explanation, assumptions, notation, emphasis, or presentation.
- Read `references/choose-working-surface.md` when deciding where prose,
  planning state, mathematical development, evidence, or scratch work belongs.
- Read `references/maintain-chapter-companions.md` when creating, updating,
  reviewing, or retiring a chapter companion.
- Read `references/organize-thesis-source.md` when splitting, joining, moving,
  or naming active thesis source.
- Read `references/import-legacy-material.md` when consulting legacy prose,
  recovered branches, or obsolete thesis notes.

## Claims And Mathematics

- Read `references/mathematical-status.md` for every task that writes, changes,
  reviews, or relies on mathematical claims, proofs, or algorithm guarantees.
- Also read `references/work-in-formal.md` when the editing or review surface
  is under `formal/`.
- Read `references/explain-computation-backed-claims.md` when a claim depends
  on generated witnesses, exact verification, or numerical checks.
- Read `references/propagate-claims-across-thesis.md` when adapting claims into
  the abstract, introduction, conclusion, or another summary surface.
- Read `references/trace-thesis-content.md` when substantiating candidate
  prose, preparing authoring coverage, checking source-to-thesis omissions, or
  reconstructing disputed provenance.

Use `$paper-conventions` for source papers, `$empirical-research` when
work interprets or changes empirical evidence, and
`$codex-session-log-parsing` only for focused provenance reconstruction that
cannot be resolved from current owners and Git history.

## Explanatory Planning And Prose

- Read `references/choose-chapter-purpose.md` when choosing a chapter or
  passage's role, boundary, or explanatory placement.
- Read `references/prepare-writer-input.md` when gathering material for a
  later writer.
- Read `references/draft-thesis-prose.md` before producing a substantial
  publication-facing passage.
- Read `references/explain-proofs.md` when deciding how to present a proof.
- Read `references/revise-existing-prose.md` before substantially revising,
  compressing, reorganizing, or replacing an existing draft.
- Read `references/review-reader-understanding.md` when testing what a
  candidate makes its reader understand or infer.
- Read `references/review-thesis-work.md` for a broader judgment-based thesis
  review.
- Read `references/review-rendered-thesis.md` when the final PDF or integrated
  page context can affect the decision.
- Read `references/plan-from-readiness.md` when assessing readiness or planning
  backward from a costly review or submission gate.

Reader questions, explanatory purpose, and prose determine one another.
Revisit them when a draft or review exposes a missing question, false
prerequisite, or better order. Existing prose and companions are fallible
inputs: neither preservation nor replacement follows from active status,
length, prior review, or writing cost.

## Figures And Other Assets

- Read `references/choose-presentation-medium.md` when deciding whether prose,
  a figure, a table, a listing, or another display best serves the reader.
- Read `references/manage-publication-assets.md` for generated, copied, or
  regenerated publication assets.
- Read `references/design-tables-listings-displays.md` for non-figure assets
  requiring design judgment.
- Read `references/model-figure-reader-effects.md` for every nontrivial figure.
- Read `references/design-geometry-process-figures.md` for mathematical
  diagrams, projected geometry, local-coordinate panels, or algorithm
  sequences.
- Read `references/design-quantitative-plots.md` for empirical or numerical
  plots.
- Read `references/develop-integrate-figures.md` when implementing or
  materially redesigning a figure.
- Read `references/review-figures.md` before accepting or assigning review of a
  new or materially changed figure.

## Agent Workflow And Evaluation

- Read `references/coordinate-writing-agents.md` before distributing
  substantial thesis preparation, drafting, or review work.
- Read `references/delegate-chapter-ownership.md` when a new autonomous
  session may own a whole chapter or thesis slice through preparation,
  production, review, and return.
- Read `references/allocate-model-effort.md` when choosing model or reasoning
  effort for thesis-writing work.
- Read `references/learn-from-writing-trials.md` when a writing trial may
  change later routing.

Use `$gpt-56-harness` for evaluation integrity and reusable agent-facing
instructions. Preserve writing-specific outcome measures here, not a duplicate
evaluation protocol.
