## What value is purchased, and what already exists

The purchase is scientific readiness for downstream authoring: complete arguments
at selected strength, coherent mechanisms/relationships, empirical designs and
outcomes with truthful provenance, actual implementation/arithmetic and replay
boundaries, usable figures, and independent assembled/use checks. Human prose
quality, eventual PDF PASS, new theorems and quantified writing-time savings are
not included claims.

The selected chapters, included fragments, DS appendix and two selected-product
account inputs contain approximately **39,940 whitespace words**. This is a
LaTeX/source workload observation, not human reading time, unique mathematical
content or inference cost. Substantive foundation, QP, flow, variation, HKO/P*,
product, empirical, trust and AI accounts already exist. Discovery identified
no missing necessary theorem implication requiring a new campaign; this is a
bounded inspection, not certification of every proof.

An assembly route uses these full original proof/science units at frozen
identities. Agents may read LaTeX originals directly; there is no requirement
to convert every proof into fresh Markdown. New account text must still explain
purpose, implications and relationships: an index of links cannot fulfill the
contract. Original domain owners remain authoritative; active manuscript inputs
are preserved.

Concrete differences the finished object must make:

- **HKO and search become distinguishable at the point of use.** The account
  exposes the full finite-predicate → positive feasible upper sections → uniform
  transverse deficit → common differentiability remainder → symmetry/polar
  chart/Hausdorff neighborhood chain. It makes clear that the proof does not
  require continuing every nearby optimizing capacity branch. The proof already
  exists; the added value is checked assembly with the variation, numerical and
  optimizer interfaces, not discovering that implication anew.
- **Empirical population and evaluation rules cannot be silently interchanged.**
  The initial scalar-selection pool has 100,000 generated bodies but 1,675
  distinct evaluations across overlapping selection rules. Fresh validations,
  the low-ridge tail, transfer, balancing and local optimizers are separate
  designs. The account explains enrichment versus improving a particular body,
  numerical ratios versus certified capacity, and historical versus current
  evaluator guarantees. These distinctions already have source support and
  must remain together through downstream authoring.
- **The selected product results acquire one precise scope comparison.** Rotation,
  independent affine factors, product-position deformation and arbitrary
  symmetric partners have different classes and proof mechanisms. The selected
  symmetric-partner theorem, absent from the manuscript, is included in the
  account; its numerical interface uses the minimum of ten maximal fitting scales, each with its own
  translation. The proof figure describes normalized containment,
  not an arbitrary numerical fit. Newer accepted F11/fixed-partner research is
  explicitly outside selected manuscript scope and is not silently promoted.
- **A reproduction promise is tied to a particular route.** Each relevant claim
  states input, implementation/producer, arithmetic boundary, environment,
  command and side effects, comparison, and actual assessed status. Retained
  traces, fresh generation, inherited successful checks and unestablished
  recreation remain separate. No figure replay is presented as reproduction
  of every historical capacity evaluation.

These are accountable deliverable differences. Their downstream time saving is
unknown. The proposed use checks determine whether scientific clarification
is still missing from actual authoring; they do not magically measure universal
productivity or human writing acceptance.

## Evidence for this choice, including evidence against it

1. The 8 October selected symmetric-partner pilot had an original packet,
   supplement and fuller rewrite. All three initial consumers recovered the
   theorem and rejected the same removal of symmetry. It demonstrated **no
   mathematical recovery advantage from rewriting the complete proof**.
   Source review exposed a real numerical-interface gap; revision 2 explained
   the ten independent maximal fits and other interfaces, and a targeted repeat
   consumer recovered those additions. No human text was newly authored and no
   authoring/supervision saving was measured. This favors targeted assembly
   over routine reexposition; it is one unusually well-developed case, not a
   whole-project performance test.
2. Current proof and empirical source inspection shows substantial reusable
   material and identifies concrete high-risk interfaces/design distinctions.
   This lowers dependence on new research, but does not prove reliable autonomous
   integration. The selected manuscript files matched main at launch; main has
   newer unselected research requiring status reconciliation rather than broad
   import or new proof work.
3. A separate four-task archival-consumption study found no demonstrated net
   reconstruction-cost saving: adding a report increased source reading 7.3%,
   added 20 reads and was slower in all four pairs. Both baselines were already
   curated; one pair had unequal information and there was one consumer per
   condition. It concerns a different packet/task. It is adverse evidence about
   making an extra account a mandatory duplicate prerequisite, not proof that
   this scientific account has zero value. The design therefore offers task-specific
   entry points and replaces scattered interface work rather than requiring
   every writer to read everything twice.
4. This session has not delivered the requested account and has had communication
   and review failures. That is relevant adverse evidence about the coordinator,
   not something erased by a reviewed plan. The proposed ownership, observable
   scientific checkpoints and limits address specific failure opportunities;
   their effectiveness in a sustained run remains unproved.

No numerical success probability or return-on-investment multiplier is warranted.
The defensible claim is narrower: a bounded assembly attempt can purchase useful,
checkable scientific units from existing supported material; it might still fail
to complete or save downstream work.
