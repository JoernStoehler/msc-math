# Ridge Mechanism Discriminator

Date: 2026-07-04

Status: compact promoted snapshot of a local mechanism-discriminator packet.
The tracked artifacts under `artifacts/current/` are the source of truth for
the current interpretation and are usable without any live `/tmp` state.
Regenerating those artifacts requires the retained/generated input artifacts
and local full feature cache described below.

Question: among existing retained-table diagnostics and generated
`promising-scalars` candidates, which ridge-area explanation is best supported?
The compared hypotheses are low ridge magnitude signal, ridge-area
concentration/distribution signal, product/combinatorial proxy,
small-area-fraction story, and extreme-tail Goodharting.

This is a data-analysis packet. It does not validate a new generated-candidate
proposer. A generated-candidate proposer claim is allowed only for rules frozen
before independent `sys` evaluation. The concentration split in this packet is
post-`sys` diagnostic-only. No HKO-distance or flank claims are used.

## Result Interpretation

Low ridge magnitude survives as a bucket-local empirical signal, with a controls
caveat. Retained fixed source/facet buckets show strong low-sum/mean
enrichment. Generated 1M and 100k per-bucket low ridge rules improve selected
mean `sys` over matched baselines. In the 100k packet, per-bucket low ridge
magnitude rules improve mean `sys` by about `0.248` to `0.332` over matched
baselines, depending on the scalar.

Product/combinatorial proxy remains a major confounder, but a pure proxy story
is too strong. Pooled retained ridge residuals mostly collapse after
source/facet/product and combinatorial controls. The 100k count-only rules
collapse, and the scalar-feature overlap matrix shows count rules select the
same 100 rows. However, generated per-bucket low-ridge rules still enrich
inside exact product buckets.

Concentration/distribution is plausible only as a diagnostic seed. Among the
387 low-ridge selected 100k rows, high normalized entropy and low max/top-3
share split mean `sys` upward by about `0.047` to `0.051` overall. This was
mined after `sys` evaluation, so it is not a proposer claim.

The small-area-fraction story weakens. Conditional low-ridge splits on
small-area fractions have near-zero or negative mean-`sys` deltas overall, and
many per-bucket median splits do not separate rows because the fractions are
discrete.

Extreme-tail Goodharting survives as a caution. The low-ridge generated tails
are enriched, but selected maxima remain below `sys = 1`: the 1M per-bucket
low-sum summary tops out near `0.866920080910149`, and the 100k
`promising-scalars` union tops out at `0.867546058507634`.

## Limitations

- Retained-table rows are diagnostics only because every row already has `sys`.
- The concentration split is post-`sys` and uses the local feature cache; it is
  useful for choosing a future frozen rule, not for claiming proposer validity.
- The 100k compact tracked packet omits full feature/geometry caches. This run
  could do row-level concentration analysis only because the local feature
  cache was still present.
- Regeneration requires `/tmp/sys-ds-tail-invariant-analysis/` or equivalent
  tail-rule artifacts plus the full 100k feature cache. The compact outputs are
  tracked so future readers do not need those large/local inputs for ordinary
  interpretation.
- Classifications are empirical packet labels, not theorem statements.

## Next Packet Recommendation

Do not run another larger single-scalar low-ridge scale-up by default. If this
line is reopened, freeze exactly one concentration add-on rule before seeing an
independent generated run, probably one of:

- low ridge magnitude within bucket plus high normalized entropy;
- low ridge magnitude within bucket plus low max share or low top-3 share.

The independent validation packet must preserve selection-before-`sys`, run a
target-field audit, and compare against the scalar-only per-bucket low-ridge
boundary from the 100k packet.
