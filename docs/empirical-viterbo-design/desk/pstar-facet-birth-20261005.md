every fixed direction exposing a face of codimension at least two gives strict
ratio loss for all sufficiently shallow cuts. Facet-parallel shallow cuts have

below the original capacity one**. This is the cuts-extremizer property:
strict shallow capacity loss transfers to deeper parallel cuts by monotonicity.
It is not an all-depth strict ratio theorem.
