# Mathematical Status

Use this reference whenever work writes, changes, reviews, or relies on a
mathematical statement, proof, proof sketch, algorithm guarantee, or comment
claiming correspondence with mathematics.

State hypotheses, quantifiers, edge cases, and guarantees precisely enough to
expose wrong claims. Distinguish a proof idea from a complete argument, a
trusted main case from unchecked cases, notation trouble from a mathematical
gap, and an agent review from Jörn's acceptance.

Agent-written mathematics may be expert work, but writing, reviewing, or
committing it does not make it Jörn-reviewed. Record status where a later
consumer could otherwise overstate the result.

For theorem-critical new arguments, use independent review as additional
evidence rather than authority. When testing whether the argument stands on
its own, give the reviewer the statement and necessary sources without
coaching it toward the intended derivation; verify reported gaps and repairs.

Preserve the current mathematical reason for definitions, statements, and
proof methods. Leave irrelevant attempt history in Git. Use grep-able LaTeX
labels and references rather than hardcoded theorem numbers.
