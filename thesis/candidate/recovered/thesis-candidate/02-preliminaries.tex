\section{Preliminaries}\label{sec:preliminaries}

A polytope has edges and vertices where the smooth definition of a
characteristic no longer applies. We first extend that definition to convex
bodies with nonsmooth boundaries. We then introduce a variational description
of capacity in terms of closed curves that need not lie on the boundary.
This will allow us to modify curves when deriving the finite capacity formula.

All convex bodies below are compact convex subsets of \(\mathbb{R}^4\) with
non-empty interior. Unless stated otherwise, the origin lies in the interior.
