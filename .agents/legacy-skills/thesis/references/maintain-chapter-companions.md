# Maintain Chapter Companions

Use this reference when creating, updating, reviewing, or retiring a
chapter-local `*-content.md` companion.

Preserve the chapter's current writing state that future work cannot recover
cheaply: purpose and downstream use, source routes, accepted interpretations,
claim limitations, useful old prose, unresolved contradictions, fallback
branches, concrete review findings, and reopen conditions. Keep actual
mathematical, empirical, and external facts with their owning sources.

Give the companion enough local status and source hierarchy to be understood
without a second process document. Duplication of a short local boundary is
acceptable when it prevents context-dependent misuse. Do not impose a fixed
schema: use prose, lists, tables, or mappings according to the chapter's live
decisions.

Prefer exact source paths and identifiers plus a short explanation of
relevance over copying long source summaries. Mark an unsupported item as
`needs source` rather than allowing it to look accepted.

Update the companion when a source, accepted decision, support boundary,
review finding, or chapter role changes. Remove superseded planning and
generic process reminders once they no longer help the owner. Retire the
companion only when its durable state has moved into accepted prose, owning
sources, or another explicit owner.
