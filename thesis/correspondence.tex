\subsection{Notes}\label{subsec:notes}

\begin{description}

\item[Two normalizations.]
  We call ours \emph{period normalization} and theirs \emph{HK2017 normalization}.

\item[Centering constraint.]
  We include a centering constraint on the dual variable; HK2017 does not.

\item[MATLAB sign convention.]
  MATLAB minimizes a negative quantity; we maximize a positive one.

\end{description}
