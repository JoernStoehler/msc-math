# Import Legacy Material

Use this reference when consulting or importing material from
`thesis/legacy/`, recovered branches, old notes, or obsolete thesis surfaces.

Treat legacy material as mixed source, not as a draft to polish in place.
Extract potentially useful claims, proofs, source pointers, labels,
explanations, caveats, and omissions. Revalidate each retained item against
current owners and conventions before adapting it into active prose or a
companion.

Do not infer current support, scope, wording, or explanatory order from prior
length, review history, or apparent completeness. Use old prose as an omission
and comparison source after choosing the current reader purpose.

When importing labels, citations, or cross-references, run a targeted audit of
their current targets. Keep `thesis/legacy/README.md` as the directory-local
status sentinel; keep concrete historical mismatches with their legacy owner
rather than generalizing them into current policy.
