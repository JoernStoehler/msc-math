# Bounded prior-art check: entire linear orbit of the HKO pentagon product

Checked 29 October 2026 against the proposed statement/reduction in `/tmp/msc-math/restart/geometry-frontiers/affine-hko-compressed/PROOF.md` (mathematical audits are separate). Let `H=P_5×_L R_{-π/2}P_5`. The proposed theorem is `rho(AH)=c_EHZ(AH)^2/(2 Vol_4(AH))≤(3+√5)/5` for every `A∈GL(4,R)`, with equality exactly for positive dilations of linear symplectic images of `H` (plus irrelevant translations). This is a global theorem **on one full linear orbit**, not a theorem for arbitrary nearby ten-facet bodies or all convex four-bodies.

## Project comparison and calibrated conclusion

The maintained [independent affine pentagon theorem](/home/joern/.codex/worktrees/research-restart-20261028/msc-math/topics/products/pentagon-affine-products/README.md) treats `(G P_5)×_L(H P_5)` with **separate `G,H∈GL(2,R)`** and proves the sharp HKO ratio and equality within that product family. The proposed full `GL(4,R)` orbit permits maps that mix the `q` and `p` coordinates and need not remain products in the original splitting. The maintained [HKO feasible-section theorem](/home/joern/.codex/worktrees/research-restart-20261028/msc-math/topics/variation-and-local-maxima/hko-local-maximum/theorem/README.md) establishes a **local** maximum among nearby exactly-ten-facet bodies, modulo symmetries. Its F≤11 extension is also local. Neither project result is a global assertion on every `A H`.

**Assessment:** I did not locate an existing all-`GL4` HKO sharp upper bound or its equality classification in the inspected primary sources or targeted searches. If the proposed proof survives independent audit, its precise contribution is the global upper bound and rigidity **for the entire linear orbit of the HKO product**; the HKO benchmark, symplectic invariance, and the general idea of a full linear-orbit optimization have prior art. Do not call it a proof that `H` maximizes among all products, all ten-facet polytopes, or all convex four-bodies. This is bounded negative evidence, not a priority certificate.
