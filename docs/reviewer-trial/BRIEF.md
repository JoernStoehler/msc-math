## Important corrections from the discussion

- Feedback need not mean a full document review. A sentence-level slop judgment may take~5 seconds; test that rather than assuming every judgment costs minutes. Async questions or short chat reactions are suitable, with minimal necessary context. Human recognition needn't include explanations or repairs.
- Existing general reviews missed audience/relevance/emphasis objections. This does not establish that dedicated specialists fail. Possible distinct operations: reader prerequisite reconstruction, reader-question matching, explanation-budget review, relevance-by-deletion, slop classification, reconstruction of an argument. Don't constrain search to these.
- Reuse a small annotated development text repeatedly; hold back another text/labels for transfer. Freeze predictions before asking Jörn. Challenge-selected weaknesses are useful; include ordinary and agent-endorsed cases to expose shared blindspots. They answer different sampling questions.
- Context matters: Jörn reversed an opening-sentence objection given an appropriate preceding section. Do not force intrinsic context-free labels.
- A hundred cheap specialist passes might be affordable. Their useful marginal findings matter, not agreement or number of criticisms. Aggregation/repair can be tested later; don't use those harder tasks to postpone a scoped detection test.
- Don't spend the hour making infrastructure. End with concrete workflows, frozen outputs, actual judgments, scope of evidence, limitations, and next decision. Preserve disappointing results.
