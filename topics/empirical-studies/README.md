# Empirical studies

These studies ask how recorded systolic ratios vary across sampled polytopes,
which geometric invariants explain or predict the upper tail, and what local
search reveals about the landscape. Their populations and evaluators differ;
a retained finite table, a fitted extrapolation and an optimizer trajectory
support different claims.

- [Geometric discovery](discovery.md) follows the broader research desk through
  ridge circulation, moment counterexamples, complementary representations and
  zonotope partial results, with historical proposals kept separate.
- [Producers and schemas](producers-and-schema.md) follows random/product
  populations through geometry identity, numerical targets, retained snapshots,
  preparation and method consumption. It distinguishes the June historical
  corpus from newly certified-capacity run-local rows.
- [Methods and interpretation](methods.md) explains retained baseline,
  representation, ridge-feature, tail, reference and proposer work, with actual
  input versions and limits on extrapolation.
- [Evidence closure](evidence-closure/README.md) retains the historical
  replay, mechanism/selection synthesis and writer-facing source interpretation.
- [Retrospective revalidation](retrospective-revalidation/README.md) records
  the later geometry/schema restoration and separate current capacity checks:
  14,335 bodies covered by scalars or intervals, one unresolved, with
  uncertified volume and no claimed certified systolic-ratio intervals.
- [Product theorem applicability](family-theorem-mapping/README.md) identifies
  seven random-product buckets excluded from above-one discovery by checked
  triangle/quadrilateral hypotheses, without certifying old numerical targets.
- [Landscape experiments](landscape.md) maps random sweeps, ascent,
  variable-facet search, fixed-shape controls and sampling acceptance to their
  source, retained evidence and evaluator assumptions.

The executable/data packages now live alongside these accounts at
[polytope-datasets](polytope-datasets/README.md),
[polytope-invariant-table](polytope-invariant-table/README.md),
[sys-datascience](sys-datascience/README.md) and
[sys-landscape](sys-landscape/README.md). Cargo package and binary identities
remain unchanged. These topic accounts own synthesis; producers and method
packets own code, exact schemas and retained outputs. Historical receipts keep
the original source paths and hashes; relocated current consumers use the new
paths rather than treating historical provenance as a current command map.

No finite random sample, fitted tail or unsuccessful search proves the
unrestricted conjecture. Historical numerical labels, missing run provenance,
reference reuse and changes to the feature universe must remain visible in
interpretation. This topic adds no research programme and does not promote
claims into the selected thesis.
