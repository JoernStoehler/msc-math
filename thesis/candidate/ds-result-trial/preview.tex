\noindent\textit{Context from the preceding pages.}\par
\noindent The experiments evaluate
$\operatorname{sys}(K)=c_{\rm EHZ}(K)^2/(2\operatorname{vol}_4(K))$
numerically.
For a four-dimensional polytope, $R$ is the sum of its ridges' absolute
symplectic areas divided by $\sqrt{\operatorname{vol}_4(K)}$, and $C$ is
the largest ridge area divided by their sum. The preceding discussion
found an inverse association between $R$ and the systolic ratio.
A $k\times m$ product has polygonal factors with $k$ and $m$ sides.
The preceding comparison gives $R=8$ and $\operatorname{sys}=1/2$ for
the cube, versus $R\approx8.944$ and $\operatorname{sys}>1$ for HKO.
