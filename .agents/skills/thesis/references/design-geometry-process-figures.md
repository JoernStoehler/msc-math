# Geometry And Process Figures

## Projection And Depth

Choose cues according to which spatial relations the reader must recover.
Projected wireframes show edges but can leave crossings, occlusion, face
incidence, and depth ambiguous. When three-dimensional structure matters, use
materially complementary views, visible faces, occlusion, shading, depth
ordering, or another cue that supplies the missing information. A second nearly
identical camera adds little.

Cropping and object occupancy determine how much physical page area encodes the
geometry. Excess empty bounds can make correct detail imperceptible at fixed
thesis width. State projection distortions when they affect a likely reader
inference; do not imply that a topological or projective explanatory view
preserves metric quantities.

## Local Coordinates And Small Multiples

Repeated panels invite comparison even when the producer did not intend it.
State which quantities remain comparable. Shared coordinates and scales can
support position or magnitude comparisons. Independently chosen frames or
autoscaling may expose local detail while invalidating comparisons of position,
orientation, length, area, or apparent change in size.

When interpretation depends on it, state the basis, metric, origin,
orientation, projection, and scaling. For example, Euclidean-orthonormal axes
preserve Euclidean angles and relative lengths within one local plane, but
independently chosen origins and orientations do not make coordinates
compatible between panels.

Give persistent mathematical objects stable visual encodings. Do not change a
color or marker's meaning across panels without an overriding reader benefit
that is made explicit.

## Algorithm Sequences

A process figure should externalize:

- the state in each panel;
- the transition between consecutive states;
- what persists and what changes;
- the output, termination, or closure condition.

Layout, arrows, labels, and prose should reinforce the same traversal order.
Place transition annotations in connective space rather than where they compete
with state labels or mathematical objects. If closure matters, make the final
return visually identifiable with the initial state rather than relying only on
the caption.

## Examples And Captions

Name examples by properties useful to the thesis reader, not by producer
provenance. Seeds, attempt numbers, and fixture names normally belong beside the
producer. Captions should identify the mathematical objects and intended
takeaway, then resolve predictable false inferences created by the display.
