For a complete two-page sample, the level of procedural detail is appropriate. Lack of full reproduction specifications is not by itself a writing defect here. Two small terminology/context gaps remain.

## 4. Anchored issue ledger

- **Quote:** “The new experiments bound the error in capacity” and “In the original samples”. **Reader consequence:** These establish a distinction between experimental generations without clearly identifying which subsequent results belong to each. The reader can infer that the later intervention tests are new, but cannot confidently attach the capacity-error statement to every result reported here. **Severity:** Minor context ambiguity. **Uncertainty:** Moderate; accompanying material might establish the distinction, but this standalone sample does not.
- **Quote:** “Rotating a Lagrangian product can therefore change its capacity”. **Reader consequence:** “Lagrangian product” has not been introduced, although the coordinate construction supplies the relevant example. A reader with ordinary graduate background but little symplectic terminology must infer its connection to the earlier products. The surrounding explanation still makes the experimental purpose clear. **Severity:** Minor terminology gap. **Uncertainty:** Low about the absent introduction; moderate about its practical difficulty for the intended reader.
