# What the non-Lagrangian positioning theorem adds

28 October 2026, 23:36 UTC. Bounded comparison, not a priority claim.

## Exact distinction from earlier project results

Inspected `thesis/chapters/11-product-position.tex`, `topics/products/product-rotation/README.md`, and the full `topics/products/product-position-research/README.md` plus its earlier `lift-persistence.md` account.

The selected chapter proves an initial capacity increase away from symplectically orthogonal factor planes along a fixed rigid-rotation path. Its triangle example has Lagrangian endpoint18/5, symplectic endpoint9/2, and some intermediate positions improve both endpoints. The 18September research note also gives a family with fixed endpoint capacities but differing intermediate profiles. Each comparison keeps the relative affine identification of the two factors fixed. The lift-persistence result separates orbit containment from capacity minimality. None of those statements bounds the full family of independent affine factor changes.

The present theorem has different quantifiers: **every** linear image of Δ×Δ whose two original factor planes are Lagrangian has rho≤2/3, while one explicit image with non-Lagrangian factor planes has rho18/25. Every pair of triangles lies in this same full-affine orbit. The coefficient-table mechanism reuses the earlier project's one-parameter closure/120-order observation. The added mathematical ingredients are the exact global crossblock polar, its rank-one hull description, and a strict witness exceeding the global Lagrangian optimum. Do not claim the closure reduction or nonmonotone rotation phenomenon itself is new.
