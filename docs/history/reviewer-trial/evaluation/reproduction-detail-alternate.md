## Details worth retaining

- **Historical-target qualification:** “Reproducing their correlation does not certify either their absolute accuracy or their ordering by mathematical capacity.” This prevents the reader from treating the recorded association as an established association with mathematical capacity. The distinction matters even for the rank-based conclusion; uncertainty is not confined to the reported numerical magnitudes.

- **Generation versus evaluation counts:** The pool of 100,000 products and the selected/control union of 1,675 evaluated bodies support different inferences. Retaining both prevents the subunit maximum from being read as the result of 100,000 capacity evaluations. The count of 485 distinct selected candidates also makes clear that the evaluated union includes more than the selected candidates alone; the number of selection sets provides context for overlap rather than an independent sample size.

- **Transfer restrictions:** “frozen rho and ridge selectors,” “different, separately factor-area-normalized source,” “disjoint-control means,” the two bucket types, and “91 distinct evaluated bodies” jointly delimit the transfer evidence. They indicate that the selection rules were held fixed, the source changed, the comparison groups did not share bodies, and the observed improvement rests on a finite evaluation. Moving these conditions away would make the concluding claim of useful but bounded candidate prioritization harder to assess.

These judgments concern placement of reproduction details only, not acceptance of the mathematical account as a whole.
