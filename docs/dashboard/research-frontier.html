What controls capacity, and which geometry transfers?

The common quantity is sys(K) = cEHZ(K)² / (2 vol₄(K)). The HKO pentagon product has value (3 + √5)/5 > 1. The useful questions are which methods explain that value, which classes they control, and where their implications stop.

Split product closure into planar triangles

Some HK maximizer for a Lagrangian planar product uses at most three facet directions in each factor, with half the mass in each factor. Bilinearity reduces a capacity value to paired closure triangles and their interleavings.

Established route · imported criterion

Calibrate one body by translation covers

The BMP normal-triangle criterion turns c(K ×L Q) ≥ 1 into finitely many triangle placements when Q is polygonal. Unit out-and-back segments impose the difference-body ceiling WQ(K).

BMP Theorem 3.3 · primary source, not a theorem discovered here.

Still needed for arbitrary asymmetric optimality: a sharp minimum-area simultaneous cover. The symmetric theorem does not supply it.

Established · independently agent-checked

A uniform symmetrization plateau

For a fixed regular pentagon Q and Kt = (1 − t)K + t(−K), the difference body stays fixed. For every partner, capacity equals the width ceiling throughout t ∈ [(3 − √5)/2, (√5 − 1)/2].

Mechanism: carry a normal template's diameter using two points of K; the third vertex has a uniform interior margin in the symmetral.

Checked: all templates, scaling and endpoint algebra; the constructive proof and independent audit. The interval is sharp: an explicit trapezoid has exactly this capacity-saturation interval; the proof records its difference body and forced template placements.

Research: partner_geometry; independent check: plateau_check; integration: root.

All odd regular pair profiles, n,m ≥ 5

The uniform formula is c = (1 + cos(π/n))(1 + cos(π/m)) sec dL(θ), with L = lcm(n,m) and dL the distance to (π/L)ℤ. HKO is the sharp systolic maximum throughout this class, with the stated pentagon equality positions.

Method: translate a circle around one closed step triangle; the interior vertex and a positive partner step force a deficit. A continuous minimum-angle inequality controls all factors with at least seven sides; the pentagon cases use their specific closure geometry.

Which arrows remain conditional?

  • Angular containment → rotation profiles. It does not imply tensor containment for independent arbitrary affine maps.
  • Calibrated symmetric comparison body → all symmetric partners. It does not resolve minimum area for asymmetric covers.
