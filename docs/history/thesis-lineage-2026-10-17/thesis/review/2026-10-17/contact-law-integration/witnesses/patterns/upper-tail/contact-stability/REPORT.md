# A contact defect predicts the nonregular HKO upper tail

The first-order systolic loss at HKO is an explicitly computable, nonsmooth geometric defect. It is the maximum of twenty linear forms, generated by **one shape contrast plus an absolute orthogonality defect**, over five cyclic positions and two factor roles. Its frozen, parameter-free prediction was accurate on a fresh 60-product panel, including 41 exact ratios above one: median relative ratio error **0.00467%**, worst error **0.18043%**, and $R^2=0.99844$ on those 41 cases. All 60 above/below-one classifications were correct.

This is a local product result. The raw score depends on its HKO normal chart; its linearization is invariant on the tangent quotient. The [canonical extension](CANONICAL.md) separately addresses actual product-coordinate invariance. This packet supplies neither a universal invariant for arbitrary four-dimensional convex bodies nor a global maximality proof. The repository already proves HKO local maximality in a broader class; the new content here is the explicit directional loss law and its frozen upper-tail prediction, conditional on the imported capacity formula.

## Definitions and exact local law

Use the retained algebraic HKO dual-normal presentation

$$a_0=(1,0),\quad a_1=((\sqrt5-1)/4,1),\quad
 a_2=(-(\sqrt5+1)/4,(\sqrt5-1)/2),$$
$$a_3=(-(\sqrt5+1)/4,(1-\sqrt5)/2),\quad
 a_4=((\sqrt5-1)/4,-1),\qquad b_i=Ja_i.$$

The product is $P(A)\times_LP(B)$, with $P(A)=\{x:a_i\cdot x\leq1\}$. Write $z=(A,B)\in\mathbb R^{20}$ and $z_0$ for this reference. Its ratio is $\rho_H=(3+\sqrt5)/5$ and maximal normalized HK objective is $Q_0=1/10$. Let $V(z)$ be the product of the two planar areas.

Each factor has five positive three-normal closure vertices near $z_0$. For a three-index support $I=(i,j,k)$, continue its weights by

$$\alpha_I=\frac{(\det(a_j,a_k),\det(a_k,a_i),\det(a_i,a_j))}
 {\det(a_j,a_k)+\det(a_k,a_i)+\det(a_i,a_j)}.$$

For a cyclic word $w$ on a closure pair, let

$$Q_w(z)=\frac14\sum_{i,j}\epsilon_{ij}(w)\alpha_i(z)\gamma_j(z)
 a_i\cdot b_j,\qquad
 \epsilon_{ij}=1\text{ if }A_i\text{ precedes }B_j,\ -1\text{ otherwise}.$$

For every branch active at HKO, form the row vector

$$g_w=\left.\mathrm d\log(8Q_w^2V)\right|_{z_0}
 =\left.\left(2\,\frac{\mathrm dQ_w}{Q_0}+\frac{\mathrm dV}{V_0}\right)\right|_{z_0}.$$

The exact census has fifty active cyclic-word branches but only twenty distinct $g_w$. Their coefficients are in [predictions.json](predictions.json); [fullword.json](fullword.json) independently binds them to all $25\times120=3,000$ cyclic words. Define

$$\boxed{D(\delta)=\max_{w\text{ active}}g_w\cdot\delta.}$$

**Local theorem, relative to the imported HK formula and product six-facet reduction.** There are $r,C>0$ such that every genuine five-by-five product in this labelled chart with $\|\delta\|<r$ satisfies

$$\boxed{\left|\log\frac{\operatorname{sys}(z_0+\delta)}{\rho_H}
 +D(\delta)\right|\leq C\|\delta\|^2.}$$

All twenty forms annihilate the nine-dimensional tangent of translations, common dilation and product-preserving linear symplectic transformations. Their span has rank eleven and their sum is zero. Therefore $D$ descends to a positive, generally asymmetric polyhedral gauge on the eleven-dimensional quotient: $D\geq0$, and $D(\delta)=0$ exactly on the symmetry tangent. It is not asserted to be a norm.

**Proof.** Every pair determinant and every three-row affine determinant at the reference is nonzero, and all nonzero closure weights have strict sign. Thus no two-point closure vertex appears and exactly the same five positive three-point supports persist locally. The imported reduction makes capacity the reciprocal of twice a maximum over their finite cyclic-word branches. Each branch and the area product is analytic in this chamber. The exact census checks that all branch objectives are at most $Q_0$, with largest inactive objective $(-5+3\sqrt5)/20$ and strict gap $(7-3\sqrt5)/20$. Continuity removes inactive branches in some neighborhood. Taylor-expand the remaining fifty analytic branches; the finite number gives one uniform quadratic remainder. Taking their maximum commutes with the common volume differential, producing $D$ and the displayed expansion. Exact elimination verifies rank eleven, a uniform strictly positive relation (the twenty rows sum to zero), and annihilation of nine independent symmetry tangents. If $D=0$, all row evaluations are nonpositive with zero sum, hence all vanish; their common kernel is precisely that tangent. This proves the quotient statement. These arguments provide an existential neighborhood and remainder constant, not a numerical radius. ∎

## A single contrast and a contact-order cusp

Let $s=\sqrt5$, $k=(3-s)/2$, $d=(5+s)/8$, and

$$K_A=\begin{pmatrix}0&-d\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 K_B=-K_A^T.$$

Write each normal perturbation uniquely as

$$\delta a_i=r_i a_i+t_iK_Aa_i,\qquad
 \delta b_i=u_i b_i+v_iK_Bb_i.$$

One generating shape contrast is

$$\begin{aligned}
L={}&\frac{3-s}{5}(r_0+r_1+u_0+u_4)
 -\frac25(r_2+r_4+u_1+u_3)
 +\frac{2(s-1)}5(r_3+u_2)\\
&-k t_1+\frac{s-1}{2}t_3
 -\frac{s-1}{2}v_2+k v_4.
\end{aligned}$$

The two template forms are exactly

$$L\ \pm\ k(t_0-v_0)=L\ \pm\ k\,\mathrm d(a_0\cdot b_0).$$

The derivative of the reference orthogonality $a_0\cdot b_0=0$ splits the two tied contact orderings. Thus the template pair contributes $L+k|t_0-v_0|$. Rotate it through the five simultaneous cyclic positions, and apply the factor-quarter-turn swap $(\delta A,\delta B)\mapsto(-J\delta B,J\delta A)$. These ten paired modes generate all twenty gradients. [symmetry.py](symmetry.py) verifies the exact pullback permutations and two ten-element orbits; [symmetry.json](symmetry.json) retains them. Reflection adds no new forms.

This decomposition explains why a smooth scalar based only on regularity or a low moment can miss the leading loss. For example, $\delta a_0=a_0$, $\delta a_1=-a_1$, all other perturbations zero, has zero cyclic average and

$$D(\delta)=D(-\delta)=\frac{2\sqrt5}{5},\qquad
\operatorname{sys}(z_0+t\delta)=\rho_H\left(1-\frac{2\sqrt5}{5}|t|+O(t^2)\right).$$

This is an explicit nonsmooth directional mechanism, not evidence that every conceivable smooth feature is inadequate. The action-moment lane separately states the needed symmetry assumptions for its smooth-invariant obstruction.

## Global continuation: the missing implication

A positive contact defect establishes coercivity near the HKO orbit after removing its symmetry tangent. Global maximality would require a global mechanism that every product away from that orbit has a capacity upper branch with ratio at most $\rho_H$, and would need to control switches to supports inactive at HKO. The local positive-gradient relation and inactive gap do not provide this implication: their coefficients and active set are tied to the reference. A fixed-chart $D$ can be distorted by a finite symplectic coordinate change even when true ratio is unchanged. Canonicalization addresses that coordinate defect; it does not supply the missing global branch inequality. Six-facet births and nonproduct perturbations also lie outside this twenty-coordinate theorem.
