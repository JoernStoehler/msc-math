## The mathematical picture, in plain terms

The normalized quantity is `sys(K)=c_EHZ(K)^2/(2 vol₄(K))`. The original
four-dimensional Viterbo bound `sys≤1` is already false: the HKO pentagon product
has ratio `(3+√5)/5>1`. Today's work is not trying to prove that false inequality.
The selected [introduction](../../thesis/chapters/01-introduction.tex) instead asks
about local maxima, other high-ratio families, and geometric relationships.

| Claim one might investigate | Current evidence | What remains genuinely uncertain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HKO is globally extremal, or bounds a specified larger product class | Proved restricted/local results; no global proof in this slice | Whether a remote, more general body beats it. Another positive polygon case cannot settle this |
| Every odd regular pair with both side counts at least five has full tensor containment `T₆⊆T₄` | Exact 5×5 source proof and checked strict 5×7 certificate; all-odd **angular** containment is weaker. The known 3×3 failure refutes a version including triangles, not this ≥5 hypothesis | Whether scalene closure types or removal of the pentagon factor break full containment. No calibrated probability is available |
| A rotated 5×7 pair is optimal among its independent affine images | Explicit feasible affine ascent | This claim is now disproved. The replacement question is the value and equality cases of the unknown affine maximum |

A finite family can supply exact examples, mechanisms, or a counterexample to an
intermediate claim. One more positive pair is not an unrestricted theorem.

The inherited selected manuscript already contains a sharp independent-affine
**5×5** theorem, rotation results, and a centrally symmetric partner theorem.
Those are not contributions of today's run. Today's **5×7** result gives the
capacity for all independent affine images of that particular pair, by checking
full matrix containment rather than merely its rotational projection. Its
imported ingredients remain the sparse reduction and Haim–Kislev formula.
The new certificate and its geometric implication are retained contributions;
priority/novelty against the literature has not been established.

The new elementary affine deformation says something more specific: the best
rotation is **not** the best affine shape in the 5×7 family. We do not know its
sharp affine optimum. We do know an exact finite optimization formulation, and
that looking only at matrix-polytope vertices could miss an optimum on a
one- or two-dimensional face. This is a proved obstruction to one tempting
shortcut, not evidence that the family beats the HKO benchmark.

Separately, today's local-cover proof controls arbitrary convex partners
**near the calibrated partner**, using the imported normal-triangle criterion.
It removes a continuum gap in an existing local argument. It does not solve the
global arbitrary asymmetric-partner problem. There is no dependency requiring
a global 5×7 optimum before this local result can be explained or assessed.

## What further attempts could actually deliver

These are conditional outcomes, not new execution assignments. “Failure” here
means failure of the specified attempt, not disproof of its target theorem.

| Concrete attempt | Success worth retaining | Partial or failed outcome | Evidence that changes the assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Follow the proved affine ascent to its next contact constraints; then analyze that contact face | An exact improved candidate and a proof on its contact family; a global theorem only if all other faces are controlled | Exact candidate without global bound, or too many unresolved competing faces. No sharp theorem in either case | A short symmetry classification and manageable face types favors completion; many inequivalent active sets or degenerate stationary families makes the proposed 30–60 minute slice less promising |
| Compress one 5×7 certificate representative into a short exact identity | A readable proof ingredient, with a verified symmetry map to the remaining representatives | Dense identities no clearer than the existing checker, or an identity only for an easy representative | A short identity for a hardest representative is stronger evidence than successful rewriting of an easy one. Failure of one ansatz says little about existence of another |
| Test one larger pair, choosing 5×9 for its scalene triple or 7×7 for its simpler geometry | Either a checked containment certificate for that pair or an exact separating witness refuting the corresponding containment claim | Numerical near-boundary output requiring diagnosis; no theorem | A strict separation would materially change a generalization conjecture. One more positive pair is weaker evidence and does not distinguish many possible structural explanations |
| Explain and place today's results without adding a theorem | A concise candidate theorem/proof insert, with prerequisites, trust boundary and proposed placement, suitable for Jörn's assessment | The insert duplicates the existing story, adds too much machinery, or exposes a dependency not yet adequately explained | Side-by-side candidate prose makes reader value assessable. Merely compiling it would not establish that it belongs in the final text |

A positive result on the first three attempts is not equally valuable. A sharp
new affine theorem changes the mathematical claim. A compressed proof may
change readability without adding a claim. A second positive finite pair may
be useful only as supporting evidence or an appendix example. A negative
separating witness could be more informative than that second positive pair,
but no evidence currently gives its probability.

## Compare variations and additions, not only replacement projects

The immediate baseline is to retain today's checked results and improve their
explanation; it does not require any new conjecture to be true. Against that
baseline, several cheap variations deserve comparison before a full extension:

1. **Vary the determinant task:** instead of trying to solve the global optimum,
   invert the proposed proof. Ask what would make the ≤2-dimensional-face
   reduction useless: exhibit an uncontrolled stationary face or show that the
   ascent endpoint has no optimality certificate. A minutes-scale source probe
   could identify the obstruction the larger optimization must overcome. It is
   informative because an explicit reduction and ascent already exist; its
   expected marginal return is unmeasured.
2. **Vary the explanation task:** draft one candidate theorem-and-example insert
   using the already proved ascent, alongside a version that omits 5×7 entirely.
   This compares added reader value and dependency burden without assuming a
   new theorem will arrive. It is additional editorial work, not a research
   success proxy.
3. **Add an independent adversarial view alongside a chosen route:** give the
   exact containment assertion and known 3×3 failure to a fresh reasoner and ask
   for the smallest plausible obstruction, rather than another positive-case
   proof. Its useful output is a falsifiable witness family or inequality, not
   a list of ideas. It might prevent a larger positive-case search, but there
   has been no controlled comparison demonstrating that advantage.
4. **Change the brainstorming input:** a targeted literature check on maximum-area
   linear images of a regular polygon in another polygon could reveal a theorem
   covering the determinant problem. This differs from unguided idea generation.
   A source with matching hypotheses would radically lower the remaining proof
   burden; nearby results without the needed linear-image or equality conditions
   would not. No such applicable source has been found or ruled out in this slice.

The alternatives are complementary. An exposition draft can proceed alongside
an adversarial mathematical probe if both are selected; they compete for review
and integration attention even when their compute is inexpensive. Cross-project
opportunity comparison belongs to the shared allocation discussion, not to a
claim that this Math menu exhausts everything worth doing.

## What remains unknown before recommending a larger allocation

We lack (i) a candidate insert showing final-reader benefit, (ii) the size and
structure of the remaining determinant face analysis, (iii) evidence comparing
alternative idea-generation methods, and (iv) a targeted novelty/applicable-
literature assessment. The earlier 15–60 minute ranges were planning allowances,
not calibrated forecasts of theorem delivery. The evidence supports concrete
small discriminators; it does not establish which route has the largest expected
value per minute. No fresh priority question is being asked on that basis.
