\subsubsection*{What Sage verifies}

SageMath checks the finite predicate above over the ordered field
\(\mathbb Q(t)\), where \(0<t<1\) and \(t^4-10t^2+5=0\).
Table~\ref{tab:hko-verification-interface} organizes the computation by proof
obligation.  Its HKO formulas are hand-derived inputs, so exact evaluation does
not prove their geometric correspondence.

HKO base point and symmetry directions

The finite choices read from \path{witness.json} are the selected words, the
five-column minors, and the beta coordinates held fixed in each feasible
section.  Floating-point entries in the witness are search hints and
diagnostics only.  Sage reconstructs candidate exact beta values and accepts a
row only after all rowwise checks in the table pass.  After the \(26\) rows
have passed, it performs the rank and positive-kernel checks over the same
ordered field.  A failed proof-facing check raises an exception, and the
successful summary is written only after all checks pass.

The complete executable and a line-by-line explanation organized by proof
obligation are stored in
\path{experiments/hko-local-maximum/theorem/verify.sage.py} and
\path{experiments/hko-local-maximum/theorem/verify.sage.py.explained.md}.
Keeping them with the witness and verification summary makes the complete
audit available without asking the thesis reader to reconstruct the proof
interface from several pages of implementation excerpts.

This separates the trust boundary.  Rust searches for the finite witness but
is not trusted for theorem correctness.  Sage checks exact algebraic
equalities, inequalities, ranks, and the positive kernel relation from the
hand-supplied formulas.  It does not prove the Hausdorff-local chart, the
existence and use of the transverse slice, the upper-function implication, or
the Taylor-remainder argument.  Nor does exact substitution prove that the
hand-supplied geometry and volume-variation formulas represent the intended
polytope, and the computation does not verify SageMath itself.  Those
mathematical identifications and implications are supplied by the surrounding
proof.
