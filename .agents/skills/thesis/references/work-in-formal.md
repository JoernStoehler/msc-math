# Work In Formal Mathematics

Use this reference for work whose editing or review surface is under `formal/`.
Also use `mathematical-status.md`.

Write `formal/` for future agents as the primary readers and for Jörn as the
mathematical reviewer. Optimize it for correctness, verifiability, and reuse
by thesis and experiment work rather than publication polish.

Keep developer-facing proof details, alternative formulations, status
annotations, and theorem-development dependencies visible when they improve
checking. Do not silently promote a formal note into accepted thesis proof.

Before requesting Jörn's review, rebuild `formal/main.pdf`, name the
mathematical items and dependencies by content or label, give rendered numbers
only as optional locators, and state the review questions in priority order.

One earlier review accepted a definition that pointed forward to a short
genericity proof because the prompt focused on mathematical support. Jörn
wanted the proof directly after the definition. Treat this as evidence that a
support-only formal review does not establish exposition order, not as a
universal placement rule.
