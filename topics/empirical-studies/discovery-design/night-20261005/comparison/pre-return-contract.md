## Shared-premise and domain checks

The benchmark is `B=(3+sqrt(5))/5`, with `sys=c_EHZ^2/(2 vol_4)`.
Arbitrary 5+5 products, fixed regular pentagon with arbitrary partner, nonproduct
ten-facet bodies and arbitrary convex four-bodies are distinct domains. A
counterexample propagates only into containing classes. Genuine hull sides and
facets must be counted rather than generator points.

For the fixed regular pentagon route, containing independent translates of all
ten templates gives `c>=1`. An exact cover with area below the HKO partner area
therefore gives a strict ratio improvement. At equal area, this gives `sys>=B`,
not necessarily exact equality without a capacity upper bound or accepted global
bound. Altering one fitting triangle need not alter the minimum over all ten.

The all-feasible normalized dwell-weight relaxation is already universal through
Minkowski closure/normalization. Second-order arguments require their actual
envelope regularity and complete branch scope; endpoint/crossing degeneracies
cannot be disposed of by a Hessian computed on one smooth branch. Product
structure and an assumed open support chamber must be checked on examples.

Hamiltonian shear already separates capacity/volume from bulk Williamson
spectrum. A distinct equality deformation of a ball can be mathematical content,
but repeating its established spectral separation is a solved control. A new
equality mechanism for a polytopal local maximum requires its own convexity,
symplectic-equivalence, local-domain and nontriviality checks. Exact covariance
variation alone does not prove a new maximum or reverse failed determinacy.
