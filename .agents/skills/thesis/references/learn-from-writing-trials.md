# Learn From Writing Trials

Use this reference when a writing, review, model-routing, or delegation trial
may change future work.

Use `$gpt-56-harness` for evaluation integrity and reusable agent-facing
instructions. This reference supplies only the writing-specific outcomes by
which that evaluation should be judged.

Compare whether the resulting passage is a useful new starting point and how
much expert editing or rewriting it needs. Inspect the reader understanding it
creates, mathematical and source calibration, explanatory organization, and
whether preparation reduced rediscovery without replacing primary sources.
Fluency, prose volume, confidence, and instruction recitation are not useful
substitutes.

Classify useful failures by the writing decision they should change: reader
model, source route, passage scope, proof spine, task decomposition, writer
prompt, model allocation, or whether prose generation should have begun at
all. Keep accepted thesis constraints distinct from mutable writing-workflow
priors.

Preserve a writing-specific lesson durably only when its observed behavior and
repair cost are likely to change future routing enough to repay maintenance
and context cost. Otherwise keep the evaluation packet in scratch as directed
by `$gpt-56-harness`.
