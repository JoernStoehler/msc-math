\subsection{A five-case comparison of local behavior}
\label{subsec:five-case-local-maxima-comparison}

Equality in Viterbo's bound does not itself imply local maximality: the exact
rotated-pentagon profile from
Theorem~\ref{thm:rotated-pentagon-product-formula} crosses
\(\operatorname{sys}=1\) and then increases above one.  Conversely, HKO has
value \((3+\sqrt5)/5>1\), and
Theorem~\ref{thm:hko-ten-facet-local-maximum} proves that it is locally maximal
in the ten-facet space.  Between these controls lie three equality candidates
for which the present evidence motivates, but does not prove, local
maximality.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{.25\linewidth}p{.19\linewidth}p{.46\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Body & Systolic ratio & Local status \\
\midrule
Regular triangle--hexagon, angle \(0\) & numerically \(\approx1\) & conjectured local maximum in the fixed nine-facet chart \\
Regular square--square, angle \(\pi/4\) & numerically \(\approx1\) & conjectured local maximum in the fixed eight-facet chart \\
CH2021 six-vertex body \cite{CH2021} & exactly \(1\) & conjectured local maximum among nearby fixed-nine-facet bodies, across adjacent combinatorial cells \\
HKO & exactly \((3+\sqrt5)/5>1\) & proved local maximum in the ten-facet space \\
Rotated-pentagon crossing & exactly \(1\) & proved not locally maximal \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Each fixed-facet conjecture is made in the local labelled dual-vertex chart,
modulo translations, positive scaling, and the identity component of the
linear symplectic group.  For the nonsimple CH2021 body, it includes the
adjacent combinatorial cells reached by nearby fixed-nine-facet bodies.  The
conjectures neither allow facets to be added nor assert local maximality among
all convex bodies.  The triangle--hexagon and square--square equality values
in the table are numerical reconstructions; no exact proof of those two base
equalities is used here.  In contrast, an exhaustive exact HK2017 calculation
reproduces \(\operatorname{sys}=1\) for the displayed rational CH2021 body
\cite{CH2021},
while making no claim about its neighborhood.

The finite screen recomputes the capacity scalar after perturbing each of the
three conjectural bases at three radii.  It tests signed directions from an
orthonormal quotient basis, a sparse antipodal random cover, the
\(SO(4)/U(2)\) orientation circle, and, for regular products, relative
rotation.  No target probe produced a material nominal increase, and none
produced an interval-separated increase.  The target capacity intervals were
broad, so these misses are nominal-scalar diagnostics only.  This negative
observation is meaningful only together with the pentagon control:
the theory-derived relative-rotation and \(SO(4)/U(2)\) probes recover its
known improvement, whereas the generic signed quotient basis and sparse
random cover miss it.  Thus a thin improving cone can escape the generic
screen.  The three misses support the conjectures and motivate branch-aware
exact analysis; they neither certify local maximality nor exclude untested
improving germs.
