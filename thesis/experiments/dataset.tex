\subsection{Algorithm Variants}
\label{sec:dataset-algorithms}

We implement four algorithm variants for computing EHZ capacity:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
Variant & Input polytopes & Cost & Fastest on \\
\midrule
HK2017 pruned & Any convex 4-polytope & Exponential in $F$ & Generic, $F \le 12$ \\
HK2017 unpruned & Any convex 4-polytope & 10--100$\times$ slower & (Tests only) \\
HK2017 SVD-only & Any convex 4-polytope & ${\sim}1.5\times$ slower & (Tests only) \\
Billiard & Lagrangian products only & Polynomial & Lagrangian products \\
& ($K_q \times_L K_p$, polygons) & & (100--1000$\times$ vs HK2017) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Algorithm variants. HK2017 unpruned and SVD-only are used only for
  correctness and ablation tests, not production datasets.}
\label{tab:algorithm-variants}
\end{table}

\subsection{Correctness Verification}
\label{sec:dataset-correctness}

We verify algorithm correctness through:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Literature agreement}: 8 polytopes with published capacity values
    (Table~\ref{tab:literature-polytopes}). HK2017 pruned reproduces all published
    values within $10^{-8}$ relative error. For Lagrangian products, billiard also
    agrees within $10^{-8}$ relative error.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{llr}
\toprule
Polytope & Source & Published $c_{\text{EHZ}}$ \\
\midrule
Simplex & Y. Nir thesis 2013 & $0.250$ \\
Hypercube & HK2019 Ex 4.6 & $4.000$ \\
HKO Pentagon & Haim-Kislev-Ostrover 2024 & $3.441$ \\
Lagrangian $\triangle \times_L \triangle$ & HK2017 + billiard & $1.500$ \\
Lagrangian $\triangle \times_L \square$ & HK2017 + billiard & $1.500$ \\
Symplectic $\triangle \times \triangle$ & Moser's theorem & $1.299$ \\
Symplectic $\triangle \times \square$ & Moser's theorem & $1.000$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Literature polytopes with published EHZ capacity values used for verification.}
\label{tab:literature-polytopes}
\end{table}

  \item \textbf{Algorithm agreement}: All applicable algorithm variants (HK2017 pruned,
    HK2017 unpruned, billiard) are run on 5 random generic polytopes + 5 random
    Lagrangian products. Results agree within $10^{-8}$ relative error. Additionally,
    2,867 Lagrangian products from sweep datasets cross-validate HK2017 vs billiard.

  \item \textbf{Conformality}: 11 scaled polytopes verify $c(\alpha K) = \alpha^2 c(K)$
    within $10^{-8}$ relative error.

  \item \textbf{Symplectic invariance}: 11 transformed polytopes verify
    $c(MK) = c(K)$ for $M \in \mathrm{Sp}(4)$ within $10^{-8}$ relative error.
\end{enumerate}
