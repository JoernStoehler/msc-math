# Independent review of roadmap v2

Reviewed `docs/coordination/roadmap-v2.html`, the retained DS rejection and current task-state briefing. This is an actual bounded content/dependency review, not approval of the thesis or a forecast validated by subsequent success. Anchors below refer to the HTML source lines.

**Verdict: useful status map, but not yet a defensible completion forecast.** Named owners, a fixed intermediate PDF, explicit nonacceptance and a final reserve are real improvements. The map still hides the amount of unread writing behind a DS “threshold” and leaves the critical human-review start conditional without calculating the consequences. No evidence establishes that the current writing process will produce an acceptable whole thesis by 22:00.

## Blocking work and dependencies

1. **DS completion is larger than repairing its entrance (lines 10, 13, 19, 23).** Jörn rejected the opening and did not read the rest. An acceptable replacement opening therefore cannot establish that the complete chapter meets the reading threshold. The 16:10/16:20 checkpoints should identify whether they cover an opening, the whole argument or the whole chapter. A named owner must deliver the complete coherent DS candidate, with unresolved defects and a completion estimate. This is a remaining submission prerequisite; its duration is unknown.

2. **AI lane is materially underspecified (line 14).** The current constraint is to restore v1 and apply the actual annotations with minimal changes after the broader v2 rewrite introduced rejected regressions. “Specific opening/author-intent feedback” does not state that task. The lane needs the v3 artifact and a check against v1 plus the annotations, including preservation of unaffected passages. Its correction and eventual human judgment remain prerequisites even if DS succeeds.

3. **The final artifact has future work, despite “checks complete” (lines 8, 15–16, 20).** Those checks concern the current 93-page snapshot. DS/AI replacement must be integrated, chapter joins and summaries checked where affected, and the resulting delivered PDF identified. The named integrator can own this without another global audit. Mark present checks as completed for that snapshot and the final changed-material checks as pending.

## Information gaps and unsupported assumptions

4. **The timing arithmetic is missing (lines 10, 18–19, 26).** With 150 minutes of human assessment, a start at 18:00 reaches 20:30 before any subsequent structural repair. Allowing another 1–2 hours for such a repair moves the corresponding start to 16:00–17:00, before accounting for interruptions or checking that repair. These are consequences of the roadmap's own estimates, not measured durations. The 90–150-minute assessment allowance has no stated basis in unread coverage or Jörn's remaining availability. By 17:00, the pessimistic single-repair route has used its pre-reserve slack. Waiting until 20:30 to identify deadline danger is too late.

5. **Human review coverage is absent (lines 15, 19).** A bounded synthesis check is not a writing assessment of every chapter. State which material has prior human acceptance, what changed afterward, and what remains unread. A whole-PDF verdict legitimately depends on an acceptable final artifact; independent preparation or useful reading of unaffected chapters need not depend on DS. Do not assume additional human reading is authorized or available merely because it could run earlier.

6. **Coordinator throughput remains unknown (lines 25–26).** The corrected statement about 161 seconds of explicit waiting is honest; it does not measure idle time or prove overload solved. Publication ownership removes one queue, while root still owns approach decisions and feedback prioritization. Report actual readiness-to-delivery delays when they occur; the two-minute target is not established performance.

**Recommendation:** publish this review beside the roadmap now. Make the existing checkpoint expose complete-artifact scope, unread coverage and the latest feasible assessment start. Do not add another roadmap-review cycle or represent an agent-approved opening as evidence of likely whole-thesis PASS.
