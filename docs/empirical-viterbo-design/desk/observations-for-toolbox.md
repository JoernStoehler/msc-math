# Evidence available when choosing discovery tools

2026-09-18. Compact research context, not a complete result catalogue. The
[agenda](research-agenda.md) records ownership; the source packets below retain
the detailed scopes. A negative for one representation is not closure of the
underlying scientific question.

## Already observed, with interpretation limits

* Historical table: 14,336 bodies, 4,096 generic and 10,240 products. Its active
  features are face-lattice counts/incidence and symplectic ridge-area summaries.
  A later join adds extreme-vertex covariance. This is not broad coverage of
  geometric or symplectic measurements.
* Rho/ridge scout: 14,334 joined rows, positive within all 18 buckets. Previously
  known associations with sys force much of the association; the features were
  not independently selected without reference to sys. Several ridge summaries
  are algebraically or nearly redundant. Source: `target-free-relation-scout/`.
* Existing projection methods include PCA, k-means and isolation forest; existing
  prediction methods include shallow trees/logistic models, conditional tail
  rules, residualized associations and grouped validation. Repeating a named
  method on the same columns is not new coverage. Their old success criteria
  primarily concern high-sys selection, not target-free mathematical discovery.
* Collision incidence checks found no common direction across the four tested
  pairs. Apparent 4-versus-1 cyclic branches came from same-type block-order
  multiplicity. All 10,240 product winners use six facets (three of each type).
  None of these observations excludes richer pairing arrangement or dynamics.
* Two-/three-bounce near-ties were less common in the upper sys decile (11.3%)
  than elsewhere (21.1%). More ties is not the existing explanation for high sys.
* SO(4) orientation changed seven of eight witness values; U(2) controls passed.
  Ridge mediation failed in five of eight sign comparisons. Fresh IID sampling
  won four matched allocation blocks, although orientation improved three of
  four bases. Allocation performance does not settle orientation mathematics.
* Tangentialization improved eleven of sixteen examples and worsened five.
  Support branch equality need not mean envelope relevance: in a corrected
  64-point tangent sample only 27 jointly minimized. Existing support-variation
  results already cover inward movement unused by all active data under their
  chamber hypotheses.
* Seven of ten original product buckets are theorem-excluded from true sys>1;
  they remain useful mathematical populations and controls. Rotations outside
  the symplectic group can leave the product theorem's hypotheses.

## Current opportunities and exclusions

P5 times arbitrary convex polygon has a dedicated owner and a calibrated cheap
ten-template fitting evaluator; no discovery collection has run yet. The ten
scales and contact data are not ten new symplectic capacities. The independently
affine pentagon theorem is now known within the project; arbitrary partners
remain outside it. Generic four-dimensional discovery must remain represented.

Useful output includes new geometric information, relations/inequalities,
meaningful separating families and mechanisms. A faster capacity algorithm alone
does not currently motivate a substantial research line. Nor does a result on
another narrowly parameterized family automatically do so. Broad exploration
does not require an exact conjecture in advance. Numerical observations,
conjectures, certificates and proofs remain distinct.
