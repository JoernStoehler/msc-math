## Recovery alternatives

**Maintained records/Git first; targeted logs second.** Start from retained cases
and artifacts; recover patches or trajectory excerpts for consequential gaps.
Likely fastest on this material, unmeasured. Failure: confident summaries become
premises and undocumented incidents disappear. Reject outputs that repeat superseded
findings or infer absence from missing documentation.

**Trajectory/index first; maintained projections second.** Start from lineage and
event indexes, preserving selected visible records as sourced cases. Can recover
knowledge never written into the repo. Failure: broad scanning, inherited duplicates
and inaccessible content create apparent completeness. Stop when chronology adds no
decision-relevant distinction; index silence cannot prove absent activity.

**Artifact/change first; decision recovery second.** Trace affected versions,
patches, reviewed inputs and outputs, then recover authority and explanations.
Strong for harmful edits and missing integration. Misses abandoned plans, delivery
failures and human overhead; “no diff” cannot mean “no incident.”

A provisional comparison pairs the first two on the same small cases, with artifact
evidence challenging both. Stop retrieval when additional evidence cannot distinguish
a named uncertainty affecting triage or handoff. Preserve unavailable-source gaps.

A maintained case should retain attribution, source/version anchors, observations
versus hypotheses, disputes/corrections, intervention application/outcomes, present
relevance and recheck conditions. It can own recorded disposition without certifying
every assertion. Raw evidence and current assignments keep their existing owners.
Incident records contribute corrective-action tracking; versioned case management
contributes amendment history; regression corpora contribute negative controls.
These analogies fail when they force one root cause, equate implementation with
effectiveness or turn selective historical labels into accuracy estimates.

## What evaluation can establish

Jörn's labels need operational definitions rather than assumed universal terminology:

- **Off-shell historical shadow:** recommend actions at a frozen historical point
  using contemporary information. Exposes bad advice; does not observe downstream effects.
- **Off-shell branched replay:** reconstruct state and actually continue under the
  candidate. Observes a new branch; historical downstream events cease to be valid
  inputs after divergence. Reconstruction fidelity remains uncertain.
- **On-shell prospective use:** apply the intervention in current work. Observes
  deployment behavior; one success establishes neither general reliability nor causality.
- **Higher-abstraction model:** predict behavior from an explicit model. Helps select
  probes, but predictions remain hypotheses and depend on the abstraction.

Candidates may change decomposition or information routing rather than imitate the
old trajectory. Selection, hindsight, solution leakage, shared lineage and changed
tools/instructions/context limit inference. Existing trajectories do not supply
counterfactual outcomes or action probabilities for stronger off-policy estimates.

## Coordination and first comparison

Required functions, where consequential, are preserving objectives/authority,
resolving priorities/dependencies, delivering actionable results, retaining corrected
knowledge, integrating outputs and closing/transferring ownership. A central agent,
registry, incident store or benchmark is a possible implementation, not necessary.

Central orchestration can connect whole-goal decisions but trap knowledge and become
a bottleneck. Distributed peers can continue independently but duplicate reasoning
or leave interfaces unowned. Bounded integration ownership may combine benefits,
but can quietly expand into central control. This hybrid preference is untested.

The [audit timeline](../history/process-audit/REPORT.md#pivotal-timeline-and-dependencies-with-costs-kept-separate)
and [stopped-coordinator handoff](../history/coordination/stopped-run-handoff.md)
record coordinated parallel work and failures, not causal topology comparisons.

A cheap first comparison is one interface rehearsal: concurrent outputs, an
unresolved obligation and a later correction. Compare recipients, ownership,
final disposition, repeated retrieval, central-context demands and user rescue;
include independent authorized work that should proceed without ceremony.
Use context reversal, withdrawn objections and superseded plans as intake controls.
Losing their distinctions rejects a candidate; passing does not certify an
architecture. No multiple project lifecycles need be launched for this comparison.
