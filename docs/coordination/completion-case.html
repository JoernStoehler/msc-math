These are proposed responsibilities, not ten already active assignments. Root and writing lead allocate Astra/Sol medium as appropriate; Luna gathers evidence and runs narrow checks. Writing lead chooses concrete child owners; integrator and root already have named owners.

Activation follows dependencies: cold readers need candidate text; whole consistency needs settled claims; layout checks need a build. Readers can inspect available sections while authors finish others, but ten lanes are not productive for all five hours. No need to invent work to spend fifty agent-hours. A second DS candidate is useful only when a specific alternative structure tests the writing crux; ten simultaneous DS rewrites are not ten independent deliverables.

Progress and cost units differ. A complete accepted chapter or a verified property of the frozen PDF is progress. A model call, thread or review attempt is an activity. One attempt may improve several artifacts; several attempts and shared experiments may support one accepted chapter. Retain activity-level token/quota observations, connect them many-to-many to outputs, and label shared or unallocated cost. Do not sum overlapping lane durations into wall time or count repeated reviews as separate accepted work.
