# Writer's diagnosis before reading the editorial return

Observed text, not an attribution of Jörn's full reason:

- The first paragraph says “contributes to computational symplectic geometry” and lists algorithms, experiments and proofs. It names work categories but does not explain why the capacity/volume question needs investigation or what capacity tells a reader that volume cannot. The term capacity is used through three opening paragraphs before even its normalized ratio appears.
- The empirical paragraph says populations extend beyond explicit families, which is relevant, but provides no concrete result or scientific tension by which a reader can understand what that approach has accomplished. Its last two sentences teach generic evidence cautions before the reader knows the investigation.
- The AI paragraph says participation makes the project “an occasion to examine” AI. This explains why a section exists, rather than what research role was delegated or what the project can teach within its limits. The source reflection has a specific mathematical example and a substantive contrast between mathematical argument production and task/exposition difficulties.
- The actual geometric questions come at the end of the Viterbo/HKO account. The opening has thus acquired a preface of categories rather than a developed reason for those questions. Its contributions then list theorem statements and numbers at nearly equal prominence, before the literature paragraph gives the embedding/convex-geometry significance that could help a reader value them.
- Source alignment and layout are genuine checks but are insufficient to establish whether an unaided reader can reconstruct the question, stakes, role of methods and principal contribution. The previous reviewer judged both functional adequacy and source alignment; the human result contradicts its adequacy judgment. No inferred causal account of that review failure is established.

Source-backed repair direction: develop the common capacity/volume problem and its significance, explain why finite polytope computations make an empirical investigation possible, then distinguish the local theorem from empirical patterns and side-family results by what each tells us. Explain AI's actual research role using the local-proof example and the reflection's limits. Do not invent a chronological chain in which data caused the main theorem: project-facts item 9 explicitly allows several research questions under the common computational/experimental/proof-by-computation frame. Do not turn this into a proof of AI effectiveness or new methodology novelty.

Conventional guidance consulted (24 October; interpretive aids, not human preferences):

- University of Manchester, Academic Phrasebank, “Introducing work”: dissertation introductions commonly supply context/value, a problem or gap, aims, methods, significance and structure; order is not fixed. https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk/introducing-work/
- Harvard College Writing Center, “Introductions”: orient the reader sufficiently to appreciate the thesis; explain the particular problem's stakes and the work's contribution, rather than only general topic importance. Avoid context/detail that does not serve that task. This is essay guidance, not a thesis-specific universal template. https://writingcenter.fas.harvard.edu/introductions
