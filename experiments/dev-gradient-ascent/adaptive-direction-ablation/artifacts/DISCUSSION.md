# Adaptive direction ablation

Generated from 9 trajectories (n=3 paired start/radius cells).

Direct observations: 9 / 54 proposals had a nominal multi-branch set; 3 start/radius pairs had distinct first directions. The six generic starts were single-branch in smoke, so retained output is the separate narrow-gap hard-state slice. Its base state has four nominal branches; later accepted states collapse to one. This deterministic evidence is descriptive and does not establish stationarity or local maximality.
