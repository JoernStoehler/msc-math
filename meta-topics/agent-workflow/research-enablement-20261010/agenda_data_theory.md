# Agenda data theory — 10 October comparison

Historical research comparison, retained from 337f on 12 October. Recommendations were not selected for execution. Source claims have not been re-audited during integration; the current topic owners supersede this dated snapshot. Paths in code spans retain historical source identity.

Two programmes look strongest; a third is worthwhile only after a specific relation emerges. Existing work already demonstrates the intended loop: an empirical ridge-concentration boundary led to a conservation-law theorem, while a moment inequality surviving every supplied example was disproved analytically by an omitted geometric family.

1. **Explain near-minimal ridge concentration through a quantitative circulation theorem.** The meaningful starting object is the complete signed ridge-area field on the facet-adjacency graph, rather than another pooled ridge/sys correlation. The independently audited argument in main’s `desk/relation-discovery/ridge-circulation.md` gives inverse-Simpson count \(N_2>3\) for general four-polytopes and \(N_2>4\) for Lagrangian polygon products or centrally symmetric bodies; both constants are sharp infima. Historical checks support implementation consistency, not the universal statement. The repository’s named effective-count column is Shannon \(N_1\), so formulas must distinguish them.

   A useful new conjecture is a **stability statement**: if \(N_2\) is close to its class lower bound, most flux must lie near a shortest admissible circulation, with an explicit bound on the omitted mass. Prove the graph implication first, then determine what geometric degeneration it actually forces. Abstract circulations can falsify the graph implication; they do not automatically constitute realizable polytope counterexamples. Explicit thin simplices, thin boxes, competing short cycles and symmetric nonproduct bodies are adversarial controls. The earliest discriminator is an extremal graph construction breaking the proposed stability estimate, or a proved concentration estimate whose remaining realization obligation is explicit. This needs complete ridge incidence and signed fluxes, not new capacity runs. A capacity consequence would require a separate argument.

   This also corrects interpretation of the old low-ridge enrichment: it survives some within-bucket controls and a finite alternative-source transfer, but two frozen equality-family paths reverse its optimizer promise. Neither the historical correlation nor the stability theorem would justify “smaller ridge sum means larger sys.”

2. **Replace scalar asymmetry with directional obstruction to pentagon-template fitting.** For fixed regular \(Q=P_5\), ten triangle-fitting scales determine capacity. The retained strict-gap paths show that initial \(c/A_2\) deficit and difference-body area excess do not order saturation onset. Disk-cloud and translated-template contrasts are an existing concrete puzzle, not a reason to acquire another large sample.

   Seek a short support inequality describing why a binding triangle fails to fit at the fixed-width ceiling \(A_2\), initially within a specified normal/contact chamber. Normalize support deficits by \(A_2\); retain directional supports, placements and dual witnesses. This avoids trivial dilation and replaces an incomplete asymmetry scalar with the geometry that actually enters fitting. The earliest return should be one exact width-preserving perturbation separating a proposed directional summary, or a proved closed-triple constraint explaining its threshold. Merely reproducing the onset LP is preparation. Changing one fitting scale does not establish changed capacity unless the minimum over all ten changes.

   Adversarial families should include pure odd support harmonics, contact-switch boundaries and engineered template hulls. Existing harmonic fibers already have equal entire width fields, area and all ten fits but different polar products; saturation therefore requires more than another generic “descriptor collision.” Central-symmetrization monotonicity and piecewise-linear fitting are forced by known concavity/LP facts. October’s accepted normal-fan theorems narrow the unresolved class: the fixed-axis all-angle distortion family is settled, while arbitrary partners and the general-matrix cone positivity remain open. Infrastructure already exists: small LP evaluator, raw geometry, support measurements and contact data. Exact reconstruction of the chosen contrast is the next dependency; a general optimizer is not.

3. **Conditional option: a mathematically constrained moment-inequality frontier.** This is useful ordinary convex geometry beyond capacity prediction, but it needs a concrete revised target before substantial work. The previous proposal
   \[
   b_4\ge16/3+(2/3)b_3
   \]
   survived all 49 supplied rows, then failed on strictly convex radial third-harmonic perturbations. Its failure appears at fourth order, despite agreement at leading order. The collection mixed related descendants, source controls and coupled construction parameters; it never supplied independent universal validation.

   A worthwhile continuation would derive a restricted sharp inequality or envelope using explicit harmonic/convexity hypotheses, then attack it with other pure harmonics, mixed modes and polygonal limits. Simply lowering the fitted coefficient until the old examples pass would provide little geometric understanding. First discriminator: symbolic expansion on an independently chosen perturbation mode showing either a violation or the precise restriction needed. Existing moment integration and exact harmonic calculations suffice; no capacity pipeline is required. Check literature before claiming novelty.

Across these programmes, exploration can inspect targets and adapt features openly. Once a statement or proposer is selected, later validation must freeze its formula, domain, feature definitions, sampling/conditioning law and rejection accounting before target exposure. Independent seeds test repeatability within a law; changed generators and adversarial constructions test broader scope. A complete observed success rate remains finite evidence. One rigorous in-domain counterexample refutes the conjecture; failure to find one cannot establish its universal quantifier.
