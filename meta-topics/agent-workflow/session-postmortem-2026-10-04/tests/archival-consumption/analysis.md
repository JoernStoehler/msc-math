# Actual-history archival-consumption evaluation

**The packet did not demonstrate a net reconstruction-cost saving.** All eight answers were adequate under the preregistered descriptive threshold, including all four archive-only answers. Packet access produced a few more explicit qualifications, but every packet consumer took longer; total source reading increased. The apparently largest quality difference is confounded by a historical fact available only through the packet. These results do not overturn the earlier generic-decision trial's lack of robust incremental benefit.

This distinct evaluation addressed the user's historical-reuse purpose more directly: consumers had to reconstruct original assignments, retained useful results, dispositions, authorization, source qualifications and experiment limits from actual records, without asking Jörn. It still did **not** measure reconstruction from an unselected raw-log corpus. The baseline was already a carefully prepared archive of relevant visible messages and exact receipts. Finding, selecting, redacting and packaging those families had already been done by investigators.

Four authored tasks each had one fresh native consumer with the bounded archive and one with the same archive plus optional access to the00:34 v2 candidate. All used inherited gpt-6-astra/xhigh, no model overrides or paid/API calls. No case or criterion changed after sampling. Every consumer had480 seconds and25000 delivered source words; packet reading counted. Two condition-blinded scorers rated factual recovery, decisions, fidelity, uncertainty and usefulness on the frozen0–18 rubric. The threshold was preregistered but not independently validated. ## Task3 information confound and adjudication

The packet exposed a separate Fix-child fresh/fork experiment through OUTPUTS.md. That child was outside task3's bounded baseline archive. The packet answer retained it; the baseline answer's unqualified “No forked model call was run” attracted a penalty. Scorers could see FS L95 in another task's canonical exhibits, although task3's baseline consumer could not. That reinforces why the raw score difference cannot be interpreted as an equal-information reasoning effect.

The additional probe is real and before the cutoff: the source owner verified starts at23:03:07.831 UTC and a completion report at child sourceL136,23:06:10.010 UTC. It was not a post-cutoff leak. R's own statement concerned its own fresh-exec investigation. The separate fresh/fork comparison still did not isolate context enrichment or establish equivalent effective context. Both answers' proposed next comparison remained substantially similar.

**Adjudication is separate from locked scores:** retain all numerical judgments, but give the task3 difference no credit as evidence of improved reasoning or equal-information retrieval quality. Neither silently rescore the baseline using facts it could not access nor turn the packet's extra fact into a fair experimental win. The experiment uncovered a source-access design limitation and a provenance gap: production subsequently added exact evidence `fp-l136`. The tested candidate remains byte-identical; that repair is not an evaluated improvement.

## Cost, limits and delivery integrity

Across four pairs, archive-only consumers read70786 charged source words in63 reader calls; packet consumers read75940 in83 calls: **+5154 words (+7.3%) and20 calls**. All four packet latencies were longer, by2.4,23.4,13.3 and15.5 seconds. Summed individual elapsed time was388.4 versus443.0 seconds; this sum is not the wall time of parallel execution. The near-cap archive C/R consumer stopped at24979 words, leaving little meaningful retrieval capacity. Nobody reached the eight-minute limit. One consumer per condition/task cannot distinguish stable effects from sampling variation.

Consumer native usage was7269814 cumulative input-plus-output tokens:6875136 cached input,365946 uncached input and28732 output. Archive and packet uncached input were172114 and193832 respectively. Scorer usage, including delivery repair, was845589 cumulative tokens:725248 cached input,99974 uncached input and20367 output. Combined consumer/scorer usage was8115403 cumulative tokens. Cached counts repeatedly include history; these are not unique words, billed spending, account quota attribution or realized future savings. Authoring, archive curation and investigator coordination were not instrumented as clean incremental costs and are not included or assigned invented estimates.

A mechanical launch typo (`start .`) caused seven harmless parser failures, corrected autonomously by those consumers; one omitted the punctuation. Their time and native usage remain counted. No consumer was replaced or rerun. All subsequent commands stayed within the assigned reader; no live project changes or user contact occurred.

A more consequential delivery failure affected both scorers. Their requested30000-token outputs were capped downstream at12000 approximate tokens. Serialized native tool records retained complete text, while both scorers correctly reported missing central sources. Stored full output therefore did not prove effective input delivery. The same scorers reread four smaller unchanged canonical sections and supplied explicit corrections; original incomplete ratings remain. Source/runtime inspection explained the exact truncation counts. Each consumer output was at most18776 UTF-8 text bytes (4694 approximate tokens including headers), below that effective cap; the four repair chunks also fit. See protocol-notes.md and the separate runtime audit for the distinction between stdout, stored results and constructed model input.

## Practical follow-through, without a new trial

The packet is a durable map of historical findings and outputs, but this trial does not establish that adding it to an already well-curated archive saves future work. Task2 and task4 traces show packet reading added almost directly to otherwise similar archive reading. The task3 consumer also searched the evidence appendix unsuccessfully for the separate probe's timing/provenance. A shorter topic-specific route from a decision to the exact supporting excerpt/code/receipt, with the observation cutoff and owner visible, is a concrete navigational improvement worth making. The added Fix-probe excerpt addresses one observed missing route. Any such production edit remains untested; no further experiment is authorized by this study.

This evaluation is closer to the user's purpose than the generic paper cases, because it requires retained incident knowledge. It remains a tiny, researcher-authored consumption test under an instrumented reader, with a curated baseline, a source imbalance, imperfect blinding and near-ceiling adequacy. It does not establish avoided future human reconstruction, general reliability, historical-Sol behavior change, sustained live coordination or causal repair of the original failures.
