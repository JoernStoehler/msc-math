\section{Ambient rotations and fresh sampling}
\label{app:trial-ambient}

The ambient action
\[
 P\times Q\longmapsto U(P\times Q),\qquad U\in SO(4),
\]
preserves Euclidean shape and volume. Capacity can change because $U$
need not preserve the symplectic form; the image need no longer be a
Lagrangian product for the fixed form.

Fresh sampling attained a larger maximal numerical ratio than the tested
rotations in each of four comparisons with equal numbers of capacity
evaluations. The searches shared their starting body. Rotation improved
three of the four starts. For example, a quadrilateral product improved
from $0.328$ to $0.529$ under the tested rotations, while fresh sampling
reached $0.774$. These results concern the prescribed rotation schedule
and starting products; they do not compare all ambient rotations with all
products from the sampling law. The schedule and complete allocation are
retained with the experiment's methods.
