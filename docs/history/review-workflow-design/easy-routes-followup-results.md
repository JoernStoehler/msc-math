# Easy writing routes: global and incremental follow-up

Completed 2026-09-18. Two final candidates, a no-change control, three fresh-reader inventories and a separate source audit are frozen. No human verdict has been consulted or inferred.

## Question and controls

Can two inexpensive revision operations improve an already source-grounded substantial DS section, and what concrete obstacles remain? Both start from the byte-identical reader-route final at easy-writing-routes commit 801ebda2. Root independently queued that unchanged original for human review. The no-change option is retained as `baseline.md`; neither new candidate silently replaces it.

The global operation reshapes the whole section after receiving the known dense-selection-paragraph concern. The incremental operation asks a fresh reader to reconstruct successive prefixes, revises in response, and then exposes the next prefix. Both have the actual scientific purpose and existing historical human feedback; neither discovers those preferences autonomously. All drafts and sources are in [the run directory](../../experiments/writing-quality/runs/20260918-easy-followup/).

## Evidence that is directly inspectable

The [baseline fresh-reader inventory](../../experiments/writing-quality/runs/20260918-easy-followup/evaluation/baseline-reader.md) was produced from the candidate alone without prior reviews. It flags ambiguous evidential status of the opposing numerical paths and the absence of a selected-versus-control result in the first large-screen paragraph. It treats splitting the long paragraph as a preference, not an independently established obstacle. That distinction prevents the supplied density concern from becoming ground truth by repetition.

The global author independently reframed the first large-screen maximum as insufficient for judging selection benefit. The final explains three experiments in separate paragraphs and gives the section explicit scientific questions. The [fresh final-reader inventory](../../experiments/writing-quality/runs/20260918-easy-followup/evaluation/global-reader.md) nonetheless identifies unresolved transfer-rule/source and numerical-target lineage questions. These are specific remaining issues, not a consensus declaration of quality.

## Interpretation limits

These are single executions, with different intermediate information and review scopes. More explicit final prose may reflect supplied scientific details rather than an intrinsic advantage of an architecture. Prefix reading can surface missing prerequisites but may also solicit explanations already available later; global review can see relationships but may miss local gaps. The trial does not estimate those error rates.

An agent's claim to understand does not establish Jörn's acceptance. Independent reconstruction and source comparison provide artifacts to compare against his marks, not calibrated quality scores. Final inventories must remain unmerged where reviewers disagree about whether an issue is substantive, contextual or preferential.

## Incremental result and final inventories

The [incremental final](../../experiments/writing-quality/runs/20260918-easy-followup/incremental/final.md) expands the original through three actual reader-prefix cycles. Initial questions prompted product and bucket definitions. Subsequent questions prompted a precise theorem locator, qualification of numerical-path inference, and recovery of exact selection/comparator definitions. The final uses a table of transfer means and explicit bucket-level concentration groups. These are observable additions backed by retained source snapshots, not an AI preference score. The route acquired further information from sources, so any advantage cannot be attributed solely to staging the review.

The [fresh final reader](../../experiments/writing-quality/runs/20260918-easy-followup/evaluation/incremental-reader.md) still cannot identify the transfer controls' sampling baseline or the geometry of the opposing paths. Their observations do not require adding every detail to this section: stable references or changed scope could resolve some dependencies. The [source audit](../../experiments/writing-quality/runs/20260918-easy-followup/evaluation/source-review.md) verifies added means, group sizes, aggregation and theorem label, but flags an inherited unsupported inference about dataset membership, ambiguity in the caveat's application to later targets, and a small terminology change from 30 selection sets to 30 rules. None is silently repaired after final review.

Source and reader checks answer different questions. For example, the transfer table's numbers can match the retained record while its omitted control rule still obstructs interpretation. Likewise, locating a real theorem label does not make the raw LaTeX reference display correctly in standalone Markdown. The incremental source-note appendix explicitly remains draft documentation requiring thesis integration.

## What changed and what did not

Both routes executed cheaply enough to remain plausible local interventions. Both improved specific inferential visibility, and both left inspectable obstacles. Readers supplied actionable questions without an overall validated quality detector. This supports another artifact-based test; it does not establish a reliably passing authoring workflow, calibrated completeness of issue detection, transfer to another genre, or thesis-scale throughput. No other genre was attempted in this follow-up, deliberately avoiding a third superficial trial.

The worktree remains active retained experimental work because root and the review desk need stable absolute candidate paths. No production sources, scientific data producers or loaded harness files changed. A reader subagent independently committed its first prefix prompt/output as 469781a8; the coordinator commits the remaining coherent bundle. Both commits preserve thread provenance.
