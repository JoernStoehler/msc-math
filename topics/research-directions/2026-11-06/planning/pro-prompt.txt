ATTRIBUTED STARTING EVIDENCE (PROJECT ACCOUNTS, NOT INDEPENDENT PROOF VERIFICATION)

Objects and conventions. For convex bodies in R^4, S(K)=c_EHZ(K)^2/(2 vol_4(K)); a ball has S=1. EHZ capacity is the least action of a generalized closed characteristic. A Lagrangian product P ×_L Q places two planar factors in q- and p-planes, unlike a product in two symplectic planes. Haim–Kislev–Ostrover's externally published pentagon-product counterexample has S=(3+sqrt(5))/5. The unrestricted upper bound one is therefore false; useful questions remain about sharp bounds, restricted classes, geometry, dynamics and computation.

Finite capacity computation. The Haim–Kislev formula uses normalized facet rows a_i, ordered facet words sigma and nonnegative weights beta with sum beta=1 and sum beta a_sigma=0. With Q=sum_{j<k} beta_j beta_k omega(a_sigma_j,a_sigma_k), capacity is 1/(2 Q_max) over the complete feasible family. Some minimizing representation uses distinct facets. One feasible word gives an upper bound on capacity, not a certificate of improvement in S; global coverage is consequential. Fixed-word KKT systems are linear; singular systems have stationary families. The Rust implementation has an outward-enclosure/exact-fallback scalar route and a separate rational product route. Exact arithmetic does not prove complete enumeration. Outputs of tied discrete words are not all physical minimizing trajectories. Search cost grows sharply with combinatorial complexity.

Local optimization and facet changes. The project has a fixed-ten-facet local maximum theorem for HKO, modulo translations, dilations and linear symplectic maps. A later agent-reviewed proof extends it to at most eleven genuine facets in an existential Hausdorff neighborhood. A different ten-facet hexagon–quadrilateral product P* has ratio one and a non-strict local maximum with two equality parameters modulo symmetries. Its general eleven-facet extension is unresolved, although some facet-birth patches were proved. Local proofs use feasible action sections and positive relations among derivatives, not an assumption that every KKT branch varies smoothly.

The October campaign proves HKO optimal over its full GL(4) orbit, yet seven of ten specified simultaneous radial cuts increase S nearby. For these profiles, D+ log S=A sum w_i-B max w_i with 6A<B<7A: up to six nonzero cut speeds have negative derivative, seven unit speeds positive. A twenty-facet member has an exact-certificate ratio about 1.051914 at parameter 3/10. This does not determine the minimum number of facets allowing ascent; twelve through sixteen remain open. A general simultaneous-splitting derivative theorem already exists under a rank condition on every maximizing word. There is also a nonpositive first-variation result for at most two specified characteristic-invariant affine hinges retaining original rows; it is not a twelve-facet neighborhood theorem. Continuation at 7/20 and two-parameter extensions were later imported but remain unreviewed leads. These local results have agent proof/certificate reviews, not external refereeing.

Planar products and convex geometry. A product has a capacity optimizer using at most three facets per factor, but this does not classify every optimizer. Regular-polygon rotation profiles and some independently affine factor classes are solved. For a fixed regular pentagon, all centrally symmetric partners and several restricted asymmetric normal families have results; arbitrary asymmetric partners remain open. Width information can miss odd support-function data. A universal difference-body formula fails for triangle pairs. There are existing billiard/translation-covering formulations and finite triangle-type reductions for polygonal factors, potentially replacing full four-dimensional orbit enumeration with planar area minimization. The universal lower-area bound, rather than just constructing an arrangement, is the important missing implication. General-matrix cone reductions are not positivity proofs.

Dynamics. The QP formulation computes scalar capacity on degenerate products. The project's flow-graph method instead composes affine tubes following actual facets and solves closed-word fixed-point equations. Its conditional correctness theorem requires nonzero relevant symplectic pairings, independence of every subset of up to four facet rows, and finite-orbit regularity; products violate these hypotheses. Capacity continuity does not by itself describe orbit limits, disappearing segments, orbit families or changes of coding. Exact implementation has explicit unsupported/singular cases. Chaidez–Hutchings is an external source for polytope Reeb-dynamics ideas, not a proof of this implementation's claims.
