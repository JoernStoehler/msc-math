The later experiments supply more concrete comparisons. **Severity:** Minor at this sample’s scope; potentially consequential if this sentence were the sole support for a thesis claim about selection effectiveness. **Uncertainty:** Moderate, because the sample does not establish where fuller evidence is presented.

It is peripheral to the mathematical account, so the omission does not block the argument. **Severity:** Minor. **Uncertainty:** Moderate; surrounding thesis context may make the phrase fully informative.
