# Bounded prior-art check: a finite HKO-intersection improvement

Checked 29 October 2026. The project now has finite checks passing for the **four-dimensional** body `K_{3/10}=H ∩ L_{3/10}^{-1}H` (twenty facets), with reported `rho=c_EHZ^2/(2 Vol_4) ≈ 1.0519141388294875`, against `rho(H)=(3+sqrt(5))/5≈1.0472135955`. Independent mathematical and replay audit of this fixed finite value is still pending; this is a literature comparison, not verification of the value.

## Inspected primary-source state

- [Haim-Kislev–Ostrover, *A Counterexample to Viterbo's Conjecture*, arXiv:2405.16513v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.16513v3), §1.1 Question 2 and Proposition 1.4, calls the HKO pentagon value the largest then known in **four dimensions** and asks for the maximal ratio among all convex bodies. Its higher-dimensional symplectic 2-product constructions achieve powers of that value; these larger numbers in dimensions eight and above are not four-dimensional comparators. The paper contains no `H ∩ L^{-1}H` example or value `1.0519` in the inspected statements.
- [Haim-Kislev, *Dynamical extensions of Zoll to nonsmooth convex bodies*, arXiv:2511.16644v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16644v1), Example 1.12 and Proposition 1.13, repeats the HKO value and discusses numerical single-cut capacity behavior and restricted near-boundary cuts. It reports no finite improved EHZ ratio or simultaneous multi-cut family.
- [Balitskiy–Mitrofanov–Polyanskii, *Triangle covering problems and the Viterbo inequality in the plane*, arXiv:2603.12495v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12495v2), §6.1, reproduces HKO via planar product covers. Its apparent extremality discussion concerns a **fixed regular-pentagon factor in a Lagrangian product**, a narrower class than a mixed-coordinate intersection. It reports no larger four-dimensional value.
- [Chaidez–Tanny, *The Pu Inequality and the Viterbo Conjecture with Finite Symmetry*, arXiv:2610.31906v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.31906v1), introduction, continues to cite HKO as the counterexample and proves the weak EHZ bound in a quaternion-symmetric class. It provides no competing nonsymmetric improved example.

No primary source reporting this finite value, an earlier nonproduct HKO descendant, or a larger **convex four-dimensional EHZ** counterexample surfaced. Generic searches for `Viterbo` and decimal values were badly contaminated by geographic and unrelated numerical hits, so their negative result is weak evidence. ## Assessment and wording

If the pending fixed-value audits pass, the verified mathematical assertion will be an **explicit four-dimensional convex body improving the HKO EHZ ratio** by about `0.00470054` in absolute ratio. The checked literature supports saying it exceeds the **HKO value identified as largest known in the authors' 2025 version**, with no larger four-dimensional primary example located in this bounded update. It does **not** support an unqualified world-record or first-ever claim. It also does not determine the global optimal convex-body ratio, improve the strong-capacity coincidence statement in a distinct logical way, or compare numeric ratios across different symplectic dimensions without normalizing the question.
