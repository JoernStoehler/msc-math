# Claim/source map for the replacement context

Checked 16 September 2026 against the linked primary-source versions.  This is
an evidence map, not an exhaustive bibliography.

| Claim used in the passage | Primary source and pinpoint | Scope / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Viterbo's proposed inequality is \(c(K)^n\le n!\operatorname{vol}(K)\) for convex \(K\subset\mathbb R^{2n}\). | C. Viterbo, *Metric and isoperimetric problems in symplectic geometry*, JAMS 13 (2000), Theorem 5.1; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0894-0347-00-00328-3). The formulation is also reproduced as Conjecture 1.2 in [HKO v3, lines 68--74](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.16513v3). | The universal statement is now false. The term “Viterbo inequality” remains useful for asking whether the inequality holds on a specified subclass. |
| Haim--Kislev and Ostrover disprove the conjecture in every symplectic dimension at least four; in dimension four their example is a Lagrangian product of regular pentagons with ratio \((3+\sqrt5)/5>1\). | P. Haim-Kislev and Y. Ostrover, *A counterexample to Viterbo's conjecture*, Ann. of Math. 203 (2026), Theorem 1.3 and Proposition 1.4; [journal page](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2026/203-2/p05), [arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.16513v3). | The paper disproves the universal inequality; it does not determine the largest possible ratio or local optimality of the pentagon product. |
| On every convex body in \(\mathbb R^4\), the EHZ and cylindrical capacities coincide. | A. Abbondandolo, O. Edtmair and J. Kang, *On closed characteristics of minimal action on a convex three-sphere*, arXiv:2412.01777v1, Corollary 1; [HTML, lines 117--130](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.01777v1). HKO v3 independently reports the consequence in [§1.1, lines 154--155](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.16513v3). | The stronger dynamical theorem—every systole bounds a disk-like global surface of section—assumes a **uniformly convex** domain (Theorem 1). The capacity equality in the last sentence of Corollary 1 extends to **every convex body** by approximation/continuity. Neither statement says that the Gromov width agrees with EHZ. |
| The Gromov width can be strictly smaller than EHZ on a convex four-dimensional body. | Abbondandolo--Edtmair--Kang, discussion preceding Corollary 1, especially [lines 110--124](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.01777v1); HKO, Theorem 1.3. | Thus “all normalized capacities agree” is false even though \(c_{\rm EHZ}=c_Z\) in dimension four. |
| The Viterbo inequality holds for a planar Lagrangian product \(K\times Q\) when one factor is an arbitrary quadrilateral; the same paper treats triangular factors and affinely regular hexagonal factors. | A. Balitskiy, I. Mitrofanov and A. Polyanskii, *Triangle covering problems and the Viterbo inequality in the plane*, **arXiv:2603.12495v2** (19 Aug. 2026), Theorem 1.2 and §§4--5; [latest abstract/version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12495), [Theorem 1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12495v2). The triangular sharp bound appears in §4, in particular the area bound at §4.2. | This is a result about Lagrangian products \(K\times Q\subset\mathbb R_q^2\times\mathbb R_p^2\), not arbitrary four-dimensional polytopes. Symmetry \(c(K\times Q)=c(Q\times K)\) lets either factor play the stated role. A general orthogonal rotation mixing the two Lagrangian planes leaves this scope. Version 2 supersedes the v1 cited in the later review packet. |
| Recent nonsmooth Zoll work studies the pentagon product through hyperplane cuts. Certain boundary-near cut directions are proved additive, while generalized-Zoll status is unknown and broader “cuts extremizer” evidence is numerical. | P. Haim-Kislev, *Dynamical extensions of Zoll to nonsmooth convex bodies*, arXiv:2511.16644v1, Example 1.12 and Proposition 1.13; [HTML, lines 203--214](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16644v1). Definitions and the smooth implication are at [lines 70--101](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16644v1). | Do not turn the numerical sentence in Example 1.12 into a theorem. Proposition 1.13 concerns specified cut normals and cuts sufficiently near the boundary; it is not local maximality under arbitrary facet perturbations. |
| Haim--Kislev gives a finite optimization formula for EHZ capacity of a convex polytope. | P. Haim-Kislev, *On the symplectic size of convex polytopes*, GAFA 29 (2019), Theorem 1.1; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00039-019-00486-4), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.03494). | The thesis's QP algorithms and upper-function construction are derived from this imported formula; their reductions, implementation contracts, and local certificate are separate claims. |
| Chaidez--Hutchings develop combinatorial Reeb dynamics for four-dimensional convex polytopes and a computer-search framework. | J. Chaidez and M. Hutchings, *Computing Reeb dynamics on four-dimensional convex polytopes*, J. Comput. Dyn. 8 (2021), abstract and main results; [journal page](https://doi.org/10.3934/jcd.2021016). | The thesis's flow method is an adaptation with its own exact hypotheses. It should not be described as automatically computing EHZ for every polytope. |
| Zoll contact forms are local systolic maximizers in the smooth contact setting. | A. Abbondandolo and G. Benedetti, *On the local systolic optimality of Zoll contact forms*, GAFA 33 (2023), Theorem 1 and the convex-domain application; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00039-023-00624-z). | This is a smooth precedent, not a theorem about the nonsmooth HKO polytope. The thesis's fixed-facet local theorem is complementary rather than an instance of it. |
| The capacity--volume problem remains linked to Mahler's conjecture on centrally symmetric products. | S. Artstein-Avidan, R. Karasev and Y. Ostrover, *From symplectic measurements to the Mahler conjecture*, Duke Math. J. 163 (2014), Theorems 1.6--1.7; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1215/00127094-2794999). See also [BMP v2, lines 180--182](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12495v2). | HKO invalidates the unrestricted Viterbo conjecture, not this restricted equivalence or the centrally symmetric question. |

## Triage decisions

- The obsolete claim “\(c_{\rm EHZ}=c_Z\) is open for convex bodies in \(\mathbb R^4\)” is excluded. It was true as an older status report, but was superseded by arXiv:2412.01777 in December 2024.
- The all-convex-body capacity corollary is kept separate from the uniformly-convex dynamical theorem.
- The Balitskiy--Mitrofanov--Polyanskii scope is stated only for Lagrangian products and cites the current v2, not v1.
- Haim--Kislev's numerical evidence about cuts is not upgraded to proof.
- No novelty or priority claim is made for the thesis beyond the stated, repository-supported scope of its results.
