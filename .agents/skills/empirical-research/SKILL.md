# Empirical Research

Route work to the one-purpose references needed for the live decision. Do not
load the package as a process checklist or assume its references exhaust the
ways research can fail. Detailed scientific facts, current beliefs, data, code,
and completion state remain with their repo or active scratch owners.

## Status

This package is an early, evidence-informed working hypothesis. Its defaults
have been exercised on only a small number of task shapes; their net value,
boundary conditions, and interactions are mostly unmeasured. Skill-compliant
work can still be scientifically poor, unnecessarily expensive, or worse than
an omitted alternative. Adapt defaults to current evidence and report material
burden or failure without manufacturing process telemetry during routine work.

Read `references/improve-empirical-research-workflow.md` when explicitly
testing, diagnosing, or revising this process knowledge. It owns the current
problem-finding and change-evidence discipline.

## Questions, Experiments, And Interpretation

- Read `references/design-experiment-packets.md` when turning a question,
  hypothesis, anomaly, or proposed method into a bounded empirical packet.
- Read `references/interpret-results.md` when deciding what an observation
  changes, what it does not show, and whether it matters to the thesis.
- Read `references/coordinate-research-lines.md` when leading or coordinating a
  multi-packet line, choosing delegation/review structure, or managing a
  portfolio of competing lines.

Keep observations, hypotheses, inferences, predicted outcomes, experiment
proposals, and value judgments distinguishable when their differences affect a
decision. They may be linked in one argument or working model; they need not be
forced into one fixed ontology or document format.

## Data, Artifacts, And Handoffs

- Read `references/manage-data-artifacts.md` when producing, changing,
  regenerating, or checking experiment data and generated artifacts.
- Read `references/review-packet-readiness.md` when deciding whether a packet
  can serve another researcher, a demonstration, thesis writing, or
  publication/reproduction.
- Use `$rust` when project-specific mathematical, numerical, observability,
  performance, or reusable-crate contracts matter in Rust code.
- Use `$licca` before writing any command Jörn should run on, to, or from
  LICCA. Keep its elevated-resource and Slurm rules in that skill.

## Investigation Displays

- Read `references/design-investigation-displays.md` when a plot, table, compact
  report, or interactive view is meant to help researchers notice structure,
  test an interpretation, expose artifacts, or choose the next packet.
- Use `$thesis` instead when the display's main job is reader-facing explanation
  or publication design. An investigation display may later become a thesis
  candidate, but that is a new consumer and review transition.

## Agent Workflow And Evaluation

- Read `references/allocate-model-effort.md` when model/effort, context mode,
  decomposition, or review configuration materially affects a recurring
  research choice.
- Use `$gpt-56-harness` for evaluation integrity and reusable agent-facing
  instructions. Preserve empirical-research outcome measures here, not a
  duplicate general evaluation protocol.
