# Primary source for the 3D zonoid projection inequality

The exact inequality is Theorem 5.1 of Fradelizi–Madiman–Meyer–Zvavitch, “On the volume of the Minkowski sum of zonoids,” *Journal of Functional Analysis* 286 (2024), Paper 110247; arXiv:2206.02123v1, https://arxiv.org/html/2206.02123. For a zonoid `A` in R^3 and unit `u,v`, it states

`|A|_3 |P_{span(u,v)^perp} A|_1 sqrt(1-<u,v>^2) <= |P_{u^perp}A|_2 |P_{v^perp}A|_2`.

For orthonormal `u,v`, this is precisely volume times the length of the common 1D projection bounded by the product of the two projection areas. The proof first uses GL(3) covariance to reduce to coordinate directions `e1,e2`, then approximates a zonoid by a zonotope with generators `(x_i,y_i,z_i)`. It writes the determinant-sum inequality. For fixed y,z, both sides are convex piecewise-affine in each x_i. It reduces the inequality to critical values where `(x_i,z_i)` are parallel and to the limits at infinity; those cases reduce inductively to the R^2 case and Bonnesen's planar inequality. Thus the source has an exact coordinate-elimination method that may be structurally relevant, although this does not itself transfer to the rank-four symplectic inequality.

Citation-number distinction: in Fradelizi et al., “Volume and Projection Inequalities I: Zonoids and Courtade’s Conjecture,” arXiv:2608.12681v1, bibliography item [10] is Manui–Ndiaye–Zvavitch, “On the volume of sums of anti-blocking convex bodies” (2025), https://arxiv.org/html/2409.14214. That anti-blocking paper's introduction says the weaker dimension-three zonoid projection-ratio inequality is valid and points readers to its reference [20], the FMMZ 2024 paper above. It is therefore a secondary mention, not the source of Theorem 5.1's proof. The arXiv:2608.12681 paper's Introduction credits ref [10] for the dimension-3 log-submodularity/equivalent inequalities, but its dimension-three projection theorem is explicitly and directly proved in FMMZ Theorem 5.1. Keep that citation distinction visible.
