# Causal evidence: the explicit HKO-improving four-polytope

This note supports the postmortem's main outcome: the exact body \(H_{3/10}\), with twenty genuine facets, has systolic ratio \(1.0519141388294875\ldots\), strictly above the HKO product's \((3+\sqrt5)/5\). It separates the result's mathematical dependencies from adjacent results and records counterfactuals that could have shortened the route without presupposing the final answer.

## What the result actually says

Let \(H=P\times_L(-J_2P)\) be the HKO product of two regular pentagons. The paper's twenty-row family retains each of the ten support inequalities of \(H\) and adds a second, inward-tilted row \(a_i+tD_i\) for each old row. Equivalently, \(H_t=H\cap L_t^{-1}H\), with \(L_t\) specified in the paper. For small positive \(t\), every old and new row is a genuine facet, so the cut creates twenty facets. At \(t=3/10\), exact calculation gives 75 vertices, each simple, capacity \(1019/(410\tau)=(1019/1025)c_{EHZ}(H)\), and volume \((502711205+99664289\sqrt5)/130445600\). Although capacity falls slightly, volume falls enough that the ratio rises by the exact positive difference printed in the theorem. The result is one explicit body, not an optimizer theorem for the family or all convex bodies. See `topics/capacity-and-qp/convex-systolic-optimization/sections/hko-ascent.tex` (family and infinitesimal result) and `sections/hko-moderate.tex` (finite body and proof).

The logical hinge is the capacity direction. A feasible closed characteristic gives a lower bound on the maximizing quadratic action \(Q_{max}\), hence an upper bound on capacity. It cannot by itself prove that the body's capacity is large enough for a larger systolic ratio. The certificate therefore establishes both a global upper bound \(Q_{max}\le 205\tau/1019\) and an explicit positive seven-label word attaining equality. Exact volume and the exact positive ratio difference complete the claim. This distinction is made explicitly in `sections/hko-moderate.tex` and in the independent proof-coverage note `/tmp/msc-math/restart/geometry-frontiers/hko-beyond-infinitesimal/COVERAGE-PROOF.md`.

## Causal chain and dependencies

1. **The product supplied a structured starting point.** The ten normals split into two regular-pentagon factor planes. This made characteristic closure, cut profiles, and symmetry tractable. The HKO product and its capacity were known inputs, not discoveries of this run. The separate full-affine-orbit theorem established that HKO remains optimal under every invertible linear map; it does not imply optimality among arbitrary convex bodies. That rigidity is valuable contrast and motivates leaving the affine orbit, but the exact arithmetic for \(H_{3/10}\) does not depend on the affine-orbit proof.

2. **Ten coordinated facet births gave a computable direction.** The multicut line chose one characteristic-invariant radial hinge on each of the ten facets, producing twenty retained/new rows. A general first-variation theorem reduces the right derivative of log systolic ratio to the difference between the normalized volume response and the largest occupation response among capacity-maximizing characteristics. The actual hinge profiles are convex and constant along their own characteristic directions. Those hypotheses permit a repeated-visit elimination: although a simple shortest characteristic exists, that fact alone would not control every maximizing occupation measure; the elimination shows a simple maximizing word has at least as large a response. Exact enumeration then covers the remaining simple words: 190 maximizing charts (50 points, 140 intervals) give maximum response \(1/125\), while the facet-volume response is \(8/625-8\tau^2/9375\). Their difference yields derivative \((20+64\sqrt5)/9375>0\). Thus every sufficiently small positive parameter improves the ratio. See `sections/hko-ascent.tex`, especially its repeated-word lemma and exact response certificate; the complete source/replay/review map is `certificates/hko-ascent/README.md`.

   This establishes nonlinear Hausdorff instability, but not a useful explicit parameter or a finite-body global capacity. The derivative computation, simple-word catalogue, and volume response are genuine prerequisites to this infinitesimal theorem; they are not by themselves a certificate at \(t=3/10\).

3. **The moderate-parameter line converted a candidate into a theorem.** Root assigned the beyond-infinitesimal line around 00:14 UTC on 29 October, explicitly seeking a materially stronger body or a precise moderate-parameter global-capacity route; its continuation record is `/tmp/msc-math/restart/geometry-frontiers/hko-beyond-infinitesimal/CONTINUATION.md`. A feasible seven-word at \(t=3/10\) supplied the exact candidate \(Q=205\tau/1019\) and its proposed ratio. That witness selected a useful target, but it was only a lower bound on \(Q_{max}\). The line therefore split proof obligations rather than promoting the numerical/feasible candidate prematurely.

   The global upper-bound proof exploits the special geometry and finite word structure. Exact vertex enumeration gives a simple twenty-facet body and the exact directed transition graph. The published simple-minimizer theorem reduces a capacity minimizer to a simple word; graph adjacency and the sign of the symplectic pairing further restrict transitions. For every word, an exact positive direction of the quadratic action on the closure kernel rules out an interior maximum; zero-extension propagates each such obstruction to cyclic extensions. There are 77,927 certified prefix-curvature exclusions. An integer traversal then covers 43,110 surviving cycles with 2,326 symmetry-reduced leaves and zero unknown cycles. Exact KKT checks handle all leaves. Singular stationary systems need special care: one arbitrary particular solution with a negative weight does not prove the affine family infeasible. The final method first compares a family's constant objective with the witness, then uses a proved containment bound for higher families, and exactly enumerates vertices of the remaining bounded nonnegative-parameter polyhedra. The final counts are 984 singular families already at/below target, 21 excluded by the coarse containment bound, 25 excluded by exact feasibility enumeration, and zero feasible singular families above target. The exact positive seven-word witness matches the upper bound.

   Separately, exact enumeration of the 75 simple vertices and Lawrence's volume formula, evaluated with two generic linear functionals, gives the exact volume. The checks are all over \(\mathbb Q(\tau)\), with exact sign decisions; numerical decimals are summaries, not acceptance predicates. Independent proof-coverage and extracted-copy replay reports are recorded in `/tmp/msc-math/restart/geometry-frontiers/hko-beyond-infinitesimal/COVERAGE-PROOF.md` and `/tmp/msc-math/restart/moderate-hko-independent-replay/REPLAY.md`. The latter independently recomputed the geometry, graph, curvature exclusions, leaves, attainer and volume, with a negative control showing that a missing leaf creates unknown cycles. No costly producer rerun is needed for this postmortem.

4. **Audits, integration, and delivery were part of the claim's support.** The analytic coverage review, finite replay, assembled mathematical and reader reviews, root's 56-page inspection, and final archive replay address distinct risks. They do not constitute external refereeing or human thesis acceptance. The final packet's README records the exact PDF and source-archive hashes and their scope. ## Other branches: related, but not prerequisites

The ten-cut family first produced the twenty-facet derivative result. In parallel, exact weighted-response analysis showed that within this fixed radial nonnegative-speed cone, any seven of the ten unit cuts ascend and at most six nonzero speeds do not. This supplies a distinct seventeen-facet local ascent and a facet-count discriminator; it did not construct or certify \(H_{3/10}\). A later two-arbitrary-hinge analysis proved nonpositive first variation for two characteristic-invariant affine hinges retaining all old rows, both same-facet and distinct-facet cases. That is a useful obstruction, but it does not prove twelve-facet local maximality, control higher order, or bound arbitrary hinges. The full-affine-orbit theorem is another independent result. These distinctions are explicit in the final paper README and in `sections/hko-ascent.tex`, `sections/hko-two-hinge-appendix.tex`, and `sections/affine-hko.tex`.

The fixed-body certificate does not settle the maximum along the one-parameter family, the optimum among bodies with a fixed pentagon factor and arbitrary asymmetric planar partner, or the global convex-body maximum. Discovery reconciliation preserves these as separate open questions; it also records that exact \(t=3/10\) producer scripts hardcode targets and counts and must be revalidated at a new parameter (`meta-topics/project-management/research-discovery-reconciliation-20261029.md`).
