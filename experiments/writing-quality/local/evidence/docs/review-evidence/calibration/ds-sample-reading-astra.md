## Predictions

1. Opening: “within the AI-assisted exploration” introduces an editorial
   category without explaining anything about the actual experiment. Removing
   that phrase would make the opening more direct. Likely minor reading/style
   objection; no mathematical consequence.
2. “The search sought values above 1” needs its research context. The thesis
   already contains a known body above 1. A reader may wonder why that is the
   search target and which family/new examples the experiment seeks. This may
   be supplied by the surrounding chapter, absent from this isolated sample;
   do not treat it as proof that the experiment lacks a worthwhile question.
3. The paragraph beginning “In the original samples” communicates the idea
   but gives no magnitude or concrete fresh-selection comparison. A reader
   cannot tell how useful selection was. One actual comparison would give the
   paragraph a result to carry forward. The sample intentionally omits the
   full historical methods, so this concern is narrower than demanding a
   complete methods chapter in two pages.
4. The separate-factor normalization paragraph is mathematically useful, but
   the map decomposition interrupts the paired experiment between its setup
   and result. A reader may prefer the normalization explained before the
   four-arm setup, or the derivation moved just after the paired result. This
   is a low-confidence placement prediction, not an established defect.
5. “These paired comparisons show why an average gain should not become a
   rule ...” restates what eleven improvements and five deteriorations already
   demonstrate. It may feel over-explanatory to the intended reader. A shorter
   sentence about the dependence on the starting body would suffice.

## Positive controls and limits

The sample defines its central ratio, sampling geometry and ridge descriptor
before using them. It explains why a rotation can change capacity and gives
a concrete counterexample to rotation beating fresh sampling. These are
specific improvements over the previously diagnosed style; they do not
establish comfortable human reading or PASS readiness. I predict less decoding
burden than in the diagnosed legacy examples, but this is not a measured
comparison and the overall text may still fail Jörn's standard.
