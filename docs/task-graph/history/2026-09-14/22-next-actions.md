## Resolve first; defer; do not do

Resolve before the main wave: exact consumer/input/schema, measured runner,
resource caps, and short experiment contracts. Partial readiness means a verified
subset plus exact blocker. Invalid or timed-out routes produce retained findings;
continue independent work without automatically opening broad repair.

Defer until active evidence: final wave breadth, polished figures, large certified
reevaluation, twenty-slot feasibility, and stop/continue judgment. The 91-row
alternative-source reevaluation needs a named evaluator-comparison question;
it is not automatically the best new experiment. Equal-opportunity tail analysis
is a bounded explanation repair, not a diverse search strategy.

count missing code/data as science; fill slots with correlated estimators on
unchanged columns; fan out solver jobs without measurements; infer certification
or global prevalence from the historical table; merge the rejected hostile-search
branch; or refactor generally without a named consumer.
