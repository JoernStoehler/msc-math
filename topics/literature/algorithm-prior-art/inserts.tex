Algorithms for polygonal Lagrangian products have also been developed through
Minkowski billiards. Krupp and Rudolf enumerate face choices for two- and
three-bounce trajectories and solve the resulting geometric problems using
linear programming~\cite[Section~7.3]{KruppRudolf2022}.
The reduction below works instead with the balanced facet weights in the
Haim--Kislev formula: it replaces the continuous weight search by pairs of
vertices of two closure polytopes.

% Variation chapter, after its opening account of the two calculations.
Leipold studies a related optimization problem for volume-preserving linear
images of a simplex~\cite[Sections~9.2, 11.3, and~12.3]{Leipold2026}.
There the balance weights are fixed, capacity evaluation reduces to a
linear-ordering integer program, and active permutation branches provide
first-order information for numerical search. Here the facets move
independently, so both the feasibility constraints and the optimizing weights
can change. We derive the corresponding derivatives in facet coordinates.
