# Develop And Integrate Figures

Use this reference when implementing or materially redesigning a figure after
its reader purpose is sufficiently clear. Also use
`model-figure-reader-effects.md` and `manage-publication-assets.md`.

Write a short task-local brief naming the intended reader inference,
relationships that must be perceptible, valid and invalid comparisons, likely
false inference, epistemic role, intended location and size, source truth, and
producer. Do not turn the brief into durable bureaucracy.

Inspect surrounding prose, existing producers, and final output constraints.
Make the cheapest rough render that tests the visual concept before polishing
it. Assert mathematical or data invariants needed to prevent an impossible or
misidentified display.

Keep captions and prose explanations out of the graphic unless text is a real
figure label. Use the caption to identify the takeaway and resolve material
false inferences about projection, coordinates, scaling, selection,
uncertainty, or status.

Integrate the candidate at its intended size and inspect the rendered thesis
page. Repair reader understanding before final polish, provenance, and
regeneration work.
