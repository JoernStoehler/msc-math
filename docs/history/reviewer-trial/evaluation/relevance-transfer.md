This matters because the paths carry the passage's main restriction on the exact pentagon example: they distinguish a population association from a rule for improving an individual body. **Minimal remedy:** identify the deformation in a short sentence and point to its parameter range and numerical changes, either here or in an explicitly identified supporting account. A small trajectory plot could answer the concrete question of how the two quantities change together, but is not necessary if the description and values suffice. **Context-dependent:** if an earlier supplied chapter already develops these paths, a precise reference may be enough. Do not strengthen the numerical observation into a certified counterexample.

## Explicitly acceptable passages

2. **The correlation explanation spends attention on a distinction this reader needs.** Location: “An empirical association,” paragraph following Figure 1: “the principal observation concerns which bodies have larger systolic ratio, rather than how much the systolic ratio changes per unit of ridge area.” The preceding explanation of rank correlation makes the reported $-0.94$ intelligible without assuming statistical terminology. The rounded numerical values serve that distinction; further decimal places would not help it.

3. **The transition to candidate selection makes the change of question explicit.** Location: “Selecting new products from the observed pattern,” opening paragraph: “Can the observed association still help select promising bodies, even when it does not prescribe a direction of deformation?” The requirement that candidates be chosen without knowing their capacities explains the experiment's purpose. Distinguishing refinement among low-$R$ bodies from testing a fixed rule against another generator prepares the reader for the two different comparisons. The closing sentence returns to the same distinction between selecting from a population and improving each body, so the section has a clear mathematical destination.
