label: translations `t_0=0,t_1,…,t_9` over the fixture's exact field, a labelled
counterclockwise hull cycle, and `0<lambda<1` satisfying

```
z_00 = (1-lambda)*z_11 + lambda*z_22,
z_ja = triangle_j.vertex_a + t_j.
```

All other defining hull/contact inequalities are strict. Thus one vertex of
triangle 0 lies inside an edge generated by triangles 1 and 2. The output
`cover-stratum-fixture.json` must contain all thirty positioned vertices, the
hull cycle, exact contact equality, strict sign checks and area. The two contact
equations have rank two in eighteen translation variables plus lambda, leaving

Hessian analysis of a nonexistent stratum.

## C. Exact width-preserving fitting obstruction

### Hypothetical first-return input

Assume the initial geometry reader returns an exact reconstruction of one
strict-gap polygon `K0={x:A_i x<=h_i}`, its reflected union normal fan, and one
candidate binding template `T*` among the ten normal triangles of the fixed
regular pentagon Q. The reconstruction is over a specified real algebraic
field with certified signs; the retained floating-point contacts alone do not
satisfy this input contract. Assume:

- The cyclic fan has paired unit normals `A_bar(i)=-A_i`; every inequality is
  facet-defining, consecutive intersections are nondegenerate, and all edge
  lengths are positive. This may require an interior point of a retained
  symmetrization path rather than a redundant endpoint description.
- With `s_i=h_T*(A_i)`, an exact primal placement `(u0,r0)` and dual `y` satisfy
  `A_i·u0+r0*s_i<=h_i`, `y>=0`, `A^T y=0`, `s·y=1`, and `h·y=r0>0`.
  Three contact rows have an invertible matrix with columns `(A_x,A_y,s)`;
  their dual entries are positive and every other primal slack is positive.
- The exact difference body supplies a ceiling `a=A2(K0,Q)` with `r0<a`.
  Other templates' exact minima are not yet assumed known. The first return
  therefore certifies an obstruction at scale a, not that this one template
  controls the full capacity in a neighborhood.

The consequential conditional question is whether an odd support deformation
can move this obstruction while preserving the complete width function, and
whether it actually changes the minimum of all ten fitting scales.

### Three conditional mathematical assignments

**Worker G — construct an admissible width-preserving deformation.** Own
`support-path.md` and `support-path-exact.json` under the mock output directory; execution scratch remains separate. Solve for a rational vector `d` satisfying
`d_i+d_bar(i)=0` and `y·d<0`. Remove pure translations when interpreting the
answer: they have `d_i=A_i·v` and hence `y·d=0`. Calculate the consecutive
vertices of `K_tau={x:A_i x<=h_i+tau*d_i}` from the 2×2 intersection matrices;
their coordinates and edge lengths are affine in tau. Give an exact rational
`eta>0` for which all edges remain positive, K_tau remains bounded, and the
three-contact placement obtained from the invertible contact matrix has every
inactive slack positive for `|tau|<=eta`. Include strict endpoint certificates,
not a plot or solver tolerance. On this interval the same fan is valid, and
`h_Ktau(A_i)+h_Ktau(-A_i)` is unchanged. Prove equality of the whole width
function by extending support linearly on each cone of the common fan of
K_tau and -K_tau; agreement at finitely many rays alone needs this fan argument.

**Exact failure output:** if no such d exists, prove that y annihilates the
odd-support subspace, or return the exact incompatible constraints if chamber
or contact inequalities were imposed. In the paired coordinates, nonzero
projection of y onto that subspace admits a rational strict descent direction;
zero projection blocks this selected dual's first-order mechanism. This does
not establish that all width-preserving deformations are capacity-invisible.

**Worker D — turn the dual into a geometric obstruction.** Own
`three-contact-obstruction.md`, using G's certified interval if it exists.
Derive, by multiplying containment inequalities by y and cancelling translation,

```
lambda <= sum_i y_i h_Ktau(A_i) = r0 + tau*(y·d).
```

Use G's feasible three-contact placement to prove equality for this template
throughout the interval. Explain the weighted three supporting-line distances
which block translating the scaled triangle at scale a; retain the three
directions and their closure weights explicitly. State the full hypotheses
under which this scalar obstruction applies. Calculate how the obstruction
changes at fixed widths and distinguish that information from the pre-existing
onset LP. Output an exact formula for `r_T*(K_tau)`, its strict-gap interval and
the placement/slack proof. If this explanation only restates the generic LP
dual and yields no interpretable extra criterion or separating family, mark
that scientific limitation explicitly; algebraic completion alone does not
establish a new mechanism.

**Reviewer C — independently certify the minimum of all ten templates.** Own
`capacity-completeness-review.md` and exact witnesses for all ten fits. Reconstruct
the templates from the source orientation/scale convention rather than silently
reusing contact labels as billiard orbits. At tau=0 obtain primal/dual equality
witnesses for each `r_j`, checking `r_T*=min_j r_j`. If every other scale is
strictly larger, choose an exact interior point of K0 and mix its feasible
zero-scale placement with each optimal placement to obtain a strictly feasible
scale `lambda_j>r0`. Certify every resulting slack. Use those positive margins
and the inequality `lambda_j>r0+tau*(y·d)` to produce a smaller exact interval on which
`r_j(K_tau)>r0+tau*(y·d)`; then the evaluator theorem gives
`c(K_tau×Q)=r0+tau*(y·d)`. The result is an exact capacity separation between
bodies with identical widths and hence identical A2. Independently check G's
fan/width proof and D's dual normalization. If another template ties or lies
below, report its exact witness. Derive a narrower interval or a piecewise
minimum only if the finite witness structure permits it; otherwise stop with
the unproved capacity-response obligation. A moving fit alone does not prove
moving capacity, and a strict-gap example alone repeats the known baseline.

## A concrete fan-in rather than three unbounded lanes

In this illustrative selection root selects the leads; they start A1, A2 and the exact-input reader
for C at 00:25. That is three bounded assignments, not three complete research
programmes. C first owes its exact primal/dual/fan input within a proposed
30-minute window. If it cannot reconstruct that input, the responsible lead records the exact
missing contract and parks C; B's realizability worker can use that released
slot for its one fixture. If C succeeds, G owns a proposed 30-minute support-path
window while C's independent reviewer reconstructs all ten base fits. D can
start its dual derivation from the fixed input independently of G, but cannot
claim the certified interval until G returns. Its proposed first window is
20 minutes; the all-ten review has a proposed 45-minute first return. These
are planning caps, not measured costs.

Leads read their first returns, own unanswered reviews and record the next bounded implication. The scientific comparator receives integrated reports and compares their consequences. Root stays in active bounded wait for decision-ready envelopes, then checks resource authorization and records continuation or redirection.
At a proposed 01:45 fan-in the hypothetical scientific decisions are concrete:
F11 accepted premises or one exact gap; C a genuine moving capacity family,
a dual-subspace obstruction, or an unresolved all-ten minimum; B, if opened,
a realizable contact fixture or an incompatibility. These are assumed scheduling
checkpoints, not promised mathematical results. Root then chooses one next
proof/repair branch from the returned mathematics. It does not automatically
expand all of them or consume the full time to 05:00.

For example, if C proves that the selected dual annihilates every odd support
variation, no further work is assigned to its proposed descent direction.
The cover lead opens B1/B2 only after B's exact fixture is independently checked; they
can work in parallel on constrained variation and direct area inequality.
If F11 instead reveals an endpoint scale gap, a gap-specific analytic reader
gets that displayed implication while finite replay remains a retained result;
it does not force dropping an independent cover inequality. If all selected
paths close with sound but narrow results and no compelling next lemma, the
night ends early with those results.

Before any promotion the scientific comparator owns a final mathematical synthesis separating
proved statements, certified examples, hypothetical lemmas and unresolved
quantifiers. It retains exact failed attempts and their mechanism so the next
owner need not rediscover them. Root updates coordination for materially changed
ownership/decisions and routes any thesis-impacting claim to its source owner
and dashboard reconciliation. No manuscript or dashboard promotion is automatic
from a worker PASS. A time cutoff hands off the remaining named implication;
it cannot turn an unfinished universal statement into a result.
