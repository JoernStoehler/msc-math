## Scientific autonomy and context

**Outcome:** Advance the owned scientific question with interpreted evidence,
a useful mathematical statement, or a concrete explanation of why the attempted
route fails. You own literature checks, choice of representation and population,
implementation, small experiments, interpretation and revision of your approach.
You are not an executor of a frozen feature list. The toolbox is a set of
candidates; discard or replace them when your reasoning supports that choice.

**Accepted choices:** Keep discovery broad: target-free and capacity-related,
generic bodies and structured populations, symplectic and ordinary geometry.
Computational speed alone is not a scientific payoff without a demonstrated
research need. Another narrow case needs a reason beyond being computable.
Exploration does not need a prewritten conjecture or a promised theorem.

Read the [evidence brief](observations-for-toolbox.md), relevant portions of the
[toolbox](discovery-toolbox.md), and the [current agenda](research-agenda.md).
They link retained observations and primary-source maps. Initial prompts carry
the user conversation; source packets contain the detailed evidence. The old
invariant-only scalar table is one historical interface, not the scope of this
programme. Do not reconstruct every historical campaign before doing useful work.

**Decision responsibility:** Before trusting a new measurement, establish what
object it measures, its actual invariance and scaling, dependencies or known
identities, and numerical meaning. Select examples that can expose those issues.
The data layout follows the scientific objects: matrices, fields, paths, dual
solutions and missing/not-applicable values are legitimate. Do not force every
body into one rectangular table of purported universal invariants.
**Reason:** Most cheap features are only linear-symplectic invariants; several
plausible spectra collapse to covariance; incomplete orbit payloads are not
action spectra. Definition and population choices change the mathematical claim.

Raw geometry and lineage must remain recoverable. When exchanging results,
include a stable body ID, measurement definition/version, units or normalization,
source population, numerical status and path to the actual structured result.
This is a minimal exchange contract, not a request to build a data platform.
Do not silently identify rounded-nearby geometries as equal.

## First research window and consequential boundaries

Start now with a bounded first window of roughly 45–60 minutes per owner; return
sooner on a consequential finding or blocker. Send an early compact message with
your actual chosen scientific direction and any changed premise, then continue
without waiting for coordinator permission on ordinary scoped work. This is not
a preliminary-report barrier before research can begin.

Derivations, scoped code, calibration and small interpreted collections are
authorized. Inspect a producer's cost and side effects before running it. The
standing pause on large historical rebuilds and expensive general-capacity
campaigns remains. Escalate to the coordinator before a run needing more than
about ten CPU-minutes or significant artifact retrieval/storage; use a smaller
informative case or derivation meanwhile. These are first-window resource
boundaries, not scientific restrictions on the eventual family.

Bounded specialist subagents are available when useful; give them an independent
question and nonoverlapping ownership. Keep the parent line responsible for
synthesis. Do not recreate the whole portfolio below each owner.

First-window return: the scientific question actually pursued; the meaningful
result or limitation; evidence/source/code paths and validation; what changed
your judgment; and the next bounded step with expected payoff and total follow-up
cost. Include failed cases and distinguish observation, conjecture, numerical
certificate and proof. A definition correction or explicit insufficiency result
can be a useful return; a feature inventory or producer log alone is not.

If an approach yields only known identities or source artifacts, revise it rather
than enlarge the same sample. A concrete candidate relation can justify adverse
examples or derivation immediately. A technical failure calls for a correction
or a different route. At the window limit, preserve partial evidence and report
the unresolved crux; do not silently extend into a large campaign.

## Coordination and review

There is no requirement that all lines finish before any can advance. Exchange
small examples, definitions and candidate statements directly, with evidence
paths; send material consequences to the coordinator. Discovery-method work can
start on existing evidence and known-identity controls, then consume new data
incrementally. Native representations remain with their scientific owners.

The coordinator reviews changed directions, cross-line deductions and promotion
of consequential claims. Independent checking is concentrated on such claims,
not repeated approval of every generator choice. Jörn is asked when scientific
taste controls substantial further work, not to recover facts or rank raw ideas.
Ownership persists after a window; background execution consists of actual
dispatched turns, not an implied perpetual scheduler.

## Why this orchestration

The observed risks are premature narrowing, stale evidence, forced scalar schemas
and research judgment being delegated as mechanical feature computation. The
intervention is a short shared evidence packet plus explicit scientific ownership
and evidence returns, activated through each owner's task message. It changes no
AGENTS.md or global agent configuration. The design follows the repository's
[harness skill](../../../.agents/skills/harness-engineering-v2/SKILL.md) in spirit;
the canonical file is `.agents/skills/harness-engineering-v2/SKILL.md` at checkout
root. [Anthropic's orchestrator/worker discussion](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)
is supporting design evidence, not a guarantee of Codex behavior. Whether this
works will be judged from the owners' actual choices and first evidence packets,
not from the existence of this charter or a valid graph.
