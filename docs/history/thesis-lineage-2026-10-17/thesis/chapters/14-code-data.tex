\section{Availability of Code and Data}\label{sec:published-code-data}

At the time of writing, the public development repository for this thesis is
\begin{center}
  \url{https://github.com/JoernStoehler/msc-math}.
\end{center}
The public source reference is commit
\href{https://github.com/JoernStoehler/msc-math/tree/1bec30637070effe7ab8b0a022ae62e83f6bbcab}{\texttt{1bec30637070}}. The HKO certificate and
pentagon-rotation packet described below are present there. The accompanying
working repository also contains this manuscript and the additional source
packets described below; the public commit does not contain all of them.
The repository URL follows ongoing development.

\begin{sloppypar}
The HKO local-maximality proof and the rotation computation have
proof-facing packets in the public tree. The HKO packet is at
\path{experiments/hko-local-maximum/theorem/} in that public commit and at
\path{topics/variation-and-local-maxima/hko-local-maximum/theorem/} in the
accompanying repository. It contains the finite witness, the
exact SageMath verifier, its recorded verification summary, and the Rust
programs used to select the witness.  Rust supplies finite choices; it is not
trusted for theorem correctness.  SageMath reconstructs the algebraic objects
and checks the exact predicates stated in
Section~\ref{subsec:hko-local-maximum-computation-with-sagemath}.  Those
predicates imply the theorem only together with the mathematical
upper-function and slice argument given there.
The annotated program printed in the appendix is maintained separately as
\path{thesis/appendices/hko_core.py}. It reconstructs the same selected
words, feasible sections, and exact derivative covectors; the appendix maps
its shorter presentation to the public verifier
\path{experiments/hko-local-maximum/theorem/verify.sage.py}
(\path{topics/variation-and-local-maxima/hko-local-maximum/theorem/verify.sage.py}
in the accompanying repository).

The second ten-facet local maximum is maintained in
\path{topics/variation-and-local-maxima/hko-local-maximum/non-hko/}. Its rational witness and
Python/SymPy verifier check the complete product calculation, the 24 feasible
upper sections, the rank and positivity predicates, and the exceptional
quadratic direction. An independent checker in the same packet recomputes the
geometry and derivatives by a different triangulation and direct section
solve. These exact checks do not replace the analytic neighborhood argument
or constitute a proof-assistant formalization.

\end{sloppypar}

The rotated-pentagon packet is stored under
\begin{center}
  \small\path{topics/products/regular-products/}\\[-0.2em]
  \path{pentagon-rotation-formula-proof/}.
\end{center}
It contains the exact SageMath classifier and the recorded full-run transcript
for all \(3340\) finite words left after the mathematical reduction.  This is a computational proof route; the analytic rotation and
independent-linear-deformation proofs presented here do not use it. In the
computational route, the enumeration theorem, the
translation from checked signs to action comparisons, and the continuity
argument remain mathematical inputs.  The repository exposes the finite
computation and its output for inspection, with SageMath's exact arithmetic
and root isolation inside the software trust boundary. The maintained
affine-pentagon argument and its source-convention check are in
\path{topics/products/pentagon-affine-products/}.

The centrally symmetric partner result in
Section~\ref{sec:pentagon-symmetric-partner} has its maintained analytic proof in
\begin{center}
  \small\path{topics/products/pentagon-arbitrary-partner/}\\[-0.2em]
  \path{symmetric-partner-lemma.md}.
\end{center}
The proof uses the stated normal-triangle criterion and geometric containment,
rather than the packet's floating-point fitting experiments.

The numerical audits in Section~\ref{sec:numerics} have distinct evidence owners.
The tracked report and producers for the \(249\) KKT systems and the complete
\(88\)- and \(1271\)-word comparisons are in
\path{topics/capacity-and-qp/development/tools/general_algorithm_ablation/}.
Its \path{RESULTS.md} records the counts and outcomes; its \path{run.sh}
produces verification and numerical-audit transcripts. The available evidence
consists of the summary report and the executable producer; per-system results
can be generated with that producer.

\begin{sloppypar}
The \(88\)-product audit is retained row by row in
\path{topics/capacity-and-qp/development/tools/product_closure_route/sample5.jsonl},
with its interpretation in that directory's \path{RESULTS.md}.
The external \texttt{polytope-datasets} artifact supplies
\path{random-product.jsonl} and \path{shared-cache.jsonl} for the separate
\(10\,240\)-product observation. These are registered files,
materialized under \path{topics/empirical-studies/polytope-datasets/}; they are not part
of a plain checkout. The product report identifies the
geometry-to-cache join and its scope. These numerical packets concern finite
tests, with the production-to-development comparison recorded separately in
\path{topics/capacity-and-qp/evidence/numerics-correspondence-check/}.
\end{sloppypar}

Other non-proof evidence is tracked as ordinary repository files. The
first-order experiment supplies its
\(12\)-run finite-step panel.  The bounded generated-candidate experiment
retains \(1675\) rows selected before the systolic ratio was computed, the
corresponding \(1675\) evaluated rows, its frozen configuration, and compact
reports.  These records support only the finite experiments described in the
corresponding chapters.  They neither prove a population statement nor supply
evidence for local maximality.

The artifact registry identifies external datasets and their intended local
paths; a plain checkout does not materialize those files. For example, the
\(14336\)-row random/product provenance table is tracked. The source
random/product datasets are supplied by the \texttt{polytope-datasets}
artifact, and the method-facing invariant table by the separate
\texttt{polytope-invariant-table} artifact. The source geometries match all
\(14336\) table rows. Geometry-derived features can be recomputed for all
bodies; the interval evaluator supplies capacity intervals for 14,335 of the
14,336 geometries, with one body outside the input-size policy. These intervals
do not certify the stored numerical ratios, whose volumes are floating-point
values. Input matching, feature comparison and capacity evaluation records are in
\path{topics/empirical-studies/retrospective-revalidation/}.
Consequently, cloning the repository alone does not provide every input needed
to repeat all empirical analyses, regenerate every figure, or reproduce every
reported run.  Packet-local instructions describe available producers and
checks, but compatible software, dependencies, compute resources, and
untracked inputs may still be required; timing and other
environment-dependent fields need not reproduce byte for byte.

The public commit identified above fixes the available public source version.
Reproduction of results supported by additional packets requires the accompanying
working repository and the external inputs identified in its artifact registry.
There is no frozen thesis release or thesis DOI.
