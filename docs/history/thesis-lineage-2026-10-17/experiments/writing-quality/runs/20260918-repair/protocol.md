# Small supplied-objection repair probe

Purpose: separate repair capability from the detector. Two historical objection-bearing units; two deliberately distinct author operations; unchanged baseline remains available to evaluator. This is an engineering probe, not a statistically powered comparison or human validation.

Selected units: exact text extracted from frozen c001 (HKO subsection) and c002 (complete DS two-page sample). HKO PDF extraction contains equation-layout artifacts; authors supply only prose slots so no mathematical typesetting interpretation is needed. DS author may transcribe formulas into Markdown; meaning review must catch changes.

Authors receive original text plus explicit historical human repair criteria. They do not receive other drafts, later repaired text, or blanket claims that the source is unacceptable. HKO whole chapter historically passed despite local objections. DS original sentence-level prose was comfortable but structure failed.

A: narrow edit preserving source order/wording where possible. B: reconstruct from reader questions/mathematical dependencies. Both same input and requested factual preservation. Fresh fork_turns=none agent contexts; same inherited model. These operations differ in editable scope and planning, not just prohibition strength. Only one sample per condition.

Review: anonymized candidate IDs with operations and author ledgers removed; evaluator sees source and explicit target criteria, not author identities or historical overall verdicts. It must retain baseline if new drafts introduce material damage, report criterion-specific evidence, and distinguish new agent assessment from human acceptance. Author prose cannot be made completely blind because original source is available for factual comparison.

Artifacts are experimental only; no thesis sources or active guidance changed. Evaluation compares supplied-objection repair, not end-to-end readiness detection, and no new draft receives a human PASS by this process.
