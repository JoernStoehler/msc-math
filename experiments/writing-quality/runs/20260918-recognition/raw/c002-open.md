4. **Anchored issue ledger.**

- **Quote:** “Tests on fresh candidates showed that low-ridge selection could raise the ratios relative to controls.” **Reader consequence:** The reader can understand the proposed selection method but cannot judge how substantial or consistent its reported success was: the controls, sample size, and size of the improvement are unspecified. The following “That success” gives this compressed result a pivotal narrative role, whereas the later experiments provide concrete counts and outcomes. **Severity:** Minor in this sample; more consequential if this is the sole presentation of that result. **Uncertainty:** Moderate, because a two-page account can reasonably summarize evidence documented elsewhere, but no such reference is supplied here.

- **Quote:** “Rotating a Lagrangian product can therefore change its capacity; the rotated body need no longer be a Lagrangian product for the fixed symplectic form.” **Reader consequence:** “Lagrangian product” appears without explicitly connecting the term to the previously defined product in the q- and p-coordinate planes. A graduate reader unfamiliar with that terminology may briefly lose the connection to the examples under discussion, despite the otherwise helpful explanation of symplectic alignment. **Severity:** Minor. **Uncertainty:** Low that the term is newly introduced here; moderate about the difficulty it would cause a particular reader.
