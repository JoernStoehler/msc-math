\section*{Disclosure Of AI Use}
\phantomsection
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Disclosure Of AI Use}

Generative artificial intelligence systems were used materially in the research
and production of this thesis.  The documented workflows used Codex and Claude
Code agents.  Agents helped organize work; wrote or edited Rust, Python, and
Sage code; supported experiments and data-science analyses; assisted
mathematical proof development and proof-risk review; searched and inspected
literature; and drafted or revised thesis prose.  Their role was therefore not
limited to proofreading or language correction.

In his interactions with the agents, Jörn Stöhler specified goals, constraints,
priorities, and success criteria; posed mathematical and technical questions;
corrected errors, proof gaps, notation, and misleading framing; rejected
inadequate proposals; and approved selected next actions.  Agent output was not
accepted merely because it had been generated or reported as complete.  As the
submitting author, I take responsibility for the mathematical and factual claims
retained in the final thesis.

Different tasks used different checks, including tests and regressions, exact
certificates and executable verifiers, source inspection, agent-assisted
mathematical and proof-risk reviews, and reviews of prose and AI workflows.
These checks are not interchangeable and were not applied uniformly to every
claim or artifact.

The interaction-provenance claims in this disclosure are based on an
LLM-authored aggregate synthesis of sampled Codex and Claude interaction
records.  The underlying non-public logs are not reproduced here.  They do not
exhaust offline work or settle the first origin of every idea.  Interaction
provenance does not by itself establish acceptance or mathematical correctness.
