## Revised candidate

For each fixed facet word $\sigma$, the feasible set $M_\sigma$ is independent
of the rotation angle. Indeed, closure separates into equations in the two
factors, and the rotation in the second equation can be inverted. Thus every
$\beta\in M_\sigma$ is admissible at every angle. Its quadratic objective has
the form $Q_{\sigma,\beta}(\theta)=A_{\sigma,\beta}\cos\theta+B_{\sigma,\beta}\sin\theta$.
On $[-\pi/10,\pi/10]$, this objective is a nonnegative linear combination of
its endpoint values. The known endpoint capacities determine the endpoint
maxima of the quadratic objective and hence give a bound for every
$\beta\in M_\sigma$ throughout the interval. One explicitly given word and
weight vector attain the bound, proving the rotation formula without
enumerating the words.

Integration review must check where M_sigma, the normalization of Q and its
reciprocal relation to capacity have been introduced, whether the paragraph
belongs before or after the theorem, and whether it duplicates the detailed
proof. This local correction does not authorize a wholesale notation change.
