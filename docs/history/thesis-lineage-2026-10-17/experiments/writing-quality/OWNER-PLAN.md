## Why the steps cannot be skipped

1. Recover existing work: otherwise another experiment repeats completed trials
   or treats known human feedback as missing.
2. Preserve version-specific human decisions: otherwise approved material gets
   rewritten or old rejection gets transferred to new prose without evidence.
3. Check a current hard passage against Jörn: source audits and agent preferences
   have already disagreed with him; another AI PASS cannot close that gap.
4. Repair only what the observed objection warrants, then test different natural
   material before any claim of transfer. Passing a supplied-feedback repair is
   weaker than improving a new passage without another detailed human diagnosis.
5. Integrate chosen edits with all claims, figures, labels and caveats intact,
   build and inspect changed/adjacent pages, then obtain exact-PDF acceptance.
   Local labels and builds cannot replace that assembled-artifact decision.
