## Closed retained-table surface

The current random/product table has trusted-row, target-scan, EDA,
association, grouped prediction, linear/shrinkage, random-forest,
gradient-boosting, high-tail classification, shallow-rule, feature-ablation,
projection, clustering, and anomaly-diagnostic packets. The maintained
family-by-family account is
`../../methods/trusted-random-product-method-dispositions.md`.

`../../methods/standard-baseline-p2/` closed the previously named
lasso/elastic-net, boosting, classification, and feature-ablation gap. Absence
of another textbook estimator is not itself a research gap. A new in-table
method needs a named claim or interface that the current packets cannot answer.

## Remaining scientific interfaces

The current evidence leaves these materially different questions open:

- **Producer support:** whether another natural distribution or controlled
  geometric operation places useful mass nearer the known positive regime.
  This needs a new producer contract, not another model on the same rows.
- **Representation:** whether invariant geometry or orbit features beyond the
  current 27 combinatorial and 18 symplectic ridge-area summaries distinguish
  equality/positive examples from the random tail.
- **Generated-candidate proposal:** whether a rule chosen before `sys`
  evaluation improves threshold-relevant candidates on an independent source.
  Retained-table prediction does not answer this.
- **Rarity:** whether any stated producer admits a defensible calibrated
  prevalence or tail account for `sys > 1`. Current zero-hit data and unstable
  extrapolations do not.
- **Search algorithms:** whether a constructive, local, adaptive, or
  population search beats a declared baseline at equal cost. Finite
  improvement and endpoint/local-maximality are separate questions.
