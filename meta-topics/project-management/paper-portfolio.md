# Three candidate article arcs after the 22 October sprint

This index records retained outcomes and their limits. It is a paper-structure proposal, not a publication or novelty claim. The sprint did not establish journal acceptance, human proof acceptance, or submission readiness. The three arcs answer different mathematical questions; no result below depends on completing only one claim.

| Candidate article | Established claim/evidence | Main unresolved dependency | Proof and evidence paths intended for retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Difference-body formulas for EHZ capacities of polygon products** | Exact rotation profile for all odd regular pairs; independent-affine capacity formulas for 5×5, 5×7, 7×7, and 5×9.
