Haim--Kislev and Ostrover disproved Viterbo's conjecture with a Lagrangian
product of two regular pentagons. We investigate the geometry near their
example and use finite computation to look for further structure in the
systolic ratio of four-dimensional convex polytopes.

Our main theorem shows that the HKO body is a local maximizer among
polytopes with exactly ten facets. Nearby equality occurs precisely under
translations, positive dilations and linear symplectic transformations.

We implement, optimize and extend two capacity algorithms, based on the
quadratic program of Haim--Kislev and the flow graph of Chaidez and Hutchings.
We also study the first-order variation of capacity. As a side theorem,
we prove an explicit capacity formula for the relative-rotation family of
two regular pentagons. A short analytic proof shows that the HKO body is a global maximizer
of the systolic ratio in this family. We then extend the capacity formula
and sharp bound to products of two independently linearly deformed regular
pentagons.

An empirical investigation looks for statistical patterns in geometric
measurements of sampled polytopes, with the aim of turning those patterns
into mathematical conjectures. We find patterns among bodies with systolic
ratio below one, but do not establish that these patterns extend to the regime
above one. This leaves open whether further progress requires different
statistical methods or new geometrically motivated measurements.
