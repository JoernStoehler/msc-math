## Current overall state

The named random and random-product populations, ordinary retained-table
methods, and frozen generated-candidate selectors have found no new source of
`sys > 1`. They do contain reproducible sub-threshold structure and useful
negative/mixed method results. The repository does not support a calibrated
global rarity estimate, a threshold-directed proposer, or an optimizer
endpoint/local-maximality claim.

This is the distinction behind the Jörn-accepted working wording in
[`../../../docs/project-facts.md`](../../../docs/project-facts.md), item 34.2:
the closed named method table found neither a new positive source nor a
candidate-proposer for finding one beyond the already explained positive
family and its controlled relatives.

## Important open questions

These are question families, not approved experiments:

1. Which natural source distribution or controlled geometric operation puts
   useful support nearer the known positive regime than the current
   generators?
2. Which invariant, orbit, or geometric representation distinguishes known
   equality/positive examples from superficially similar random-tail rows?
3. How do capacity, volume, and branch switching separately contribute to
   observed improvements, and does that suggest a controlled operation?
4. Does facet count or combinatorial complexity change attainable local
   maxima after separating generator and compute-cost effects?
5. At equal verified `sys`-evaluation cost, does a robust local, adaptive, or
   population method beat IID/local-random baselines in best value, diversity,
   or useful trajectories?
6. Can an optimizer produce endpoints that pass a declared stationarity
   diagnostic, rather than only make finite progress?
7. Which one or two reader-facing tables, figures, or demonstrations should
   carry the bounded negative account, and what additional data do they
   require?
8. Can a clean checkout reproduce the selected producer, method result, and
   thesis display from tracked inputs and documented commands?

The first six are scientific interfaces summarized in
[the method-surface map](topics/method-surface-expansion.md) and the relevant
packet READMEs. The last two are thesis-closure/readiness questions, not
automatic requests for more exploration.

## FACTSHEET 34.1: unresolved consumer choice

Three materially different roles could make “more data” operational:

1. **Bounded negative account:** an additional named producer panel used to
   strengthen the scope and readability of the finite negative benchmark.
2. **Method demonstration:** a fresh target-blind selector/proposer comparison
   used to demonstrate what the data-science method buys, without implying a
   positive-search guarantee.
3. **Search-method comparison:** an equal-cost IID versus local/adaptive
   comparison used to support a claim about search efficiency and trajectory
   behavior.

These are consumer options, not approved data plans. Dataset choice, size,
producer command, review gate, and stopping rule should be selected only after
Jörn chooses the reader-facing role. A reproducibility audit can check existing
commands in parallel, but should not silently make this scientific/thesis
choice.
