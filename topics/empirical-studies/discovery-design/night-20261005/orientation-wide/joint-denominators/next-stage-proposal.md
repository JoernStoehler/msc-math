## Possible shorter successful sign route; assess before pricing

The comparator proposes square-free multiplicity decomposition plus Sturm
counts over ordered F rather than adjoining general quartic roots. For a
quartic required nonnegative on an interval, an interior root of odd
multiplicity forces a sign change; absent odd roots, one exact positive
sample plus boundary signs certifies nonnegativity. A successful nonzero
quartic's interior zeros have even multiplicity, and its repeated irreducible
factors have degree at most two. Their equality bodies can therefore use the
existing quadratic adapter. An identically zero quartic is separate. A
failure can be exhibited by refining an odd-root isolation until a rational
negative probe is found. This substantially simplifies successful equality
fields, and may make a bounded full-family proof more practical than the
method-only allocation above.
