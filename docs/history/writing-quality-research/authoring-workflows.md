# Evidence on AI scientific-writing workflows

Read-only literature investigation, 2026-09-18. No thesis work resumed. Primary sources inspected; empirical claims below are narrower than their authors' promotional conclusions. No study found establishes PASS-quality mathematical thesis exposition with current Codex models.

## Evidence matrix

| Source / task | Design and outcome | What transfers; what does not |
|---|---|---|
| [Self-Refine, NeurIPS 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.17651v2) — seven tasks, GPT-3.5/ChatGPT/GPT-4 | Same-model generation, specific feedback, revision. Blind author-judge evaluation on 150 examples/task preferred refinement over direct output: sentiment 75% vs 21.43%, acronyms 44.59% vs 12.16%, dialogue 47.58% vs 19.66%, remainder ties. Specific-versus-generic feedback ablations improved task scores: sentiment 43.2 vs 31.2, acronyms 56.4 vs 54.0; code 27.5 vs 26.0. Math gains were negligible without reliable error identification. | Actual evidence that generation defects need not be critique blindspots, and specificity can add value beyond reminders. But short subjective tasks; judges were paper authors, much evaluation automated; not scientific exposition. Localization and actionable instructions were bundled, so no isolated estimate for highlighting alone. |
| [ARIES, 2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.12587v1) — scientific revisions from human reviews | Human papers/reviews/edits, manual subset 42 papers/196 comments/131 edits; synthetic alignments 1,678 papers/3,892 comments/3,184 edits. GPT-4 generated plausible revisions but followed literal requests too closely and supplied fewer technical details. Highly compliant edits: GPT-4 94%, humans 68%. Comment-edit alignment substantially below humans; indirect comments harder. | Directly relevant failure mechanism: satisfying feedback wording differs from repairing its underlying issue. The dataset supports testing repair and interpretation separately. Old GPT-4, review-addressing rather than prose PASS; real authors sometimes appropriately reject feedback. |
| [ReviseBench, Findings ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.887.pdf), [code/data](https://github.com/CGCL-codes/ReviseBench) — full paper revision plus experiments | Twelve accepted ICLR 2025 papers; original source, human review, code/data; human camera-ready reference. GPT-5, DeepSeek-V3.2, Claude-4-Sonnet, Gemini-2.5-Pro, o3/o4-mini in OpenHands. Best win rate against human revision 8.33%, primarily Gemini-3-Pro judged; human audit agreed on 20 sampled comparisons. Failures included superficial additions, incoherent insertions, rebuttal language left in manuscript, layout failures, missing experiments and sometimes fabrication. | Current-ish direct negative evidence against assuming human feedback plus generic agent scaffold solves revision. Small, highly selected set; includes expensive experiments and infrastructure, so not an estimate of pure writing ability or model upper bounds. |
| [PaperOrchestra, April 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.05018v1) — full AI paper synthesis | 200 accepted CVPR/ICLR papers reverse-engineered into inputs. Gemini-3.1-Pro backbone; outline, literature, drafting, refinement. Eleven researchers, 40 papers, 180 paired judgments: reported overall-quality win margins 14–38 points against AI baselines. Refinement ablation positive but LLM-only. Literature human–judge Pearson correlations just .087/.276, versus .567/.646 for overall quality. Authors observed judges reward rigid formatting more than humans. | Closest positive workflow evidence. Pipeline versus single-call baseline conflates compute/tools/decomposition; reconstructed inputs and possible contamination; not mathematics. Critical Goodhart admission: refinement gained scores by listing missing baselines as limitations; authors prohibited explicit limitations. Do NOT copy that rule. Human-relative superiority is not absolute acceptance. |
| [LiRA, 2025 preprint / AAAI 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.05138v1) — literature reviews | GPT-4o-mini pipelines; research-before-writing, structured drafting/refinement; explored different-model reviewer Gemma3:4b. Human evaluation: three subject experts compare 30 snippets to human writing; another team scores 15 snippets against baselines. Main evaluations cover structure, coverage, relevance, and citation support. | Useful implemented workflow and prompts; evidence scope is snippets of review articles, with small industry-internal human samples and much automated assessment. Different-model reviewer is an intervention to test, not proof of independent errors. |
| [Re3, EMNLP 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.06774), [DOC, ACL 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10077) — fiction over 2,000 words | Re3 combines structured plan, repeated context injection, continuation ranking and consistency editing: human judgments improved coherent plot +14 points and premise relevance +20 versus same-base direct generation. DOC's hierarchical detailed outline/control further improves over Re3: coherence +22.5, outline relevance +28.2, interestingness +20.7 points. | Evidence that changing composition architecture can improve global coherence beyond admonitions. Older models, fiction and bundled interventions; no direct mathematical-exposition effect size. |
| [Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet, ICLR 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798) | Intrinsic self-correction without outside feedback often fails and can degrade reasoning performance. Authors distinguish self-correction from settings containing external correctness information. | Relevant caution for using self-review to certify mathematics. Not a writing experiment; cannot generalize its negative result to all stylistic refinement. |

## Direct answers to the hypotheses

- **Keywords versus explanation:** No clean scientific-writing study found that isolates a compact issue label versus a full explanation with equal contextualization. Self-Refine offers adjacent evidence that specific actionable feedback beats generic feedback, but does not identify whether the gain comes from localization, prescription, examples, or additional tokens.
- **Bad habits versus blindspots:** They are not equivalent: Self-Refine demonstrates useful same-model critique/refinement on some tasks. ARIES and ReviseBench show why that fact is insufficient for scientific revision. Capability must be assessed by operation and text type.
- **More agents:** Positive studies typically bundle roles, tools, computation, retrieval, and revision. They do not establish an agent-count effect. Independent contexts are not independent error distributions.
- **Local improvements versus overall acceptance:** PaperOrchestra explicitly observes both judge–human disagreement and score exploitation. Its refinement-only result is not human validation of the optimized criterion.
- **New direct abstract-editing evidence:** Sibling detector agent covers [Process-Oriented Evaluation, June 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.15583v1). Read its computational-metric caveat before treating rubric gains as prose quality.

## Cheap experiments justified by this evidence (proposals, not executed)

1. Hold draft and identified issue fixed; compare issue label, exact highlighted span plus label, and span plus explanation/action. Equalize revision budget. This directly probes whether detailed feedback is worth Jörn's verbalization cost.
2. Separate critique accuracy from revision success: supply a known human objection to the editor, then assess whether it repairs the underlying problem without simply echoing the objection.
3. Compare local repair against content-first reconstruction on a structural failure, with the same facts and compute. Existing studies motivate both; none chooses the winner here.
4. Preserve the original and allow no-change as a candidate. Blindly compare original/revision, check mathematical preservation, and reject self-awarded score gains unsupported by actual text differences.
5. Test held-out problems of the same type before expanding to another chapter type. Different-domain transfer is a second experiment, not a consequence of local success.

These sources can supply prompts, examples, failure taxonomies and baselines immediately. They do not remove the need for a small task-local evaluation against Jörn's actual reading.
