## 1. What blocks a pentagon triangle at the width ceiling?

**Object and present evidence.** Fix the regular pentagon Q. For arbitrary planar
convex K the ten normal-triangle fitting scales determine `c(K×Q)`; `A2` is the
two-bounce ceiling and equals the capacity after central symmetrization. The
[five retained strict-gap paths](../../products/pentagon-arbitrary-partner/first-window.md)
already have `c<A2`, a computed saturation-onset LP, and primal/dual contact data.
The disk-cloud hull starts at `c/A2≈0.910` and saturates at `t≈0.144`; the
translated-template hull starts closer to the ceiling (`≈0.937`) but saturates
later (`≈0.238`). Thus initial deficit and difference-body area excess do not
order these onsets. Recomputing those numbers is not the proposed return.

**Planning bet and reason.** My working expectation is that one can explain this
reversal by a particular directional contact imbalance, probably only on a
restricted normal/contact chamber. A universal scalar criterion is a weaker
bet: the response is a minimum of ten piecewise-linear fitting scales, and
the binding triangle or dual can change. This is a structural expectation,
not a calibrated probability of obtaining a theorem. The contact data must
first show which obstruction is actually active; an apex-versus-diameter story
is a candidate explanation, not an observed fact in those two bodies.

**What different returns would mean.** If an exact width-preserving support
perturbation changes the binding triangle's inability to fit at `A2`, the return
is a separating family for that fitting response. Separating capacity itself
also requires checking that the minimum over all ten fitting scales changes;
removing one bottleneck may leave another. Identical width data need not fix
capacity, as the current strict-gap/symmetrization paths already establish.
The new scientific content must therefore be the explicit
directional cause, its sign and its onset, not another collision. If a short
support inequality outside the optimization language exactly characterizes
failure for the two contrasts and a specified contact chamber, it gives a
restricted geometric criterion and explains why scalar area asymmetry misses
the ordering. It becomes a global criterion only after all binding templates,
contact changes and degenerate chambers have been controlled.

There is also a useful adverse outcome: two exact bodies could agree on the
proposed directional summary but have different saturation onsets. That would
falsify that summary's sufficiency, even if its correlation looked good, and
point to a missing contact direction or template switch. Conversely, if each
adversarial perturbation is blocked by the same positively weighted closed
triple of support constraints, the useful return is a proposed lemma: **within
the stated normal chamber, that closed triple forces the same fitting threshold
for every admissible width-preserving perturbation**. The universal quantifier
over perturbations still needs proof. Several failed perturbations without
this constraint derivation do not strengthen it.

**Observation that decides the branch.** Reconstruct one binding dual for each
retained contrast, evaluate its support deficit at `A2`, and solve the linear
compatibility conditions for a perturbation preserving the relevant widths.
A dual yielding only the existing onset LP is preparation. A geometric
inequality with an explicit chamber and an exact perturbation or impossibility
witness earns continuation. An inconsistent perturbation ansatz eliminates
that ansatz; it does not prove that no other width-preserving path exists.

**Next step, stop and retained learning.** The proposed 30-minute comparison
buys one explained contrast and one perturbation constraint check, assuming
the retained contacts can be reconstructed. Stop when it merely relabels the
LP, or when a precise missing input blocks that reconstruction. A newly named
criterion would justify exact reconstruction and one independently designed
adverse family before any collection expansion; a full classification is a
separate resource decision. Retain which proposed directional summary failed,
the exact constraint responsible, and whether failure came from a template
switch. That information changes the measurements worth acquiring; more scalar
samples alone would not.

## 2. HKO global ambition; arbitrary 5+5 as a restricted target

**Target and baseline.** Put `B=sys(HKO)=(3+sqrt(5))/5`, with
`sys(C)=c_EHZ(C)^2/(2 vol_4(C))`. The broad ambition is `sys(C)<=B` for every
convex body in symplectic `R^4`. The concrete restricted target is the same
inequality for every Lagrangian product of two planar pentagons. This does not
cover all ten-facet polytopes, and differs from card 3's fixed regular factor
with unrestricted partner side count. Triangle/quadrilateral products and all
pairs of affinely regular pentagons already have bounds in the inspected record.
The [affine-pentagon owner](../../products/pentagon-affine-products/README.md)
and [non-HKO owner](../../variation-and-local-maxima/hko-local-maximum/non-hko/README.md)
record these baselines. The residual target needs a genuinely non-affinely-regular factor. HKO and the
ratio-one hexagon–quadrilateral `P*` already have local ten-facet packets; `P*`
is a different local mechanism, not equality at the larger HKO benchmark.

**Directional planning bet.** I expect the first thirty-minute comparison to
identify a precise ingredient that cannot yet be transferred globally more
readily than to deliver a usable universal inequality. The basis is the local
proofs' selected sections, neighborhood estimates and symmetry quotients, versus
an uncovered arbitrary-pentagon class with no supplied global reduction. This
is a qualitative planning judgment, not a calibrated probability. It favors
returning a failed implication and its replacement lemma over descriptive
certificate comparison. Theorem, counterexample and classification probabilities
are unknown; high scientific value does not establish feasibility.

**Concrete contingent outcomes.**

- A sharp inequality for **all** arbitrary pentagon pairs makes HKO a global
  maximizer in that product class and enlarges the affine theorem. Equality
  needs its own proof; general ten-facet and convex-four-body globality remain
  unresolved. A bound for a meaningful proper subclass is useful restricted
  progress: name the exact hypothesis blocking extension.
- A certified body with `sys>B` refutes the benchmark in every containing class.
  An arbitrary convex four-body refutes broad globality; a genuine ten-facet
  body additionally refutes ten-facet globality; a genuine 5+5 product also
  refutes this residual product conjecture. A witness outside either narrower
  class leaves that narrower statement untouched.
- A new body at `sys=B` outside the claimed equivalence family refutes that
  uniqueness-at-the-benchmark assertion while potentially preserving the
  numerical bound. Without the global inequality it does not classify all
  maximizers. Check whether the new parameter is symmetry, degeneracy or a
  distinct geometric mechanism.
- An accepted new local maximum, even below `B`, adds mathematical information
  about other maxima. Its perturbation class and genuine equality directions
  must be stated. A numerical stationary point is only a candidate, and a
  certificate on one chart does not automatically cover a uniform neighborhood.
- Repeated failures of improving deformations, explained by the same checked
  constraint, suggest a proposed universal obstruction lemma. They do not prove
  it applies to every pentagon pair or every improvement mechanism. Finding no example
  above `B`, without such an explanation, is weak negative search evidence.

**Discriminating observation and implication.** Name one local ingredient one
hopes to reuse: section feasibility, an equality direction or a sign estimate.
Separate its validity outside the neighborhood from the inequality it would
need to imply. A concrete pair where it fails falsifies that *transfer*, not
HKO globality; several surviving examples do not establish universality. A
possible sufficient lemma would assert that every pentagon pair admits a
rigorously feasible upper-bound section with action at most
`sqrt(2 B area(P) area(Q))`. This is an explicit proposed target, not a derived
lemma. Any obstruction suggested by repeated search failure must likewise
retain its universal quantifier and the unproved bridge to the ratio bound.

A claimed higher-ratio witness needs rigorous capacity lower bounds and volume
upper bounds yielding a strict gap, plus the capacity method's completeness
contract and class/facet checks. An action upper bound cannot certify capacity
exceeding the benchmark. A floating near-tie is not an equality family.

**Useful partial/failure.** An exact counterexample to an intermediate inequality
cancels that route and can identify a missing shape variable. A theorem that a
named deformation cannot improve the ratio throughout a stated class could
be valuable without classifying maxima; observed unsuccessful iterations are
weaker. A precise obstruction to continuing local cancellation globally is
useful; paraphrasing known certificates is preparation. Failure to find a
counterexample does not reverse into a proof.

**Next action and stop/escalation.** The first probe owes one checked failed
local-to-global implication or one global inequality with its exact missing
lemma, assuming readable retained proofs and geometry. Stop with that output
or its concrete missing input. Continue only when it identifies a bounded
argument or construction that can change the conclusion. Escalate a specific
universal lemma with failed approaches and adversarial examples, rather than
“prove global maximality.” A new optimizer programme needs its own value and
resource case; cheap evaluations do not establish cheap scientific progress.

**Process lesson.** Existing certificates locate where reasoning stops. Keep
restricted payoff separate from broad ambition. An informative failure earns
continuation through its proposed lemma or separating mechanism, not the count
of failed runs; the remaining universal quantifier stays visible.

## 3. Fixed regular P5, arbitrary K; ten-triangle cover/area inequality

**Target and baseline.** Fix the regular pentagon `Q`, its ten normal
unit-perimeter triangles `T_j`, and the capacity-one HKO partner `H` of area
`m`. The target is: every planar convex body containing an independent translate
of each `T_j` has area at least `m`. The [documented covering criterion](../../products/pentagon-arbitrary-partner/README.md) identifies
these containments with `c(K×Q)>=1`. Dividing a general partner by its capacity
normalizes it to capacity one and divides its area by capacity squared. Thus
this area inequality proves `sys(K×Q)<=B` for all partners of the **fixed regular
factor**, with equality requiring separate classification. It does not settle
arbitrary 5+5, nonproduct ten-facet bodies or arbitrary convex four-bodies.

A cover can be replaced by the hull of ten translated templates, at most thirty
vertices, without increasing area. This known finite-dimensional reduction
leaves a continuous optimization problem. [Local rigidity](../../products/pentagon-arbitrary-partner/local-cover-rigidity.md) excludes a neighborhood
of HKO placements subject to its supplied continuum proof, not remote covers.
The [smooth-cell argument](../../products/pentagon-arbitrary-partner/smooth-cell-reduction.md) moves an interior minimum to a contact boundary but
cannot automatically iterate on coupled boundary strata.

**Directional planning bet.** I expect the first forty-five-minute probe to
resolve realizability or expose coupled variables on one non-HKO stratum more
readily than to produce global contact completeness. The basis is reusable
open-cell coordinates and a specified synthetic contact, against the source's
explicit nonlinear-boundary limitation. This is an uncalibrated planning bet;
probabilities of a global theorem or competing cover remain unknown. Compare
a direct area inequality on the same stratum: classification is a means, not
an obligatory payoff.

**Concrete contingent outcomes.**

- A universal area lower bound with the covering bridge checked settles fixed-
  regular-factor HKO global maximality. A complete contact reduction achieves
  the same only when all resulting continuous strata, degeneracies and artificial
  anchoring boundaries are handled. Finite labels alone are not the theorem.
- A rigorously feasible cover with `area(K)<m` gives `c(K×Q)>=1`, hence
  `sys(K×Q)>B`: it refutes fixed-factor optimality and broad convex-four-body
  HKO globality. It additionally refutes 5+5/ten-facet optimality only when K
  is a genuine pentagon so the product belongs to those classes. Count actual
  sides rather than generator points or the thirty-point hull bound.
- A distinct feasible cover of area `m`, outside the claimed HKO equality
  copies, gives `sys>=B`. If the numerical global bound remains true, capacity
  is forced to one and this refutes a translation/dilation-only equality
  classification while preserving that bound. Otherwise its capacity could
  exceed one and it could instead be a stronger counterexample. Area plus
  all ten containments alone must not silently be labeled exact ratio equality.
- A feasible constant-area escape for a named contact stratum removes a family
  of candidates. A rigid/stationary non-HKO boundary placement defeats that
  simple reduction strategy, but stationarity is not local minimality and local
  minimality is not a lower-than-HKO competitor. Compare its area with `m`.
- An unrealizable synthetic pattern rules out that case and may reveal a broader
  geometric constraint. It does not exclude every boundary competitor.
  No low-area cover found is weak search evidence; repeated, explainable
  obstructions can motivate a universal lemma, whose coverage remains unproved.

**Discriminating observation and implication.** First exhibit translations
satisfying the proposed vertex-on-edge contact and all claimed strict hull
inequalities. Without realizability there is no stratum to minimize. Remove
common translation, write the remaining constraints and exact shoelace area,
and check a feasible escape or direct inequality. Restricting to coupled contact
constraints can introduce curvature after eliminating variables, so the
open-cell zero-diagonal-Hessian argument does not automatically persist.
A checked failure refutes that reduction, not the desired global inequality.

On the same stratum, an area estimate may avoid further enumeration; a cover
below `m` is more decisive than a taxonomy. Exact containments already supply
a capacity **lower** bound, making strict low area a direct counterexample.
Repeated obstructions are useful when they lead to a statement “for every
feasible configuration, ...” with a named missing proof, rather than an implied
conclusion from sample frequency.

**Useful partial/failure.** A complete inequality on one substantial stratum is
restricted progress and a control. An impossibility proof can simplify later
coverage. An exact compactification or escape obstruction prevents an invalid
exhaustive programme. A competitor above `m` can distinguish local mechanisms
without refuting the bound. Contact labels with uncontrolled continuous variables
move the main uncertainty rather than solve it; one unsuccessful inequality
attempt does not exclude other inequality proofs.

**Next action and stop/escalation.** Return one realizability contract with
remaining variables, a checked reduction/inequality, or an exact incompatibility
within the inherited cap, assuming reusable cell coordinates. Stop before
exhaustive enumeration unless the result supplies a completeness route and
payoff. Continue on one concrete unresolved implication controlling that stratum;
escalate it with coordinates, feasible domain, competing inequality and candidate
falsifier. Absence of an example does not justify unlimited search. Full proof
effort and useful search coverage are not forecast from small-LP runtime.

**Process lesson.** Check placements-to-cover, area-to-normalized ratio, and
restricted-to-broader-conjecture arrows separately. Exact configuration checks
supply neither universal coverage nor the continuum argument. Prefer a geometric
obstruction or inequality over a larger taxonomy leaving continuous optimization
unresolved.


## 4. Does the F≤11 proof close the one-facet-birth direction?

**Exact target and current status.** The target is local maximality of HKO
among all sufficiently nearby convex four-polytopes with at most eleven
facets, including moving old normals. The [new candidate packet](../../variation-and-local-maxima/hko-local-maximum/f11-return-20261004/README.md)
has replaced the old open-gap status. The [initial review](../../variation-and-local-maxima/hko-local-maximum/f11-return-20261004/initial-independent-review.md)
found no demonstrated gap in its domain and Hausdorff reduction. The
[interior bridge review](../../variation-and-local-maxima/hko-local-maximum/f11-return-20261004/independent-bridge-review.md)
independently derived the section derivative, duplicate-label aggregation,
facet moment integral and cleared-polynomial identity on whole sign cells.
Full acceptance of the stored finite predicates and inherited endpoint/other-face
analytic estimates remains unperformed. No replay was run for this assessment.

**Planning bet and basis.** I would now bet the next useful return is either
acceptance of this precise local extension or one narrowly repairable premise,
rather than a new broad proof search. The reason is the explicit continuously
varying response construction and two reviews of consequential implications,
not agreement alone. Probability of a remaining finite or endpoint defect is
unassessed. The source's roughly 217-second replay is a measurement in its
retained environment; acceptance includes analytic work of unmeasured duration.

**Consequential returns.** If the finite premises and inherited analytic
dependencies are accepted, the result excludes any sequence of improving
≤11-facet polytopes converging to HKO. It closes the moving-normal one-facet
birth challenge and forces any still-possible arbitrarily nearby polytopal
improvement to use at least twelve facets. It does not rule out remote
eleven-facet bodies, higher-facet local improvements, smooth bodies, or a
larger global ratio. An isolated numerical body cannot be dismissed merely
because it has eleven facets: the theorem's neighborhood must also contain it,
and an explicit quantitative radius is not supplied by this assessment.

Acceptance would also identify a mechanism worth retaining: a positive bank
of feasible capacity upper witnesses cancels all forty aggregate old-normal
derivatives while beating the cap-volume response, with continuously adjusted
weights near endpoint directions. That explains why old-normal motion does
not rescue a small one-facet cut. It gives a possible design principle for
further extensions; two facet births have coupled responses and are not covered
by the one-birth inequality. A twelve-facet theorem would require new hypotheses
and control of that interaction, not repeated one-facet success.

If a stored positivity, stationarity, cancellation or coefficient-sign predicate
fails, the immediate result is an exact failed certificate premise. First decide
whether reconstruction, witness data, verifier code or the claimed implication
is wrong. A negative coefficient can mean only that this sufficient positivity
certificate fails, even while the polynomial is nonnegative. A missing cover
cell can leave an unproved direction without providing an improving body.
Neither is automatically a counterexample to local maximality. A failure of
the inherited relative error estimate likewise leaves endpoint sequences open;
it need not invalidate the independently checked interior response. Actual
disproof would require genuine polytopes in the stated local domain with
certified capacity/volume improvement arbitrarily close to HKO, or another
logically sufficient contradiction to the claimed local statement.

**What distinguishes these branches.** Keep finite replay and analytic acceptance
separate. The former must test actual feasible touching sections, exact mass
and forty-coordinate cancellation, cover topology and coefficient signs; the
latter must check the inherited moving two-face parametrization and relative
`o(delta²/s)` estimates at the claimed endpoint scales. A full replay PASS does
not supply that latter analytic quantifier. A sound interior result plus an
endpoint gap is a substantial restricted result, with its excluded/unchecked
directions named, rather than an all-directions theorem.

**Next step and stop.** The proposed 30-minute first audit returns exactly which
endpoint implications are checked and which remain; it does not promise full
acceptance in that cap. Finite replay is a distinct later acceptance action
after inspecting its inputs and write effects. If the audit locates one genuine
gap, spend on that gap and retain the accepted bridges; if it finds none,
complete the named acceptance premises before promotion. Reopening the old
broad gap programme has no present evidential justification. The process return
is the precise boundary between a failed proof artifact and a false theorem,
and a map of which universal directions the mechanism actually controls.

## 5. Conditional geometric mechanism: invent the missing restriction

**Expected return and basis.** My first-return bet for the proposed 20-minute
statement probe is a precise failed elimination for a proposed additional
restriction, or an explicit account of why it collapses to a tautology. A
candidate extra restriction on active weights with explicit hypotheses would
be the more ambitious first return. A ready capacity-free criterion is a later
contingent achievement, not the expected first output. There is a
substantive starting mechanism: under the stated open fixed-normal chamber
hypotheses, local maximality requires the facet-volume vector `S/(4V)` to lie
in the convex hull of normalized *active* dwell weights. However, the [active-balance source](../../../formal/active-orbit-facet-coverage.tex) is
agent-proved awaiting Jörn review. The all-feasible-weight relaxation has already
been identified as universal by the Minkowski closure and normalization
identities. There is no named extra restriction or elimination formula in the
current proposal. The probability of finding a useful restriction, and the
probability of proving a useful geometric theorem from it, are unknown; readable source
data and known local examples do not supply either estimate.

**Scientific returns if the mechanism advances.** A proved extra restriction
could describe which dwell distributions genuinely minimizing characteristics
can have, beyond nonnegativity, closure and normalization. That would be
mathematical information about the active-orbit mechanism even if eliminating
the orbit variables fails. Its relation to local maximality would come from
combining the restriction with the necessary balance; a restriction on active
orbits alone need not be specific to maxima. If that combination can be
eliminated into an independently computable geometric condition `G(K)`, with
the implication “local maximum in the stated chart implies `G(K)`,” then a body
violating `G` could be rigorously excluded from local maximality in that scope.
If the proof supplies separation of the complete active-weight hull, it may
also explain an improving support direction. A capacity-free statement does
not automatically provide that direction or establish cheap evaluation.

A condition shared by distinct accepted local maxima could expose a common
mechanism; a condition valid only in a named product subclass could explain
that subclass. Neither would classify all local maxima, imply sufficiency of
the condition, or settle HKO maximality. A necessity result may hold at bodies
which are not maxima; this limits its discrimination without making the result
false. Ridge geometry is one possible eventual field, not an established
consequence of the balance.

**Discrimination.** First ask whether the proposed active-weight restriction
excludes anything allowed by the universal feasible-weight constraints. Then
identify the exact argument that makes the eliminated statement necessary at
a maximum. A genuinely informative outcome must survive those two tests and
be computable without silently importing the capacity or a complete minimizing
orbit search as its “geometric” input. Check whether product structure or a
universal identity already implies it. Testing a selected list of HKO upper
sections cannot establish the complete necessary balance: that list is not a
complete active catalogue. HKO/P* checks also need the restriction's actual
chart and regularity hypotheses verified; a known local theorem does not
automatically put a body in this source's open support chamber. The
normal-changing extension has an additional complete smooth-envelope
hypothesis.

**Partial and adverse outcomes.** A candidate restriction whose proof remains
open is a precise conjecture and an unresolved premise. An exact derivation
showing that its elimination reduces to Minkowski identities closes that
particular attempt as a non-discriminating route. An exact violation at a
body independently established to be a local maximum, satisfying all stated
hypotheses, would refute the proposed necessary restriction or its elimination;
it would not refute the body's maximality theorem. A violation by a nonmaximum
would instead be consistent with, and potentially illustrate, an exclusion
criterion. A failure outside the hypotheses limits transfer rather than
refuting the restricted claim. “No restriction found in this window” reports
bounded method invention, not impossibility of conditional geometry. Abstract
weight countermodels can disprove a claimed algebraic implication without
being realizable EHZ polytopes; retain that distinction.

**Stop, escalation and process lesson.** Stop the initial window with the exact
restriction, tautology or failed implication; do not evaluate a body population
before there is a non-tautological statement to test. Escalate only after naming
the remaining proof/elimination obligation, its geometric scope and why its
resolution would change the research choice. If the method requires a complete
active catalogue, that acquisition is a separate burden and may defeat the
cheap-test ambition. The substantive lesson is to distinguish a necessary
condition with capacity-dependent inputs from an informative geometric
consequence; easy relaxation can erase the very information that made the
condition useful. This does not dispose of the parent question about geometry
conditional on maximality.

## 6. Nonlinear Hamiltonian control: a completed invariance separation

**Expected return and basis.** The initial analytic result is already obtained
and independently checked in the batch. For `|epsilon| <= 1/4`, the specified
Hamiltonian shear takes the unit four-ball to a smooth convex body with exactly
the same capacity and volume. Its centered uniform-volume covariance is

```
diag(1/6, 1/6 + 5*epsilon^2/144, 1/6, 1/6),
```

and its unordered Williamson spectrum is

```
{1/6, sqrt[(1/6)*(1/6 + 5*epsilon^2/144)]}.
```

Thus one spectral component varies for nonzero epsilon while the other is
fixed. This is a completed control about continuous bulk moments. It is not a
new numerical population, vertex-covariance result or external novelty claim.
The probability that it falsifies an interesting downstream relation is
unknown because no such relation has been selected. My first-return bet, if a
relation is selected and expressible on this family, is its exact symbolic
values and either a violation or an explicit non-discrimination/survival result.
Without a selected relation there is no further research-output bet: the control
already stands. The probability of obtaining a stronger theorem is unknown;
feasibility of that calculation does not forecast its scientific payoff.

**Scientific return and exact implication.** This family proves that capacity
and volume together do not determine the centered bulk Williamson spectrum,
and that this spectrum is not invariant under all symplectomorphisms between
convex bodies. Linear symplectic invariance of a covariance feature therefore
does not make it an intrinsic symplectic invariant. Fixed capacity with varying
spectrum does **not** show that spectrum fails to determine capacity: a
function from spectra to capacity could assign the same capacity to every
spectrum on this curve. Showing failure in that direction would require a
different comparison with matching spectra and different capacities. The
present control supplies no such pair.

**Contingent stronger returns and discrimination.** If an actual claim says
that the whole bulk spectrum is determined by capacity and volume, this family
is already an exact counterexample. If a claim instead involves a specific
function of the spectrum, first check whether that function varies here; the
smaller eigenvalue is constant, so the family does not discriminate every
spectral claim. A proposed exact identity `G(spectrum)=H(capacity,volume)`
fails on this family only if its left side demonstrably varies while the right
side is fixed. A proposed one-sided inequality needs its own calculation: a
violation would refute that inequality on the convex smooth class, whereas
holding throughout this family only records survival of this test. A surviving
inequality could still be useful, and an empirical predictor can remain useful
despite lack of symplectic invariance. No relation linking bulk moments to
local maximality follows from this example.

The control also distinguishes intrinsic capacity variation from geometric
feature variation within a Hamiltonian equivalence class. That can restrict
the interpretation of a measured association without denying its population
utility. A genuinely new research return would be the exact falsification or
scope correction of a named proposed relation, rather than collecting further
instances of the already established separation.

**Partial and adverse outcomes.** A relation constant on this particular curve
is an uninformative test for that relation, not a failed invariance result.
A bound that survives provides a controlled check, not global validation.
Without an actual downstream claim, the complete result remains a narrow
representation boundary and may have less marginal research value than an
extremality mechanism. A polytope feature or extreme-vertex claim cannot be
refuted merely by changing continuous bulk covariance here. Transferring the
control to polytopes would need its own approximation, feature-continuity and
capacity/volume-error arguments; approximate equality would not retain the
present exact-equality conclusion automatically.

**Stop, escalation and process lesson.** Retain the completed control now.
If a relevant relation is named, the proposed 15-minute symbolic window can
return its explicit values, a violation or its unresolved expression. Without
one, stop; additional capacity calls or polytope approximation have no selected
payoff. Escalation needs a named implication that the smooth example cannot
already decide, plus a separate transfer contract and resource assessment.
The lesson is to state the direction of determinacy and the representation
being measured before choosing a control. An exact separation can remove an
overstrong interpretation while leaving bounds, prediction and the reverse
determinacy question open.
