# papers/

arXiv paper sources for reading and citation verification.

**Naming:** `<abbreviationYear>/` — first letters of author surnames + year. Example: Baracco-Bernardi-Lerario-Mondino 2023 → `bblm2023/`

**Download:** `curl -L "https://arxiv.org/e-print/<arxiv-id>"`, untar into the directory.

**Bib entry:** Add to `thesis/bibliography.bib`. Verify author names against the actual paper — never from memory.
