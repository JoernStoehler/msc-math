# Bounded interpretation

The frozen design shows a strict reversal on both specified paths: as the
ridge feature `R` decreases from the `q01` placement to its equality endpoint,
the recorded `sys` decreases at each retained step. This is a direct numerical
observation of these two deterministic one-dimensional paths, not a result
about a random conditional distribution.

The two exact endpoint controls are consistent with the reviewed derivations:
the `3x6` endpoint has `sys=3/4` and the `4x4` endpoint has `sys=1/2` to the
retained tolerance. The `3x6/q01` row is the highest recorded row here
(`sys=0.9509718381225976`); its candidate coordinates and CDF placement were
fixed before target evaluation. Its certificate provides exact-minimizer
evidence only for its submitted f64-derived rational geometry and the stated
enumerated billiard stream.

The appropriate planning update is narrow: a general optimizer whose only
objective is ever smaller ridge sum is not presently supported by these two
paths. This does not erase prior coarse low-ridge enrichment, identify an
active-billiard-branch mechanism, or show that ridge minimization generally
lowers `sys`.

The rows are hand-designed after inspecting the frozen feature CDF and before
evaluating `sys`. They are therefore a pre-target construction, not a held-out
validation sample or repeatable general proposer. The endpoint CDF counts are
empirical placement information for `R` only and do not estimate geometry
probabilities, `sys` frequencies, or a population tail.

The technical review accepts the repaired packet for further research reuse
and guarded thesis-facing discussion; see `reviews/technical-review.md`. It
does not constitute Jörn approval, a symbolic-family certificate, or a final
thesis claim.
