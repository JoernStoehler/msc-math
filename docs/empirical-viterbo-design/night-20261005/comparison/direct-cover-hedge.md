## Why hedge the two positive mechanisms

The selected groups have actual initial mathematical inputs and bounded proof
targets. They are better supported routes to near-term new theorems than a
global fixed-Q inequality with no supplied missing estimate. This is a qualitative
feasibility comparison; success probabilities and quota conversion are unknown.

cover probe has higher conditional payoff if it finds a genuine area improvement
or a substantial lower-bound stratum, and uses different coordinates from the
support-LP rank route. One bounded hedge preserves that scientific ambition
without launching exhaustive contact classification or another vague optimizer.
