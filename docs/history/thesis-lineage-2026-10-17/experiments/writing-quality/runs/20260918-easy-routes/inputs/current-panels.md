### Paired tangentialization

Packet: `/workspaces/msc-math/experiments/sys-datascience/methods/paired-tangentialization/`.

Sixteen latent pairs, eight each for 4×4 and 4×6 products, each yield baseline, q-only, p-only and both-factor interventions. All 64 requests succeed. Both-factor minus baseline sys averages 0.0152685, with eleven positive and five negative effects. The 4×4 mean is 0.0039814 and the 4×6 mean 0.0265556. Thus the finite panel demonstrates heterogeneous effects of replacing support heights by one; it contradicts universal positive monotonicity on these examples. Maximum numerical sys is 0.7414698962.

The generator reuses normals, creates both original and unit-height factors, normalizes each factor by area, and admits a latent draw only if all four product geometries pass target-free reconstruction. There were 63 attempts, 47 rejected. This jointly admissible conditional distribution is not the historical table's sampling law. Separate factor area normalization preserves the ideal mathematical ratio (common dilation composed with symplectic reciprocal factor scaling); binary64 reconstruction makes its implemented realization approximate.

The frozen grid and records support the paired contrast. They do not support a population effect, statistical significance, a counterexample search rate, a ridge mechanism, or a broader claim that tangentialization is beneficial.

### Orientation allocation

Packet: `/workspaces/msc-math/experiments/sys-datascience/methods/orientation-allocation/`.

Four blocks, two each for 3×3 and 4×4 products, compare sixteen IID bodies against sixteen fixed orientation choices on the block's first body. The first body belongs to both arms but is evaluated once: 124 unique requests, 128 charged memberships. All succeed. IID's block maximum is higher in every block, by 0.030215, 0.046515, 0.244251 and 0.258732. Equal-bucket mean orientation-minus-IID maximum is −0.144928. Orientation nevertheless improves three of its four starting bodies. Maximum numerical sys is 0.773536.

This separates two scientific questions: changing orientation can improve a fixed body, while this particular fixed allocation lost to fresh shapes at equal request count. It does not establish that orientation search generally loses, or that the best orientations of these bodies were found. Four blocks do not justify population/significance claims.

Dispatch follows actual stored geometry: 72 product requests and 52 general ones. Four U(2) controls pass, max absolute delta sys about 3.89e−16. General SO(4) transformations preserve Euclidean geometry ideally but may leave the coordinate-product capacity route. Charged times include per-arm proposal/validation and request work; shared costs count in both scientific budgets. Compilation and serialization are not included in those arm clocks.

## Numerical contract and provenance

Adapter source: `/workspaces/msc-math/experiments/sys-datascience/methods/current-body-evaluator/`.

The scalar is evaluated on the body whose dual vertices are the **stored binary64 values**. Exact-binary64 geometry reconstruction supplies incidence and rational vertices. Product capacity is exact rational; general capacity uses outward bounds and scalar acceptance tolerance 1e−10. Volume is computed in f64 after converting exact-derived vertices to f64, so `sys = capacity²/(2 volume)` is numerical, **not a certified ratio enclosure**. Rounded transformations do not certify exact invariance of the source algebraic body. This does not invalidate numerical comparisons away from a claimed exact threshold; it limits their advertised guarantees.
