1. Executive view

The phenomenon: in the retained 14,336-body sample, smaller volume-normalized total absolute symplectic area of two-faces generally accompanies larger recorded numerical systolic ratio. The pooled Spearman correlation is about −0.938, whereas Pearson is about −0.205. Within each fixed facet-count or polygon-size group the rank relation is also inverse. This is an ordering in specified sampled populations, not a universal law.

The geometric insight: for Lagrangian polygon products the descriptor is a normalized sum of absolute edge dot products. In rotated regular pentagon products, the already proved capacity profile yields an exact inverse-square identity. Equilateral triangle products have the opposite strict ordering; designed 3×6 and 4×4 paths also numerically reverse the proposed local-improvement rule. A statistic can still rank populations usefully even if it is a bad direction for deforming each member.

The prospective test: a frozen low-ridge selection of new generated products raised their mean recorded ratio against same-pool controls. A second criterion—the largest ridge's share of total area—distinguished halves within the low-ridge tail. The fixed two-stage rule also enriched candidates against disjoint controls on one changed, separately area-normalized source in two buckets. These finite panels support candidate selection under named designs, neither a mechanism nor source-independent stability.

2. Argument the writer should make

Start with the research question: can readily computed geometric measurements help find bodies with large systolic ratio? Explain the target and the symplectic ridge descriptor before the reported correlation. The reader needs to know why a ridge can have positive Euclidean area yet zero symplectic area, and why absolute values are taken face by face. State the sampling law soon enough that “strong association” has a population.

Then distinguish three operations in the text. Post-target discovery finds an ordering in bodies whose ratios have already been evaluated. Mathematical interpretation asks why the ordering might occur; the pentagon identity gives a real answer for one family, while the triangle and path controls forbid an unrestricted answer. Prospective selection freezes geometry-only rules before evaluating capacities in new candidate pools. Each step answers a different question; later success must not be read backward as a proof of the earlier explanation.

A useful transition is: “The exact pentagon calculation shows that ridge area can encode capacity variation, but the counterexamples prevent treating smaller ridge area as an improvement direction. We therefore test the weaker task of selecting promising bodies from a population before computing capacity.” After describing that test, close on the concrete scientific gain: the feature identifies useful candidate groups under named generators, while further filtering and deformations expose limits to its explanatory power. This is a proposed explanatory order, not the historical order of discovery.

Reader-facing meaning of the terms

The systolic ratio is sys(K) = cEHZ(K)2 / (2 vol4(K)). For historical targets, write an explicit numerical notation such as sys_hat if needed. “Transfer” here means the frozen selectors' finite mean advantage on a changed source law; it does not mean transport of a theorem or source-independent predictive accuracy. A “control” is a same-source comparison body chosen without target values, with eligibility restrictions specified by its packet. “Target-free” describes selection before evaluating sys, not feature discovery before seeing any prior target.

3. Mathematical meaning

In coordinates (q1,q2,p1,p2), let ω0((q,p),(q′,p′)) = q·p′ − p·q′. For a cyclic polygonal ridge F with vertices vi, its unsigned symplectic area is |½ Σiω0(vi,vi+1)|. Sum over ridges, then divide by √vol4(K) to obtain R(K), the table field ridge_symp_area_sum_over_volume_sqrt. The separate absolute values prevent cancellation; replacing sum by mean changes the pentagon constant. Its normalization is invariant under common dilation; symplectic maps preserve each face integral and volume.

For P ×L Q with cyclic planar edge vectors ai, bj and areas AP, AQ:
R(P ×L Q) = [Σi,j |ai · bj|] / √(APAQ).

Factor faces lie in Lagrangian planes and contribute zero; each mixed rectangular face contributes |ai·bj|. This is the geometric bridge from a table column to a rotation family, rather than a fitted regression formula. The implementation requires complete correctly ordered two-face enumeration; a low sum with failed ordering could be a missing-face artifact. The mathematical source account maps the formula to code and supplies this caveat.

Rotated regular pentagons: exact restricted identity

Let P5 be the centered circumradius-one regular pentagon and Kθ=P5×LRot(θ)P5. Put d(θ)=dist(θ,(π/5)ℤ)∈[0,π/10]. The edge formula and the sign pattern of five cosines give R(Kθ)=16 sin(π/5) cos d(θ). The proved capacity profile gives sys(Kθ)=[(5+2√5)/10] sec²d(θ). Thus sys(Kθ)R(Kθ)²=16(3+√5). Within this family, increasing d lowers R and raises sys. At alignment sys≈0.947; at the HKO position it is (3+√5)/5≈1.047. The proof of capacity belongs to the rotation chapter, not to a statistical fit. This later identity was not the historical source of the earlier exploratory screen.

Boundaries that actively test interpretation

The selected chapter gives an exact opposite law for equilateral triangle products: R=8√3 cos d3, sys=(1/2)sec²(π/6−d3), and both decrease across the reduced angle. It also proves R≥8 for polygon products, with equality at centrally symmetric matched factors; an aligned cube realizes R=8 and sys=1/2, while HKO has larger R and sys>1. Independently, the endpoint-path packet records strict numerical same-direction decreases in R and sys at every retained step on specified 3×6 and 4×4 paths. The path observation concerns sampled positions, not all points of the continuous path, a random conditional law, or symbolic branch behavior.

5. Limits and writing decisions still owed

  1. Scope of the passage: this slice can be one-to-two pages if it gives phenomenon, product bridge, exact counterposed family, selection design and finite-transfer outcome. The full chapter also addresses conditional attenuation, covariance balance, shape interventions, and branch-aware optimization; decide placement against the selected Chapter 8 before replacing any source.
  2. Terminology: specify “recorded numerical systolic ratio” for historical experiments; later capacity interval revalidation does not retrospectively certify the floating volume or exact ratio. Reserve exact sys statements for proven family identities.
  3. Explanatory link: the pentagon law shows the descriptor encodes capacity in one family. There is no established implication from that law to low-R selection on heterogeneous pools. A sentence claiming that it explains transfer needs new support.
  4. Controls: a concise main-text description may say same-source unselected controls chosen without capacity values, with the hash and dual-selector exclusion in the appendix. Do not call them uniformly random or unrestricted.
  5. Figures: the existing stratified association display exposes within-group structure but is large. Its caption and final PDF placement need inspection. A table of two transfer bucket means can support a short passage without importing all analysis diagnostics.
  6. Human work: authoring and agent meaning checks can expose errors; source-backed meaning and Jörn's readability/acceptance are distinct. Jörn's prior feedback gives a target for organization, not a PASS for a new draft. Ask him for a short contextual judgment on a frozen passage if available; otherwise record that judgment as open.
