## Consequential changes to the research map

- The unrestricted pentagon-cover lower bound remains a serious target. A smaller verified cover would be informative in the opposite direction and can certify improvement without full capacity enumeration.
- The reported seven-cut threshold concerns a restricted family. Together with the reported eleven-facet neighborhood theorem, it gives a conditional local facet range of 12–17, leaving 12–16 open.
- Physical orbit families are a scientific object distinct from scalar QP optima. Nonsmooth Zoll theory adds a quotient and a concrete cut-additivity bridge.
- The full labeled symplectic pairing matrix is a complete baseline, up to linear symplectic equivalence, against which proposed compressions can be tested.
- Recent primary literature supplies additional forbidden product classes and an established quaternionic-symmetry inequality. These change what remains worth proposing.
- EHZ–Gromov-width noncoincidence already follows from a ratio above one. A potentially stronger project is an exact cut-additivity defect or a quantitative embedding result.
