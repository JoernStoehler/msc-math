# Incremental reader-driven revision

The author receives the existing complete route-reader final and the checked scientific packet. A fresh reader receives a prefix only and reconstructs the argument before identifying its own unresolved questions. The author responds using checked material, explicitly distinguishes questions that can be answered here from deferred or unavailable evidence, and releases the next prefix. This is an authoring intervention, not an independent final acceptance review. Reader context accumulates across prefixes; no quality verdict is requested.

Planned units: (1) empirical population and descriptor association; (2) product geometry, pentagon interpretation and boundary; (3) generated-candidate selection and conclusion. These boundaries were chosen before reader observations. The parent specifically requested attention to comprehension dependencies and dense selection evidence; the reader was not given that diagnosis or prescribed questions.

Allowed evidence: original inputs/ scientific packet and route-reader/supplemental-source/ concentration packet. No new mathematical experiments. Preserve substantive mathematical content and numerical evidence; delete or qualify unsupported assertions. Intermediate revisions and exact reader prompts/outputs are retained.
