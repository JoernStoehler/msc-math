\section{Numerical Implementation}\label{app:numerical}

The algorithms in Section~\ref{sec:algorithms} are stated for
exact arithmetic: predicates such as $\beta_i > 0$ and
$N^\top \beta = 0$ are decided exactly, and the KKT system is
solved without numerical error.
In a floating-point implementation, each of these operations
introduces rounding errors that must be controlled.

This appendix develops the numerical counterpart of the exact
algorithm in five parts.
Section~\ref{app:near-singular} analyses the most delicate
issue---near-singular KKT systems whose solutions are sensitive
to perturbations---and proves that such systems can be safely
dismissed.
Section~\ref{app:three-valued} replaces each Boolean predicate
in the algorithm with a three-valued version
(\textsc{true} / \textsc{false} / \textsc{unknown})
that accounts for finite-precision evaluation.
Section~\ref{app:error-tracking} describes how the algorithm
combines these verdicts to report a capacity value with
appropriate certainty qualifiers.
Section~\ref{sec:unknown-predicates} empirically validates the
three-valued predicate on a diverse sample of polytopes, checking
whether \textsc{unknown} verdicts ever affect the reported capacity.
Section~\ref{sec:ablation-pruning} analyses how successive
refinements of the adjacency graph reduce the combinatorial
search space.
