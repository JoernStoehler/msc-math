\subsection{KKT matrix inertia validation}
\label{sec:kkt-inertia}

Lemma~\ref{lem:kkt-inertia} decomposes the eigenvalue inertia
of the KKT matrix~$M$ into contributions from the restricted
Hessian~$H|_T$ and the constraint rank~$p$:
\begin{equation*}
  n_-(M) = n_-(H|_T) + p.
\end{equation*}
Remark~\ref{rem:eigenvalue-signs} uses this to read off the
second-order behaviour of~$Q$ on the constraint surface from
the eigenvalues of~$M$.
We validate this formula on all $(S,\sigma)$ nodes of the
7~known polytopes with $F \le 10$.

For each node, we compute $n_-(M)$ from the eigenvalues of~$M$
(using threshold $10^{-10}$) and independently classify~$H|_T$
by restricting the Hessian to the tangent space $T = \ker(A)$.
Table~\ref{tab:kkt-inertia} reports whether
$n_-(M) = p$ agrees with $n_-(H|_T) = 0$
(i.e., $H|_T$~non-negative definite).

\caption{Inertia decomposition check.
  ``$n_- {=}\, p$'' counts nodes where $n_-(M)$ equals

The formula holds for 6 of 7~polytopes.
The 5~mismatches in the HK-O pentagon all share the same pattern:
$H|_T$ has eigenvalues $\{-a,\, \varepsilon,\, +a\}$ where
$\varepsilon \sim 10^{-16}$ (machine epsilon), and the
corresponding~$M$ has three eigenvalues at ${\sim}10^{-16}$
that are ambiguous in sign.
The inertia formula is correct in exact arithmetic, but the
discrete eigenvalue classification cannot resolve eigenvalues
at machine precision.
