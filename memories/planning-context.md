## Reasoning worth recovering (agent synthesis)

The purpose of summaries is to help writing and review. If their useful design
depends on learning from those activities, postponing summary design can help
avoid speculative infrastructure. It does not follow that investigating those
activities is unnecessary. Likewise, successful environment checks remove some
obstacles but do not establish the sufficiency of the remaining authoring path.

Layout and authoring investigations plausibly can overlap: concrete retrieval
or ownership friction can inform layout, while layout investigation can expose
workflow obstacles. This is a current planning hypothesis, not a required
sequence or authorization to reorganize the repository.
