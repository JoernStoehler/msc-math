\section*{Disclosure of AI Use}
\phantomsection
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Disclosure of AI Use}

Generative artificial intelligence was used materially throughout the research
and production of this thesis, principally through Codex and Claude Code agents.
Agents were not limited to language polishing or executing fully specified
tasks.  They participated in choosing questions and methods; developing
mathematical arguments, software, experiments, and prose; interpreting and
criticizing results; revising work; and planning, delegating, and reviewing work
by other agents.  This breadth of AI use was approved by my advisor.

There was no stable division of labor by category.  I am not aware of any kind
of work I performed on this thesis that I did not also delegate to agents at
some point.  Agents and I both planned, produced, interpreted, corrected, and
reviewed work, often through repeated handoffs.  This overlap does not imply
equal labor shares or identical contributions to every result.  The research
and production process, and many retained components, therefore have mixed
human--AI provenance.  I do not claim sole production or sole intellectual
origin of every part of the thesis.  Repeated handoffs and incomplete records
do not support a reliable item-by-item allocation of first origin.

I nevertheless proofread the final thesis and personally worked to verify its
mathematical and factual claims and the overall message it presents.
