# What the preparation has actually tested about authoring

Root reconstruction, 18 September 2026. This is evidence accounting, not a
new authoring experiment or a probability estimate.

## Direct authoring trials

The four easy-route authors produced DS subsection candidates using copying
and editing, dialogue followed by composition, drafting followed by subtraction,
and reader questions followed by repair. The retained usage receipt records
8 minutes 38 seconds elapsed and $10.55 shadow cost for that linked agent tree.
The reader-route Candidate D subsequently received Jörn's verdict:
“Needs revision before acceptable.” Its agent checks missed many actual reader
objections. The other three candidates did not receive equivalent human grades.

The follow-up global rewrite and incremental reader-repair attempts took
9 minutes 37 seconds elapsed and $10.04 shadow cost, per their retained receipt.
They reuse the same DS material and are not independent samples of thesis tasks.
They have agent assessments but no recorded comparable human acceptance verdict.

Receipts:

- `.worktrees/easy-writing-routes/experiments/writing-quality/runs/20260918-easy-routes/usage.txt`
- `.worktrees/easy-routes-followup/experiments/writing-quality/runs/20260918-easy-followup/usage.txt`

These costs cover only locally linked descendants of those two runs, not the
whole project, other root threads, human attention or preparation of their inputs.

## Subsequent evidence

Human-feedback revisions repair known objections and preserve a paragraph that
Jörn explicitly accepted in context. The complete DS chapter now assembled is
a larger repair attempt using that feedback and recovered scientific evidence.
It has not yet received a human writing verdict. This cannot be counted as a
successful independent authoring trial.

The reviewer trial measures a different capability. Its broad misses argue
against treating reviewer approval as acceptance. Its useful narrow flags may
still help authoring; their end-to-end utility remains unmeasured. It does not
directly measure the best writing achievable by a different composition process.

## Consequence for interpreting elapsed preparation time

At least six hours elapsed between the completed dataset preparation and the
earlier integration checkpoint, but that is not six hours of direct authoring
experimentation. The two measured trial batches above sum to about 18 minutes.
Other work included mathematics, data recovery, review experiments, interface
work, discussion and coordination. Their costs must not be silently attributed
to authoring search or silently treated as irrelevant to end-to-end success.

Root also left a measured 26 minutes 23 seconds with no active root turn after
the 13:34:55 UTC checkpoint, before resuming at 14:01:18 UTC. The previously
commissioned agents had finished. That interval is coordination inactivity,
not evidence of computational difficulty or of a failed authoring attempt.

The current evidence supports neither “we exhaustively tried writing workflows”
nor “writing will work with more time.” A decision to spend further effort must
name the capability being tested and distinguish its outcome from integration
progress. This account does not revive the retracted numerical forecasts.
