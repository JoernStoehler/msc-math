## Concrete first-wave recommendation

Begin with three scientific leads: F11 acceptance and two competing discovery probes. Each lead owns its specialist prompts, returns and mathematical integration, with at most two concurrent children. One scientific comparator receives the three integrated returns; a separate observer checks pending decisions, actual handles and scope. Root assigns these outcome contracts once, then mostly stays in bounded active wait. Root receives a comparative recommendation and exceptional resource/scope decisions, not every worker check-in. This is a proposed staffing choice, not a measured capacity limit or selected execution.

Do not open six groups merely because they can be staffed. If actual results justify six distinct groups, split comparative integration between two coordinators, each receiving three lead reports. Before expanding, require a reviewer for each consequential implication, confirmed delivery/action routes, and a disposition for existing pending decisions. If those are unavailable, retain the small wave rather than adding management around an unresolved queue.

At every substantive return, the lead updates its claim/domain, adverse evidence, live assumption, rival and next falsifiable step. The comparator revises the scientific shortlist and recommends continue, redirect, park or stop. Root records the allocation and authorization decision. The observer proposes changes to check-in intervals or reporting contracts when actual queues expose a weakness; it cannot silently expand spending or scientific scope. Failed attempts change the plan only when their named mechanism supports a useful next statement.

Root uses **active goal mode only after Jörn’s departure signal**, or keeps its single turn open, to continue waiting and handling arrivals while AFK. Goal activation waits for that departure signal so chat questions remain possible beforehand. Ordinary completion of one goal turn is not root disappearance.

### Decision and mailbox contracts

**Root** owns selected outcomes, comparative resource decisions, exceptions beyond delegated scope and the final coordination handoff. At an arrival it: (1) identifies the envelope/version and required decision; (2) checks the scientific coordinator's recommendation, adverse review and actual source links; (3) decides continue, redirect, park or stop within existing authorization; (4) records the decision and receiving owner; (5) acknowledges dispatch to that owner and returns to an active bounded wait. It does not rewrite leaf prompts or silently become the mathematical integrator. If it must inspect a disputed argument, it commissions a named bounded reviewer and preserves the decision as unresolved.

**Two scientific coordinators** each own three groups' scientific comparison and durable synthesis. They decide local mathematical sequencing inside selected scope; they send root a comparative envelope rather than six raw transcripts. Each coordinator receives three group reports, not twelve independent worker conversations. Neither is the unreviewed universal judge: a reviewer from the other coordinator's branch checks the promoted implication, while each group retains its domain evidence. Cross-review takes a named output and stop point; it does not create another unbounded director queue.

**Six group leads** author task-specific prompts, dispatch and supervise up to their selected 3–4 temporary workers, integrate their mathematics, and own source retention. A lead may replace or stop a worker, issue a gap-specific repair and re-sequence existing work within its group's approved scope and resource allowance. It may not grow the concurrency allowance, extend beyond cutoff, switch to paid API, or silently change the group's scientific objective. It reports such proposed changes upward. Worker output ownership is exclusive; reviewers return findings and do not imply acceptance by agreement.

**Observer** watches root as well as the other owners: actual handles/status, unanswered decision envelopes, review age, assignment fit and available resource evidence. Root must delegate authority to freeze new dispatch/renewal and interrupt specified nonroot assignments on a scope/resource/cutoff violation; a warning-only observer is insufficient. Before execution, test its exact delivery/action routes to both coordinators and root. A sibling route's availability cannot be inferred from one parent-child success. Native messaging must not be claimed to wake an ended root. If the observer cannot act on a named branch, record that limitation before scaling and use shorter expiring packets there.

Every assignment records outcome, source baseline, output owner/path, receiving owner, scope/resource authorization, next review/expiry and cutoff. Every return carries assignment/version, claim and domain, checked evidence, adverse/unknown implications, useful rival, next bounded operation and the decision needed. A return needing no root decision stays with its lead/coordinator; a mailbox receipt is not mathematical acceptance. Each owner retains a small pending-decision/review ledger with sender, artifact, arrival, receiving owner and disposition, so observation can distinguish a real backlog from unknown backlog. Root integrates material registry changes in [current.json](../../../coordination/current.json); it does not copy every check-in there.

### Missed decision handling despite continuation is a recovery branch

Under active goal mode or an open turn, root actively waits and checks decision envelopes. Ordinary goal-turn completion is not an outage. After an envelope waits two observer passes without disposition, observer checks whether root continuation is functioning and sends one source-linked urgent message. This proposed eight-minute trigger may be shortened near cutoff. Root may be active and thinking: report that observation without diagnosing overload or declaring it gone.

Recovery is for actual quota refusal, daemon/transport failure, or observed missed decision handling despite continuation. If the decision remains unhandled after inspection, coordinators **freeze new starts, scope changes and renewals** while the observer records the concrete observed failure and unknown cause. Already authorized independent packets may finish within their existing expiry. Leads retain evidence and stop at expiry. Root continuation can handle the retained envelope once functioning; the observer checks actual disposition rather than treating a submission acknowledgment as recovery. No new wake service or transport is installed here. Do not transfer root's spending/selection authority to a scientist merely because a decision is delayed.

Active goal mode is the selected continuation mechanism after departure; it does not by itself demonstrate recovery from quota refusal, daemon/transport failure or universal stop enforcement. A correctly judged warning is not enough.

Before any large fan-out, root owns a small route/readiness trial: choose actual coordinator/observer handles, send one synthetic decision envelope, verify receiver action/acknowledgment, exercise a stop on a disposable nonroot, and test whether root stays in active wait and handles an arrival under the selected continuation mode.
