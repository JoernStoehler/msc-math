## What Failed Audit

The recovery closure treated these table-scoped facts as if they closed the
project exploration:

1. The active invariant schema has essentially two feature families:
   combinatorial summaries and symplectic two-face-area summaries. Applying
   many models to them did not test whether ridge geometry is unique among
   natural invariant descriptions.
2. Ridge sum is not unique even inside its family. Mean is redundant within
   fixed product buckets; max, standard deviation, and upper quantiles are
   competing directions.
3. Search methods rejected for lacking an active-table candidate object have
   viable project interfaces elsewhere: product angle/support coordinates,
   fixed-facet dual vertices, branch-aware local search, combinatorial
   boundaries, facet continuation, and structured families.
4. In-table prediction is not direct search, while direct adaptive search may
   legitimately use sys. The earlier account organized nearly all future work
   around enrichment filters.
5. Existing regular-product, branch/action, combinatorial-transition, and
   gradient-ascent surfaces may contain mathematics-informing evidence that
   folder-scoped closure did not assess.

Therefore the statements “no additional dataset is selected,” “remaining work
is presentation,” and “neither another method nor another search packet is
likely to repay its cost” are recovered hypotheses, not accepted decisions.
