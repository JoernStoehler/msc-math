# AI-use / Provenance Log-backed Summary

## How to read this

This is an interaction-provenance model, not a mathematical correctness audit and not thesis disclosure prose. The main evidence is raw Codex rollout JSONL under `/home/vscode/.codex`, especially user messages, user-visible agent messages, and task-completion summaries. User messages are strong evidence for what Jörn supplied in chat, but they do not exhaust Jörn's offline thinking, prior work, meetings, or source reading. Agent messages are strong evidence for what AI produced or attempted, but not by themselves evidence that Jörn accepted the output.

I did not use git history as source truth for idea provenance. Repo notes such as `docs/project-facts.md`, `thesis/use-of-ai-content.md`, and the scratch AI-use interview worksheet were treated as downstream hypotheses/context. This investigation sampled and grouped high-signal project sessions rather than every one of the 2,080 `/workspaces/msc-math` rollout files visible before the host import. Evidence strength means: **strong** = direct chat-level provenance in one or more central sessions; **medium** = direct session evidence for the work type but not full acceptance/origin; **weak** = session names or downstream context support the model but logs inspected here do not settle origin.

**Archive coverage:** the first pass used the Codex logs visible in `/home/vscode/.codex`. A host-side scan then imported additional logs into `/home/vscode/.codex/imported_session_logs/2026-07-01-host-scan` and `/home/vscode/.claude/imported_session_logs/2026-07-01-host-scan`; see `reports/session-log-import-report-2026-07-01.md`. I then added a targeted Claude/pre-Codex pass. The relevant visible top-level Claude project transcripts now include 463 `msc-math`/`msc-viterbo` sessions from 2026-01 through 2026-04; 341 matched thesis/provenance keywords, and I extracted 28 high-signal sessions. This is now a practical log-backed provenance deliverable across the available Codex + Claude archives, but not a claim that every subagent/tool transcript was read.

## Executive model

Broadly, AI was used as a high-throughput research, coding, writing, review, and orchestration partner. The logs strongly support that agents wrote or edited substantial Rust/Sage/Python code, experiment/control documentation, thesis drafts, content packets, proof-risk audits, review reports, prompt/workflow surfaces, and PaperOrchestra adaptation notes. Agents also ran tests, inspected source papers, proposed proof/exposition structures, created or reviewed exact certificate workflows, and summarized experiment results.

Broadly, Jörn supplied thesis goals, topic constraints, mathematical target framing, priorities, taste calibration, review gates, corrections, acceptance/rejection decisions, and many concrete technical questions. In the main thesis-slice sessions, Jörn repeatedly defines the success criterion, asks for specific proof/evidence structure, corrects agent framing, rejects insufficient plans, approves selected actions, and asks for broader or narrower research surfaces. This is strong evidence that Jörn was not merely accepting autonomous AI output; he was steering what counts as a thesis-successful result.

The largest remaining uncertainty is not broad AI use; that is strongly established. The remaining uncertainty is first-origin of some mathematical ideas. The imported Claude logs improve this: they show Jörn explicitly bringing experiment ideas, hypotheses, and Kai-discussed directions into chat in February-March 2026, including data-science datasets, Lagrangian 2-face/omega hypotheses, gradient search, and HKO local-maximum investigation. They also show agents writing, implementing, reviewing, and sometimes making mistakes in thesis/proof/code material. They still do not settle every first-origin question: HKO local maximality, pentagon formula discovery, and exact certificate strategy appear as already-active project threads by March-April.

## Comparison against the scratch AI-use interview worksheet

The worksheet is broadly directionally right in saying "AI-written / Jörn-steered" for code, experiments, process notes, and draft prose. It should be sharpened in these ways:

- "Choosing HKO2024 as central target: Jörn/Kai/literature" remains plausible but not fully proven. Imported Claude logs show HKO local maximality as an active Jörn-framed conjecture by 2026-03-23 and show Kai-discussed experiment ideas elsewhere, but not the original decision moment.
- "First-order local-variation proof strategy: mixed" is the right uncertainty label. Logs show Jörn asks for exact certificate/proof-tree framing and corrects agents, while AI audits and drafts the route. They do not settle first-origin of the key idea.
- "Pentagon formula discovered by AI-assisted computation" remains plausible but only partly supported. Claude logs show rotation/regular-polygon products were already a project story by early April; June Codex logs show the formula/proof skeleton already exists and Jörn drives CAS/KKT proof questions. First discovery is still unsettled.
- "Clarke rewrite rigorously: Jörn" is still too strong as a sole-authorship claim. Claude logs show Jörn correcting proof details and demanding rigor, while agents edited proof prose and sometimes had unresolved proof gaps. Better label: `Jörn mathematical review/steering + AI-assisted proof prose/source repair`.
- "Most Rust code: AI-written" is strongly supported as a broad disclosure model for inspected work, but should say "AI-written or AI-edited under Jörn constraints, tests, and selective review", not imply every line.
- "Sage verifier / executable proof trusted after Jörn review" should remain conditional. Logs show exact Sage verifier/certificate surfaces and review gates, but not final Kai/Jörn mathematical acceptance.
- "Data-science negative interpretation accepted" is partly supported, but only in bounded form: no retained random/product table row with `sys > 1` and no current candidate-proposer/source from the closed method table. The earlier Claude logs show Jörn originated much of the data-science framing and some hypotheses, but not the final negative conclusion.
- "Figures mostly AI-generated" is under-supported by inspected logs. There is evidence of AI planning/reviewing assets, but final selected thesis figure provenance remains open.
- "Existing active thesis prose heavily AI-drafted" is strongly supported. However, later Jörn rewrite/acceptance could change final prose provenance section by section.
- "Advisor contributions" remain mostly unverified by logs. Treat Kai/Elizabeth context as Jörn-reported unless meeting notes or direct messages are added.
