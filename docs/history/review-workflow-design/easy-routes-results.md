# Easy writing routes: executed DS portfolio

Four substantially different production operations produced concrete contemporary DS sections. Simple fresh readers identified and helped repair precise explanatory and evidential problems without a validated general prose detector. No candidate has received a new human judgment; this experiment establishes executable routes and useful local interventions, not PASS or reliable autonomous acceptance prediction.

## What was tried

All routes received the same frozen, newly checked scientific packet and the known historical DS objection: comfortable sentences but confused narrative, with the intended task of extracting geometric understanding from exploratory data. The target was a substantial ridge-interpretation/candidate-search section, not a complete DS chapter. The packet includes an analytic pentagon identity, opposing numerical paths and finite selector evidence that the older rejected sample lacked. Consequently improvement over the historical baseline cannot be attributed solely to workflow.

This is not evidence about editing human notes. |

## Useful evidence and limits

**Readers contributed something observable.** The original concentration phrase merely advertised success of a predeclared criterion without defining success. The first-obstacle route recovered lowest-1% ridge selection, split by largest-ridge share, larger mean overall and in at least seven of ten buckets, and the actual +0.03399 overall difference with eight favorable buckets. This is a changed explanation with source support, not an AI preference vote. It also changed the scientific information available to this route, so it cannot be treated as a controlled same-input comparison.

**Subtraction and dialogue made different scope choices.** Subtraction keeps a numerical optimizer pointer; dialogue omits the opaque concentration claim, retains transfer evidence, and explains the capacity theorem's independent role. These are legitimate alternatives for the final chapter boundary, not automatically wins or losses. Dialogue's questions went beyond phrasing into evidence adequacy; several were answered by admitting what the packet does not establish. The original final also contains malformed inline math delimiters, a concrete mechanical defect preserved in the experiment.

**Much of the narrative still converged.** The common packet already proposes a scientific order, and all writers saw the same human purpose. Most outputs move from sampling/association through product geometry and pentagons to limitations and selection. This trial did not escape all shared scaffolding or model voice. Different operations alone do not guarantee different prose. The alternative workflows were genuinely executed, but a single section and single run per route cannot identify a causal workflow winner.

**Known feedback was supplied.** This tests execution with legitimate existing feedback and richer science, not autonomous discovery of Jörn's preferences. A reader's concrete question can be useful even when that reader cannot predict Jörn's verdict. Conversely absence of a question does not establish clarity, mathematical completeness or human acceptance.

## Independent check and version control

A fresh reviewer received anonymized A–D copies and the frozen sources, including the reader route's supplemental selection evidence. It did not inspect workflow names or intermediate discussions. Its [full exact-location report](../../experiments/writing-quality/runs/20260918-easy-routes/evaluation/source-review.md) finds no definite false central formula or historical/certified-target conflation. It independently identifies the undefined concentration criterion in copy/subtraction, undefined rho in dialogue/subtraction, and the concrete resolution supplied by the reader route. This is source fidelity and local comprehension evidence, not an independently validated style detector.

Some reviewer findings depend on intended context: missing sys normalization in copy/subtraction is not a defect if the preceding chapter has already established the convention, as the shared audience brief allowed. The final manuscript still needs actual cross-references. Dialogue's stronger numerical/proof separation is useful, but its original math delimiters were mechanically broken. A separate `display-ready.md` fixes only 23 intended inline delimiters and preserves the evaluated original. The final reader route adds a long, dense selection paragraph; explaining the criterion is a real gain, not proof the resulting prose is readable enough.

Hash checks caught an evaluation snapshot race: subtraction's author added one scaling-invariance sentence after anonymized A was copied. Original A remains the evaluated snapshot; A2 is the completed candidate and the review addendum covers only that delta. C2 similarly preserves the display-ready dialogue variant. All other anonymized copies match final route bytes. No revision was silently substituted under a review.

## Disposition

Do not reject all four or select a winner by same-model taste. Keep the reader and dialogue outputs as especially informative review options: one shows source-driven repair with concrete selection evidence; the other uses reader questions before composing and treats numerical counterevidence more cautiously. Copy-edit remains a plausible low-effort option, and subtraction offers a shorter alternative with an optimizer connection. This is a recommendation about information value, not estimated human quality ranking.

The next human judgment should concern whether one complete contemporary account explains its scientific purpose and argument acceptably, with marked obstacles if it does not. A tiny locally improved sentence would miss the remaining structural uncertainty. The desk owns the interface and scheduling; no new queue request or human interruption was sent by this team.

Not tried in this bounded round: incremental versus global writing, independent-draft recombination, historical accepted-process reconstruction, and editing actual human notes. The portfolio was deliberately four real executions instead of ten superficial prompt variants. Further variants should follow concrete surviving obstacles or human feedback, rather than become a compulsory tournament.
