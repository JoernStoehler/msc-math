\subsection{An effective remainder and an explicit small step}
\label{subsec:hko-effective}

The derivative argument can be made effective without enumerating all
twenty-row permutations. The resulting numerical step is deliberately tiny;
its value is a quantitative control of the true capacity, rather than a
material increase in the counterexample's ratio.

\begin{proposition}[Effective improvement]\label{prop:hko-effective}
For the twenty-row family~\eqref{eq:ha-family},
\[
 \frac{\operatorname{sys}(H_t)}{\operatorname{sys}(H)}
       >1+\frac{t}{200},\qquad 0<t\leq10^{-30}.
\]
In particular, $t=10^{-30}$ is an explicit improving parameter.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Quantitative argument]
We state the estimates that connect the exact base certificate to the
finite step; the accompanying quantitative proof supplies the pivot and
rounding details and the exact arithmetic checks.
Put $q(t)=1/(2c_{\mathrm{EHZ}}(H_t))$ and $q_0=\tau/5$.
The minimum positive loss among the 3000 primitive triangle-pair actions is
\[
 \Delta=\frac{-\tau+3\tau^3}{20}>\frac1{50}.
\]
Every feasible weight vector on the moving twenty-row bank with
$Q_t\geq q_0/2$ and $0\leq t\leq10^{-3}$ has five positive pivot
coordinates bounded below by $1/4500$, with
the inverse augmented base minor bounded by $200$ in infinity norm.
Now fix a moving global maximizer with $0<t\leq10^{-30}$.
Repairing closure through that minor gives a base-feasible vector $\beta_0$.
The elementary chart bounds below first give
$d=q_0-Q_0(\beta_0)<10^{-20}$. Decomposing its two factor weights into their at
most forty occurrence-wise triangle vertices and deleting small marginal
weights gives an exactly maximizing vector on the same word within
$2000\sqrt d$ in $\ell^1$ norm. The gap $\Delta$ ensures that all retained
triangle pairs are winning. For the stated parameter range this rounding
preserves positivity of the selected pivot.

The smooth pivot chart has Taylor remainder at most $4\cdot10^9t^2$,
and its first-derivative function is $17600$-Lipschitz in the base weights.
At an exact optimizer that derivative is at most $2q_0/125$ by the already
proved all-word response bound. Completing the square in
$-d+35200000t\sqrt d$ yields the global estimate
\[
 q(t)\leq q_0+\frac{2q_0}{125}t+10^{15}t^2,
 \qquad 0<t\leq10^{-30}.
\]
This bounds the true optimal action from above, hence the capacity from
below; it is not a feasible-branch capacity upper bound.

On each original facet the new gauge is at least $1+tf_i$.
Since $0\leq f_i\leq2/5$, the radial volume formula gives
\[
 \frac{\operatorname{Vol}(H_t)}{\operatorname{Vol}(H)}
 \leq\int(1+tf)^{-4}\,d\nu
 \leq1-4\nu[f]t+\frac85t^2.
\]
Using $q_0>1/10$ and the exact value of $\nu[f]$, the two estimates imply
\[
 \frac{q(t)^2\operatorname{Vol}(H_t)}
      {q_0^2\operatorname{Vol}(H)}
 \leq1-\frac{20+64\sqrt5}{9375}t+4\cdot10^{16}t^2
 <1-\frac{t}{200}.
\]
Taking reciprocals and using $\operatorname{sys}=1/(8q^2\operatorname{Vol})$
proves the claim.
\end{proof}

At $t=10^{-30}$ the certified lower bound on relative gain is
$5\cdot10^{-33}$.
The useful additional ingredient is the uniform quadratic remainder for
the global optimal action. Finding a useful larger parameter or the maximum
along this family remains a separate capacity problem.
