## Outcome and scope

Obtain checked mathematical progress and useful agent-facing research packets.
Keep scientific value, truth and feasibility separate. No polished manuscript
rewrite, public release, storage migration or large historical data rebuild.

Recommended portfolio:

1. **Secure the new F≤11 result.** Inspect and replay the immutable finite
   certificate in scratch, and independently audit the inherited endpoint
   argument. Initial and interior bridge reviews have found no failed
   implication; full finite acceptance and inherited analytic acceptance remain.
   The retained replay reports about 217 seconds. Before execution, inspect
   inputs, effects and environment; a bounded execution timeout is not a claim
   about actual needed resources. Return the exact theorem, accepted predicates,
   checked analytic implications, remaining gaps and truthful reproduction route.
   Repair a demonstrated gap if bounded; otherwise preserve it explicitly.
2. **Attack a substantive global geometric inequality.** Start with the
   pentagon ten-triangle cover/area problem, comparing a direct inequality with
   one realizable boundary-contact reduction. First 45-minute window must state
   the unresolved inequality, a checked reduction, a meaningful counterexample,
   or an exact obstruction to the route. Existing solved affine/symmetric cases
   are controls. Arbitrary non-affinely-regular 5+5 products remain an alternative,
   not an assumed equivalent formulation or known optimal next target.
3. **Explore a distinct mechanism or representation.** A scientific owner can
   choose a conditional local-maximizer relation or a broader geometric discovery
   route, with a concrete comparison against the inherited feature vocabulary.
   First 20-minute statement/control probe: name a nontrivial restriction or
   explain why the tempting condition is unconditional. Then a 45-minute window
   may derive/test an actual mechanism or construct discriminating examples.
   Weak correlations and absence of patterns are not the intended payoff.
   An unresolved method sketch is not silently promoted to a cheap criterion.

These lanes are complementary, not statistically independent. A scope/value
review after their first returns can redirect or terminate them; labels and
timeboxes do not justify spending the rest of the window on an uninformative
route. Broad discovery and omitted alternatives remain eligible.

scientific leaves own mathematical understanding and their subagent fan-in.

value claims, missed returns, unresolved integration and observed resources. It
reports actionable changes to the active root and keeps its own stop/review point.
Goal mode keeps root continuation available; a stored owner label or submitted
follow-up is not evidence of execution. Review/renew supervision in bounded
intervals, including when the root changes phase. Do not create permanent services.

Use Sol 6.1 for ordinary scientific ownership/coordination, Luna for cheap
friction/source probes. A concrete deep reasoning bottleneck can justify bounded
Astra escalation; this is task routing, not an established model ranking.
