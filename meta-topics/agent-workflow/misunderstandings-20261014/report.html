Assessment and report context

A useful general prior can still produce a bad interpretation when it is not conditioned on the particular user, project and current conversational state. Jörn made this distinction on 14 October: “shit prior = perfect prior, but not conditioned on who the user is / what project they are in”. These incidents do not, by themselves, show that training supplied a bad prior, or that the agent knowingly ignored a better interpretation.

The original five-minute list contained 40 request–response mismatches. This revision retains their numbers and adds what was said before the mistaken move, what was clarified afterward, the plausible general default, the consequence and recovery, and the evidence limits. Forty is the size of this selected inventory, not a count of 40 equally well-proved semantic misunderstandings. Some cases concern poor action selection or instruction-following; two have only incomplete retrospective support.

The strongest distinction is temporal. A later correction tells us the user’s intended meaning, but does not establish that the meaning was explicit before the guess. Conversely, an agent can have the relevant constraint in the conversation and still fail to use it. The records sometimes directly show that the agent represented the unfinished obligation immediately before choosing an incompatible next action.

This is a mutable report source with a generated browser view at the same file location. Updates replace that view; they do not require another chat report, a new service, a plugin or a scheduled monitor. Reading this route does not provide shared inline editing. Pages access was successfully checked in this session, but a new Page was not created: access alone does not establish lower attention, navigation, synchronization or cleanup costs. No comparative usability claim has been measured.

What changes when the general prior is taken seriously

Several initial choices were more reasonable than the first list made them look. In incident 6, Jörn explicitly offered “branch/github PR” before the agent chose a PR. The important constraint was limited human review effort, and the established local workflow was clarified later in the recovered exchange. In incident 9, Jörn himself offered babysitting as an alternative to compare; the failure was recommending it without assessing the cost of scientific reading. In incident 14, an executor prompt was expressly requested; the failure was asking for approval without the supporting human case. In incident 34, the earlier temporary artifact really had been requested outside msc-math, before permanent promotion acquired a different project audience.

Other cases show generic habits overriding specific conditioning already present. Incident 11 requested the full reviewer message and rationale, but the agent added “concise”. Incident 10 continued a reassuring notification after “do it silently”. Incident 24 used courtesy closure after a detour while an explicit parent task remained. Incident 27 substituted unattended feasibility after value selection had already been assigned to the agent. These support a failure to apply available conditioning; they do not distinguish inattention, salience, context retention or another internal cause.

The remembered unfinished task is particularly informative. In incident 32 the agent had just retained both the resource-awareness gap and the concrete next discussion, then ended without that discussion or an established handoff. A solely missing-information account is insufficient for that observed transition. A general conversational closing habit could still contribute; the trace does not identify the internal mechanism.

An admission is evidence of a stated interpretation, not a causal measurement. Statements such as “I treated your challenge as an endorsement” help recover the mismatch. They do not prove why it happened. Plausible defaults in the entries are explanatory candidates, not observations of training, reasoning or motive.

The useful working decomposition is therefore: information not recovered; user-specific meaning initially underdetermined; supplied context not applied; correction not carried through to the next action; and a familiar local task or response pattern substituted for the parent outcome. These can overlap within one episode. This inventory does not select or validate a repair intervention.
