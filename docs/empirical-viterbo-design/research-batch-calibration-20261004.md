   understanding of why width bounds miss capacity. **Truth:** five strict-gap
   paths and their onset LP already exist; a simpler geometric criterion does
   not. **Feasibility:** 30-minute proposed cap for one binding-template/dual
   comparison and perturbation constraint check; assumes accessible retained
   contacts. **Rival:** broader controlled
   geometry that lets a different mechanism emerge.

2. **HKO and other local maxima.** **Value:** high if comparison yields a new
   constrained global bound or explains genuine equality/degeneracy; descriptive
   certificate comparison alone is preparation. **Truth:** HKO and non-HKO P*
   ten-facet local results exist. Triangle/quadrilateral
   products and affinely regular pentagon pairs already have bounds; the remaining
   candidate is arbitrary 5+5 with a non-affinely-regular factor. **Feasibility:**
   30-minute proposed cap for one local-to-global obstruction or explicit inequality
   candidate, assuming readable exact proof explanations. **Rival:** a direct
   arbitrary-pentagon inequality or a new nonproduct local maximum.

3. **Global ten-triangle cover.** **Value:** high, direct difficult inequality
   or counterexample. **Truth:** local rigidity and an open-cell reduction exist;
   arbitrary-partner global optimality is unresolved in the inspected record.
   **Feasibility:** 45-minute proposed cap for one realizable boundary
   contact contract and remaining variables; assumes reusable cell coordinates.
   Full continuous-stratum control is a later unresolved step. **Rival:** direct area
   inequality without contact classification.

4. **HKO F≤11.** **Value:** high meaningful local-extension result.
   **Truth:** a purported complete moving-normal proof arrived 4 October
   21:24 UTC; initial and interior bridge reviews found no demonstrated gap. Full
   finite-premise acceptance/replay and inherited endpoint analytic audit remain.
   **Feasibility:** old-gap execution is held; a 30-minute proposed endpoint-audit
   cap returns exact checked/unchecked implications, assuming accessible
   dependencies. **Rival:** accept and
   retain a sound proof, or repair only a specifically identified gap.

5. **Conditional geometry: exploratory method stub.** **Value:** potentially
   high if a broader mechanism or rigorous exclusion test can be invented.
   **Truth:** active-weight
   balance supplies a starting point; no capacity-free elimination inequality
   is proposed yet. **Feasibility:** optional 20-minute proposed statement probe for an
   additional active-weight restriction and exact hypotheses; retained geometry
   must be accessible. All-feasible-weight balance is unconditional/tautological.
   No named capacity-free inequality exists; this is absent from execution

6. **Nonlinear Hamiltonian control.** **Value:** useful independent representation
   boundary; narrower contribution to extremality. **Truth:** the analytical

   its two sides symbolically. Without that relation, no follow-up is recommended.
   **Rival:** test that relation symbolically; skip polytope approximation
   unless its separate payoff merits the cost.
