## Claim changes and status

- `prop:boundedness-iff`: its earlier proof claimed the feasible perturbation interval must be bounded. Rank four only rules out an interval unbounded in *both* directions. The replacement proves that at least one finite, nonzero endpoint exists, which is all the maximal-active-set argument needs. A fresh read-only math reviewer found the correction valid. The original text had Jörn review marker `57e73d9`; this correction itself has **not** been reviewed by Jörn. The proposition remains in its former `unverified` wrapper.
- `lem:bounded-triples`: the older statement quantified over every nonzero vector in the kernel of every triple. The usable triple-normal test concerns **linearly independent triples**, whose kernel is one-dimensional, with a separate rank-four precondition. The new proof invokes the corrected proposition. Rank-deficient triples are not asserted to supply a single test direction. This lemma remains unverified.
- `lem:irredundancy`: affine rank three shows that a row supports a facet. An individual listed inequality is irredundant under the added hypothesis that no other listed row equals it. Duplicate identical rows can each be removed while preserving the same polytope. The proof now covers both directions using strict slack at a relative-interior point. This lemma remains unverified; rank-only validators and duplicate-sensitive validators are **not** declared equivalent.
- Sampling procedure: the vertex-enumeration sentence now says each vertex has at least four linearly independent active **constraint** hyperplanes; a nonsimple vertex can be incident to more than four listed rows. Its original reviewed text was changed here and awaits Jörn's review. The finite all-four-tuples enumeration remains the same.
- `prop:integer-cramer`: the determinant and feasibility-gap formulas and positive LCM convention are unchanged. The proof now states the actual row-matrix equation `M_S v=D\mathbf{1}`. No new determinant claim was introduced.
- Rational-scaling lemmas: `D>0` may be any integer common denominator. The two referenced Rust paths choose the LCM. The candidate rational denominator `d>0` remains explicit in the feasibility-gap lemma. These mechanical agent-written lemmas remain unapproved by Jörn.
