# Choose Chapter Purpose

Use this reference when the role of a chapter or substantial passage is not
settled enough to guide writing.

Describe the intended change in reader understanding and why the surrounding
thesis needs it. Establish what earlier text supplies, what later text may rely
on, and which material this chapter should deliberately leave elsewhere. Use
ordinary prose; do not manufacture a fixed schema merely to make the decision
look complete.

Check the proposed role in both directions:

- Would a reader understand the later result without this chapter or with a
  much smaller bridge?
- Does the proposed chapter require explanations or facts that actually belong
  earlier, later, or with another owner?

State important questions the chapter will not settle when silence would
mislead the reader. Distinguish an accepted scope choice from a current gap or
an aspirational later result.

Purpose is provisional until tested by writing and downstream use. Reopen it
when drafting exposes an unnatural order, repeated explanation, missing
motivation, or a mismatch between the stated role and the material that
occupies most of the chapter.
