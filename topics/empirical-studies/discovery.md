# Geometric discovery beyond the systolic-ratio table

The September discovery desk investigated whether complementary measurements,
controlled families and mathematical reasoning reveal structure that one
invariant-feature table misses. Its surviving output is a collection of
geometric constraints, explicit separating examples and rejected explanations.
It is not a new capacity result, a universal Viterbo bound, or a selected current
research programme.

The [initial design](discovery-design/README.md) and its adaptive-discovery,
mathematical-discovery and method-portfolio reports preserve proposed routes.
The later [root review](discovery-design/desk/root-review/README.md) records
which research deductions and numerical witnesses survived review. Its receipts
record seven initial and four follow-up checks; those are historical checks,
not computations rerun during migration. Present assignments belong to the
[project-management owner](../../meta-topics/project-management/README.md).
September startup panes, owner dispatches and spending permissions have expired
as working instructions.

## The scout was a starting observation, not independent discovery

The [rho/ridge scout](discovery-design/desk/README.md) joined 14,334 historical
rows to canonical extreme-vertex covariance measurements and compared
within-bucket feature ranks across 18 generator buckets. It found strong
positive covariance-eccentricity/ridge-area association and discordant examples.
The script parses rows containing `sys` but excludes that field from its
calculations; its retained docstring's stronger “never loads” wording is
inaccurate. More importantly, these features had already been selected using
target associations, so the scout is not an independent target-free discovery.
Several ridge summaries are dependent. The earlier proposed outlier inspection
was superseded by the later [research checkpoint](discovery-design/desk/research-agenda.md).
Its stored summary/TSV and input hashes remain useful exploratory evidence;
they do not establish a capacity relation or justify a new producer run.

The circulation and adverse-moment packets separate proof from arithmetic
checks. The representation packets distinguish uniform-volume covariance,
extreme-vertex covariance and centering conventions. They retain matrices,
tensors, orientation forms and extremal witnesses instead of forcing every
object into a scalar invariant table. Linear-symplectic invariance is not
invariance under arbitrary nonlinear symplectomorphisms.

## Producer, evidence and current execution boundaries

The durable scientific exchange contract from the original owner note is
retained here: exchanged measurements need a recoverable body ID, definition
and version, units or normalization, source population, numerical status and
path to the structured result. Matrices, tensors, directional fields and
not-applicable values are legitimate data; a common scalar table is not
mandatory. Original geometry and lineage must remain recoverable, and
rounded-nearby geometries must not be silently merged. This is a measurement
contract, distinct from the removed session dispatch, pane names, authorization
and resource instructions.

The scout takes explicit historical/covariance inputs and writes a summary/TSV
to its requested output directory. Representation scripts generate the small
geometric panel and overwrite adjacent `artifacts/` outputs. Relation scripts
read existing bodies/collections, compute controls or contrasts and write
specified/adjacent outputs. Zonotope scripts overwrite their own adjacent JSON;
its independent integer validator uses a separate arithmetic route. The packet
READMEs own exact commands, dependencies, definitions and costs. Importing a
pure partner-moment routine does not run its collection or capacity evaluator.

All retained JSON, JSONL and TSV bytes moved unchanged during migration. No
producer, full replay or new scientific analysis ran. Syntax/path/hash checks
verify preservation and routing, not every mathematical result. Reproduction
must use scratch outputs or disposable copies when a script overwrites retained
evidence. Historical script hashes and absolute worktree observations remain
historical; current paths are routing information, not recovered execution
provenance.

The initial broad campaign proposals, earlier next-step recommendations and
owner startup instructions are not a current queue. This cleanup retains their
scientific rationale and completed results, removes temporary dispatch notes
from maintained source search and selects no further research or thesis claim.
