# Early account-to-writing pilot — 11 October 2026

## Question and stopping boundary

Can a substantive nested technical account of the difficult DS ridge/selection/transfer slice enable a fresh Sol author to reconstruct supported meaning, plan a mostly-agent writing allocation without hiding Jörn's work, and produce a coherent 1–2-page passage? This small pilot can reject a poor route. It cannot establish human acceptance, whole-chapter quality, a general effect size, or seven-day writing feasibility. No thesis file or producer was changed for the pilot.

The comparison questions were fixed before reading either passage:

1. **Source meaning:** Is the empirical association tied to its sampled population and historical numerical target; is the pentagon identity restricted; are opposing paths/families correctly used; do the generated selection and transfer comparisons retain their exact controls and finite-design limit?
2. **Writing usability:** Does a mathematically trained reader follow why a statistical descriptor is interesting, what the product geometry explains, why the counterexamples change the question, and what the candidate-selection tests actually establish? Are density, unexplained terms and transitions manageable in 1–2 pages?
3. **Reconstruction and allocation:** Which extra sources were needed, how much author effort was observable, what science or presentation decisions remain, and does the plan identify real Jörn judgments without silently making him responsible for drafting the section?
4. **Incremental value:** What did the new account contribute over the strong existing account, at what account-production and authoring cost? A tie or a better baseline passage is a useful negative finding.

## Observations and disposition

### Source meaning, owner assessment

Both passages preserve the main evidence boundary: historical numerical ratios are distinct from later capacity intervals (14,335 of 14,336, with volume uncertified); the pentagon law is exact on a restricted rotation family; contrary product examples prevent a universal ridge-minimization claim; generated candidate selection precedes target evaluation; and the changed-source comparison is finite. The primary accounts checked here support the quoted 0.626/0.316 first-stage means, 0.033992 concentration difference, 0.637/0.371 and 0.708/0.523 ridge transfer means, and the 91 unique transfer evaluations. The current Chapter 8 and the stratified figure receipt support inverse within-group rank correlations. These are source checks of reported results, not recomputation from raw rows or a fresh capacity proof audit.

The nested passage focuses the changed-source paragraph on the ridge rule and its two bucket means, with the concurrent covariance arm only where it affects control eligibility. The comparator reports both covariance and ridge gains, adding a new symbol and Williamson eigenvalues in the final part of a short passage. That is source-supported but increases explanation burden for this assigned slice. The comparator also uses the exact triangle family and cube bound correctly. No definite material source error was found on this owner read; the subsequent blinded review reached the same bounded source-fidelity finding.

### Usability, reconstruction, and allocation, owner assessment

Both drafts lack subsection headings. The nested draft begins with sample composition, generator and admission conditions before it states the observed association. This resembles a known concern in Jörn's earlier feedback, though his verdict was on a different sample and does not decide this draft. The comparator starts with a clear three-question frame, then also carries population detail before the observed relation and compresses three selection comparisons and an extra covariance definition into its final paragraphs. Neither draft includes or points to an empirical figure within the body. These are predicted contextual-reading obstacles, to be tested by the blinded reviewer and, if requested, Jörn.

The nested author reports roughly 2–3 minutes wall time and about 11 underlying tool calls; the comparator reports 3.7 minutes and about 25 shell calls. Both then opened Chapter 8, the appendix and numerous primary accounts beyond their assigned initial account. The nested author reports no missing artifact to reconstruct but made targeted checks of evaluation-union counts, control exclusions and transfer means. The comparator had to reconcile the 18 September account's superseded status and locate the current pentagon theorem after an old pointer failed. The new account appears to have avoided one dated-source/pointer detour, but this two-draft observation does not demonstrate lower total reconstruction: the new-account author still opened roughly a dozen source files. The HTML account itself was assembled from previously curated sources in roughly two local wall-clock minutes; this excludes the earlier research that made those sources available. Token and monetary cost were not measured. These single-arm times cannot establish that format caused a speed difference.

The allocation plans each reserve a contextual passage judgment and substantive placement/scope choices for Jörn, assign source checking and integration to agents, and avoid an autonomous PASS claim. Their projected agent work is 80–165 minutes for the nested draft and roughly 2–4 hours for the comparator's proposed package. These are unmeasured forward estimates; the rapid standalone authoring trial does not calibrate full manuscript integration, review cycles or mostly-agent throughput. Jörn has not seen or accepted either passage. A materially sound source account plus two fast drafts is not evidence of seven-day thesis feasibility.

### Independent reviewer and disposition

The [calibrated reviewer's frozen comparison](../calibration/live-frozen-review.md) preferred candidate 42, the new-account passage, as the stronger standalone ridge/selection/transfer account. It found no material source mismatch in targeted checks of either draft. Candidate 17 was more detailed but overloaded the transfer paragraph with covariance and a resampling qualification whose corresponding result was absent. Candidate 42 kept the ridge comparisons and transfer means intelligible. The reviewer explicitly stopped short of human acceptance. Its historical calibration and limitations are owned by the calibration packet, not this pilot.

The reviewer found a common omission: both drafts give the concentration result without stating its predeclared *descriptive* criterion, positive overall and in at least seven of ten buckets, and its distinction from a significance test. Candidate 42 additionally compresses the HKO position and the source's dual eligibility rule. Those are repairs to make before reuse. The source account itself gives the observed eight positive groups and mentions a prespecified criterion without specifying the seven-group threshold. Its organization helped this one author focus on the ridge chain, yet did not fully transmit every design fact or the historical request for visible section boundaries. Neither output is a manuscript replacement as frozen.

**Pilot outcome:** source-meaning reconstruction succeeded within the checked scope, and one blinded agent reader preferred the passage based on the new nested account over a strong existing-account comparator. Its incremental value is a clearer one-slice draft and avoidance of a stale-pointer detour, with no demonstrated reduction in source reads or calibrated total cost. The complete account → mostly-agent writing → human-usable prose route remains unproved: both drafts need editorial repair, Jörn has not judged either, integration/PDF interfaces were not tested, and the plans' agent-time projections are unmeasured. This is reason to repair and test a contextual passage before scaling prose authoring, not to infer a thesis-wide or seven-day schedule. No passage was altered in response to this frozen comparison.
