## Scheduling assessment

The [task reconstruction](timeline-notes.md), [preparation chronology](timeline-preparation-notes.md) and [timeline.json](timeline.json)
retain start/end evidence, prerequisites, preparation, unselected intervals and
uncertainty. Bars are **task-stage spans**, not uninterrupted compute or thought.
Do not add concurrent bars to obtain total elapsed wall time.

Preparation comprises six overlapping phases: orientation, source/value
calibration, quota/reset setup, candidate comparisons and worked mock,
readiness recovery, and orchestration/brief recovery. This includes user
discussion and proposals, not two hours of selected scientific execution.
Some boundaries are report/deadline estimates. Its source-recorded failures and
recovery are shown, but exact promise/compliance judgments require the original
authorization transcript and belong to the independent investigation.

1. **Parallel work did occur.** The first wave had three distinct scientific
   groups. Geometry/global-class work and P* work then overlapped. Some serial
   arrows were genuine prerequisites: joint denominator coverage before the
   global class proof, and balanced response → curvature → ambient P* assembly.
2. **An actual dispatch mistake cost approximately nine minutes.** The endpoint
   task selected around 01:52 Berlin was queued to an idle owner using a message
   that did not start a turn. The actual follow-up began at 02:01. Other science
   was running; this was a lane delay, not nine minutes of whole-program idle.
3. **The P* census could reasonably have been considered earlier in parallel.**
   Its proposal was prepared by 02:25 and the owner completed by 02:29, but the
   next science began at 03:06. The strongest recorded unselected interval is
   37 minutes. The global lane was prioritized. No mathematical dependency on
   its completion, quota refusal or unavailable P* owner was found. Shared
   coordination burden was not measured; the benefit of an alternative schedule
   is not proved. This is a concrete missed opportunity to assess, not evidence
   that parallel census would have completed full F11.
4. **The final 28 min 25 s had no selected scientific work.** Last independent
   science finished at 04:21; source integration finished at 04:27 and all-source
   disposition at 04:28. Only root and supervision recorded usage after 04:28:
   83,672 non-cached input plus 15,719 output tokens. The stop requirement did
   not itself make useful work impossible throughout that interval. Root chose
   to park broader continuations as unpriced; that choice is not justified merely
   by repeating that label. Whether it matched Jörn's approved ambitious scope
   belongs in the independent authorization investigation.

The night produced checked restricted results and useful rejected routes. It
did **not** finish research, establish the larger open claims, or exploit most
of the available subscription quota. The record supports criticism of allocation
and the idle-lane dispatch; it does not establish how much additional science
a different programme would have produced. No tested optimal schedule or
calibrated theorem-success forecast is claimed.
