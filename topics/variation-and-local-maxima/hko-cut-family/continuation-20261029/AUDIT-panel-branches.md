their scoped inference.** The two literal words supply feasible-word upper
bounds, not capacity values. The eight-label bounds rule out the sampled
bodies at t=2/5 and t=9/20 as improvements over the certified t=3/10 body.

1. The README's exact t=9/20 volume was copied incorrectly. The independently

       V(9/20) = -613821503 tau^2/1628542400 +1858768317/325708480.

   The README instead printed `-193233901 tau^2/510756000
   +584058253/102151200`. Its decimal bounds agree with the correct JSON.
2. The volume producer should assert `len(active)==4` for every feasible
   four-row intersection, rather than silently omit nonsimple vertices.
   The independent enumeration below establishes that none were omitted
   at these three panel parameters, so the retained volumes are valid.
3. The `panel.py` docstring incorrectly says volume is evaluated by Sage's
   AA polyhedron backend. Its actual method is the exact number-field
   Lawrence tangent-cone formula, with AA used for ordered comparisons.

These points were sent to root immediately. No broader parameter chamber,

## Independent checks executed

I wrote and ran a separate read-only scratch verifier,
`audit_panel_branches.py`, with the repository Sage interpreter. Its output
is `audit-panel-branches-checks.json`. It does not import or execute either
producer. It reuses Sage exact arithmetic but reconstructs the base normals
from the edges of a pentagon generated by exact 72-degree rotations, with
the partner polygon rotated by -90 degrees. It then builds the specified
moving rows from the handoff definition. This checks the literal row
conventions through a separate geometric construction.

For each rational-function branch, the verifier reads its reported weights
and Q and establishes the following identities in Q(tau)(t):

- The weight sum is one and the weighted normal sum is zero.
- The gradient G beta annihilates every vector in ker C, so the equality
  KKT stationarity condition holds without reusing the producer's solve.
- The direct ordered symplectic sum equals the reported Q.
- Each rational weight and Q has no numerator or denominator root in
  (0,1), and its value at t=1/2 is strictly positive.
- Substitution at each panel parameter exactly matches every retained
  weight and Q in `panel.json`.

Root isolation used AA comparisons `0<r<1` before any display conversion.
The producer instead converts roots to float for its interval test. That
can be unsafe for general roots arbitrarily close to an endpoint, but the
independent exact test confirms that it loses none in these particular
branches. An exact interval filter is preferable in a retained producer.

For Q7-Q8, the verifier found precisely one simple numerator root in (0,1),
no denominator root there, a positive value at t=1/4, and a negative value at
t=1/2. Thus Q7 is larger before the crossing and Q8 larger afterward. Both
are positive throughout, so larger Q gives the stronger capacity and ratio
upper bound at a fixed parameter. This is a transition of the two witnesses,
not a proven transition of the global optimizing word.

## KKT and feasibility interpretation

The producer's symmetric matrix G has upper-triangular entry
omega(n_i,n_j), symmetric lower completion and zero diagonal. Consequently
Q=beta^T G beta/2 is exactly the ordered objective, and its gradient is
G beta. The system

    G beta+C^T lambda=0,   C beta=(0,0,0,0,1)

has the correct sign and normalization; the multiplier sign is conventional.
Strictly positive weights and exact closure give a feasible normalized word.
The audit does not assert that either equality-stationary point is a global
maximum on its word, much less over all words. Neither assertion is needed:
any positive feasible Q gives

    c_EHZ(H_t) <= 1/(2Q),
    rho(H_t) <= 1/(8 Vol(H_t) Q^2).

Likewise, these feasible words need not be reconstructed as shortest boundary
characteristics for the upper bound. Calling them “feasible facet words”
avoids suggesting a stronger geometric conclusion.

## Independent geometry and volumes

At each t=7/20, 2/5, 9/20, I enumerated all 4845 four-row subsets. Every
invertible intersection was checked against every row using exact algebraic
signs. Every feasible intersection had exactly four active rows; none was
silently discarded. There were exactly 75 distinct vertices at each parameter.
The vertices of each of the 20 row faces had affine span of dimension three,
independently confirming that every listed row is a genuine facet.

I recomputed volume using the Lawrence formula with the third generic
functional ell=(1,5,25,125), distinct from both producer choices. Its slopes
at every cone were nonzero, every resulting volume was positive, and each
exact result matched both corresponding panel records. The three enumerations
took about 10.9, 11.3 and 11.8 seconds respectively.

The formula is appropriate here: the bodies are bounded by the old HKO
inequalities, contain the origin strictly, and the complete enumeration
establishes simple vertices. For active row matrix M, the tangent generators
are -M^{-1}e_j. In dimension four the four sign changes cancel in the
denominator, yielding precisely the producer's

    (ell·v)^4 / [24 |det M| product(ell M^{-1})].

Agreement of generic-function calculations alone would not prove that all
vertices were included; the explicit no-nonsimple-vertex check supplies the
missing enumeration condition for these sampled bodies.
