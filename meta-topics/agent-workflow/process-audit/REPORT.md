## Prioritized findings and interventions

### 1. Preserve the research objective at decision forks, and fund an alternative approach before polishing the incumbent

**Observed.** DS launched a portfolio framed around high-ratio search/allocation. Jörn's 13:57–14:00 feedback restored the pattern → geometric interpretation → conjecture/proof aim and explicitly allowed a better superseding frame. The agent admitted its operational frame had not established that superiority. Existing ridge anatomy had been omitted. Within minutes, the lane produced a checked product-width/mixed-area interpretation (DS `cem_review` child L413–496). Later Pro supplied an analytic rotation-profile proof replacing thousands of computational cases and an exact restricted-family ridge relation. The frozen self-review had instead proposed reorganizing the computational exposition.

**Mechanism and consequence.** A task framed as “execute promising experiments/report current results” rewards coherent local progress even when another question offers more scientific payoff. An insufficiently operationalized research frame and inherited computational machinery interact: faster execution alone can reinforce the wrong allocation. The later proof is evidence that a better approach existed, not proof it would have been discovered on demand.

**Earlier alternative.** At the initial portfolio choice, provide the original research aim, achieved evidence and live uncertainty together. Require the selector to compare one evidence-producing experiment with one bounded analytic/literature route. For rotating products, the already available endpoint result and rotation-dependent quadratic objective justified asking whether interpolation or symmetry removes enumeration; it did not guarantee success. For DS, require each pilot to say whether it improves prediction, rare-event search, or mathematical explanation.

**Intervention.** A short project decision note, consumed at handoff/portfolio changes, owns objective, incumbent approach, strongest alternative, and evidence that would trigger a switch. Replace duplicate “active scope” prose with that owner. At a genuine research fork, use an independent problem-solving assignment with freedom to replace the approach, not another verification of the incumbent. Do not mandate an external Pro session for every task.

**Impact/uncertainty.** Highest potential quality impact; uncertain discovery probability. No claim that R05/R06 would have rescued the original deadline. On the second day these results are already known: incorporating them is a project repair, while testing alternative-route selection is the transferable harness change. Experiment A below tests that behavior without awarding hindsight credit.

### 2. A literature review must test the live claim, not merely verify that an old source says it

**Observed.** R780/787 began with broad equality queries. R790 returned a secondary “open problems” page; R794 still searched recent 2025/2026 terms. R801/808 then retrieved HKO, and R815–827 explicitly searched “open question” (two syntax failures before a successful call). R835 concluded that the user's recollection was not a global theorem. Coordinator searches ran concurrently, then C2780 recorded the stale conclusion. Thus this was not simply “no browsing” or exclusively confirmation-seeking from the start.

**Consequence/mechanism.** An older primary-source remark was promoted into a current negative literature conclusion, corroborated by a reviewer working from overlapping sources. Search-result coverage failure and premature closure are directly supported; psychological confirmation bias is only a plausible contributor. The publication year on a citation did not establish the recency of the underlying claim. Time pressure and terminology mismatch are competing explanations. I found no evidence in the inspected sequence that the decisive paper was retrieved and consciously ignored.

**Earlier alternative/intervention.** For consequential contradictions of a concrete user recollection, or claims that a research direction remains open: distinguish “this source says open” from “current literature establishes open”; check later work around the relevant authors/theorem, vary terminology to minimal-action characteristics/global sections, and retain uncertainty if the bounded search fails. Let a second reviewer seek counterevidence rather than recheck the same citation. Put theorem/version/checked-date in the claim's source note, not a generic warning in every prompt.

**Impact/uncertainty.** High accuracy and research-allocation impact, low additional cost when targeted. It need not demand exhaustive literature searches. The missing December 2024 result explicitly covers all convex bodies in R4 in Corollary 1 ([primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.01777v1)). Pro's later family-exclusion literature also changes the interpretation of searches; it does not make all subthreshold or descriptor experiments worthless. Experiment A includes outdated-open-problem cases and legitimate still-open controls.

### 3. Close integration obligations on the assembled artifact; “known limitation” and “review resolved” are different states

**Observed.** The DS source map explicitly required a CH2021 construction/cross-reference; the delivery message pointed to that map (C434). D2496 later acknowledged it remained absent in the submitted chapter. Reviews resolved local findings while such interfaces remained elsewhere. The candidate mixed tracked legacy inputs and `.git/codex` replacements. New pilot evidence was not adequately represented in the availability section. The current comparison and both blind reviews document broader empirical specification gaps.

**Mechanism/consequence.** Distributed ownership ended at “my fragment is reviewed.” The integrator inherited prose obligations embedded in reports, while a successful build checked labels rather than scientific definitions or evidence completeness. Hidden storage increased retrieval/release fragility, but merely moving directories would not close obligations. There is no evidence that 1.3GB itself was a performance bottleneck.

**Earlier alternative/intervention.** Deliver a fragment with a short explicit list of unresolved interfaces, each with receiving owner and completion evidence. Close them against the assembled PDF/source bundle, or obtain an explicit scope decision. A source manifest/build target should include all TeX, figures, executable evidence and bibliography inputs outside Git internals; a clean-directory build/review should find missing dependencies. Move durable sources to ordinary project locations, leave disposable outputs in a declared build area, and retire superseded active pointers. Keep historical evidence, not competing “current” versions.

**Impact/uncertainty.** High reliability and next-sprint readiness; medium direct first-day time saving. Some disclosed historical-data limitations may be acceptable for the retained claim; not every limitation requires a rerun. Experiment B tests whether a fresh integrator actually closes known interfaces and reproduces the candidate, not just whether a manifest parses.

### 4. Review should predict reader failure and protect mathematics; narrow clean reviews cannot certify the broader product

**Observed.** The frozen DS review missed the main narrative objection. A preliminaries preflight explicitly excluded a style inventory; the chapter was nevertheless offered with implied prose readiness. Jörn immediately objected. A subsequent review found further prose issues (R703–908; `whole-thesis-reading-1700.md`). Conversely, Chapter 4 received human PASS despite several automated suggestions, and some suggested dwell-time wording needed mathematical qualification (R993).

**Mechanism/consequence.** Review tasks selected easily reportable defects and returned “none within scope”; integration treated this as broader readiness. Adding more similarly scoped reviewers would repeat correlated blind spots and can introduce regressions. These are related to finding 3, not independent additive losses.

**Earlier alternative/intervention.** Give the reviewer the intended reader, complete reading unit and desired reader understanding. Ask for the earliest consequential obstruction, and separately ask whether the unit accomplishes its purpose. Preserve the scope and uncertainty in the handoff. Freeze a few predictions before user feedback; retain passing passages as negative cases. Use mathematical checks on changed mathematical interpretations. Remove arbitrary “at most three”/“only high-severity” restrictions when the actual consumer is broad prose screening, but avoid turning every review into a comprehensive inventory.

**Impact/uncertainty.** High human-attention potential, currently unvalidated. Earlier sample comfort did not transfer even to the revised opening. The successful fixed-snapshot, blind-review and explicit chapter-vs-whole boundaries are worth keeping. Experiment C measures prevented reader interruptions and harmful edits, not number of findings or agreement on already-seen examples.

### 5. Remove invented small-task gates; make coordination completion and message delivery observable

**Observed and authority.** D73 explicitly restricted further top-level Herdr prompts while authorizing internal delegation. D512 challenged a later approval gate for small experiments; D517 admitted “material scope change” had been its interpretation, not a requiring rule. The governing base instructions already favored acting on authorized reversible work. D173 ended with workers active; Jörn restarted at D183. After the explicit keep-active correction, D2280–2283 records a later premature ending. That later case differs: the lane had been told to hold steady (D2268), and no unfinished experiment or worker task remained (D2317). Async questions also blocked peer-message delivery; an inbox fallback escaped notice during human feedback (C/D handoff messages, D2335).

**Consequence/mechanism.** User attention repaired the control plane. Broad permission language and ambiguous lane state overrode the practical scope; status files were treated as communication without reliable receipt. These costs are visible, but not all waiting was harmful—workers continued and human reading was asynchronous.

**Intervention.** Replace ambiguous inherited launch rules with one current authority statement distinguishing internal tasks, top-level launches and actual scope expansions. Small reversible probes get a brief assessment and measured stop condition, not an invented approval ceremony. A coordinator ends only after completion/acknowledged ownership transfer or a declared genuine blocker. Prefer a durable delivered/acknowledged task channel that remains usable while a human question is pending; consolidate review requests under one owner. Fix appendable immutable review hosting rather than repeatedly warning agents that new links invalidate old links.

**Impact/uncertainty.** Medium direct elapsed-time benefit, high attention/reliability benefit across long projects. Do not implement endless active waiting as the fix. The successful pilots show why removing the gate matters, but their execution speed does not establish their scientific priority. Experiment D tests delivery, interruption and lane closure with cheap synthetic tasks.

## Causal interpretation and current research

A useful interaction chain is: **research intent omitted or insufficiently operationalized → narrowed allocation to the incumbent route → local reviews of that route → integration and reader burden**. Hidden evidence and unclosed interfaces feed the same downstream burden. Delivery friction delays the feedback that could correct it. Quality improvements and faster execution are complements only if released time is actually reallocated to a worthwhile alternative or review. Summing six estimated savings would double-count these interactions.

[AgentRx, revised August 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02475v2) uses constraint checks and auditable violation evidence to localize failures. That supports explicit observed/required/violated links here, but not inventing a constraint that the thesis must contain a particular new theorem. [MAST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657) supplies useful distinctions between specification, coordination and verification failures; its categories are prompts for investigation, not proof of causation.

[FAMAS](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.13782v1) compares replayed trajectories through action abstractions and suspiciousness scores. It requires multiple runs and its assumptions do not match this one changing, parallel research episode automatically. No suspiciousness score or Shapley value has been computed here. Shapley attribution would first require defensible utility values for intervention combinations; without them, numerical shares merely disguise judgment.

Process-mining guidance distinguishes event timestamps from activity start/end records ([Microsoft's event-data requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/process-mining-processes-and-data)). The present logs can establish order, handoffs and some measured operations, not human attention or exclusive occupation of the critical path. [Anthropic's evaluation guidance](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents) usefully separates trace statements from final environment outcomes and recommends calibrated graders. This is cross-vendor design evidence, not proof of Codex mechanics.

Evidence on repository instructions is mixed: [Gloaguen et al., June revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11988v2) report no general success improvement and increased inference cost; [Lulla et al., March revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20404v2) report lower median runtime and output tokens in their setting. Neither estimates this project's effect. The practical conclusion is to compare the actual maintained context with a focused replacement and an omission baseline, not assume that adding or deleting AGENTS.md is inherently beneficial.
